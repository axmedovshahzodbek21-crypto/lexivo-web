# Sabab, oqibat va kelib chiqish|Sabab va oqibat
lead to|phrasal verb|/liːd tuː/|olib kelmoq, sabab bo'lmoq|To cause something to happen as a result.|cause · result in · bring about · give rise to|biror narsaning natijasida boshqa narsa yuz berishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|Poor diet in childhood can lead to serious health problems later in life.|Bolalikdagi noto'g'ri ovqatlanish keyinchalik jiddiy sog'liq muammolariga olib kelishi mumkin.|An unhealthy diet in early years may cause illness in adulthood.|Erta yoshdagi nosog'lom ovqatlanish kattalikda kasallikka sabab bo'lishi mumkin.|The new road is expected to lead to a sharp fall in traffic accidents.|Yangi yo'l yo'l-transport hodisalarining keskin kamayishiga olib kelishi kutilmoqda.
result in|phrasal verb|/rɪˈzʌlt ɪn/|natijasida ... bo'lmoq, olib kelmoq|To make a particular situation or outcome happen.|lead to · cause · produce · end in|biror harakat yoki hodisa ma'lum natijani keltirib chiqarmoq.|Heavy rainfall resulted in widespread flooding across the region.|Kuchli yog'ingarchilik butun mintaqada keng ko'lamli suv toshqiniga olib keldi.|The floods were caused by an unusual amount of rain.|Suv toshqinlariga g'ayrioddiy ko'p yomg'ir sabab bo'lgan.|Cutting the research budget could result in fewer new discoveries.|Tadqiqot byudjetini qisqartirish yangi kashfiyotlar kamayishiga olib kelishi mumkin.
as a result of|phrase|/æz ə rɪˈzʌlt əv/|... natijasida, ... tufayli|Because of something; caused by something.|because of · owing to · due to · following|biror narsa sababli yoki uning oqibatida.|As a result of improved sanitation, child mortality fell dramatically.|Sanitariya sharoitlari yaxshilangani natijasida bolalar o'limi keskin kamaydi.|Better hygiene was the main reason fewer children died.|Bolalar o'limi kamayishining asosiy sababi gigiyenaning yaxshilanishi edi.|Many species are now endangered as a result of habitat loss.|Ko'plab turlar yashash muhitini yo'qotish natijasida yo'qolib ketish xavfi ostida.
contribute to|phrasal verb|/kənˈtrɪbjuːt tuː/|hissa qo'shmoq, sabablaridan biri bo'lmoq|To be one of the causes of something.|play a part in · help cause · be a factor in|biror natija yuzaga kelishida sabablardan biri bo'lmoq.|Traffic fumes contribute to air pollution in large cities.|Transport chiqindi gazlari yirik shaharlarda havo ifloslanishiga hissa qo'shadi.|Vehicle emissions are one factor behind poor urban air quality.|Avtomobil chiqindilari shaharlardagi havo sifati yomonligining omillaridan biridir.|Several factors contributed to the collapse of the ancient empire.|Qadimgi imperiyaning qulashiga bir nechta omil sabab bo'lgan.
have an impact on|phrase|/hæv ən ˈɪmpækt ɒn/|ta'sir ko'rsatmoq|To have a strong effect on someone or something.|affect · influence · make a difference to · shape|biror narsaga sezilarli ta'sir ko'rsatmoq.|The internet has had a huge impact on the way people read news.|Internet odamlarning yangiliklarni o'qish usuliga juda katta ta'sir ko'rsatdi.|Online media has changed how we get information.|Onlayn media ma'lumot olish usulimizni o'zgartirdi.|Rising temperatures will have an impact on crop production worldwide.|Haroratning ko'tarilishi butun dunyoda hosil yetishtirishga ta'sir qiladi.
have an effect on|phrase|/hæv ən ɪˈfekt ɒn/|ta'sir qilmoq|To cause a change in someone or something.|affect · influence · alter · change|biror narsada o'zgarish yuzaga keltirmoq.|Even small amounts of noise can have an effect on concentration.|Hatto ozgina shovqin ham diqqatni jamlashga ta'sir qilishi mumkin.|Concentration may be affected by quite low levels of sound.|Diqqat juda past darajadagi tovush ta'sirida ham buzilishi mumkin.|The new medicine had no effect on the patients' blood pressure.|Yangi dori bemorlarning qon bosimiga hech qanday ta'sir qilmadi.
be responsible for|phrase|/bi rɪˈspɒnsəbl fɔː/|... uchun javobgar bo'lmoq, ... ga sabab bo'lmoq|To be the cause of something, or to have the duty of dealing with it.|cause · be to blame for · be in charge of · account for|biror narsaning sababchisi yoki unga mas'ul bo'lmoq.|Bacteria are responsible for most cases of food poisoning.|Ovqatdan zaharlanishning aksariyat holatlariga bakteriyalar sabab bo'ladi.|Most food poisoning is caused by bacteria.|Ovqatdan zaharlanishning ko'pi bakteriyalar tufayli yuzaga keladi.|Local councils are responsible for collecting household waste.|Maishiy chiqindilarni yig'ish uchun mahalliy kengashlar javobgar.
a key factor in|phrase|/ə kiː ˈfæktə ɪn/|... dagi asosiy omil|One of the most important causes or influences in a situation.|a major reason for · a crucial element in · a main cause of|biror vaziyatdagi eng muhim sabab yoki ta'sirlardan biri.|Access to clean water was a key factor in the town's rapid growth.|Toza suvdan foydalanish imkoniyati shaharning tez o'sishidagi asosiy omil bo'ldi.|The town grew quickly mainly because it had a good water supply.|Shahar asosan yaxshi suv ta'minoti borligi sababli tez o'sdi.|Motivation is a key factor in learning a foreign language.|Motivatsiya chet tilini o'rganishda asosiy omildir.
be driven by|phrase|/bi ˈdrɪvn baɪ/|... tufayli yuz bermoq, ... ning ta'sirida bo'lmoq|To be caused or strongly influenced by something.|be caused by · be the result of · be fuelled by|biror narsa sabab bo'lib yuz bermoq yoki unga kuchli bog'liq bo'lmoq.|Much of the growth in tourism has been driven by cheaper air travel.|Turizmdagi o'sishning katta qismi arzon aviaqatnovlar tufayli yuz berdi.|Lower flight prices are the main reason tourism has grown.|Parvoz narxlarining pasayishi turizm o'sishining asosiy sababidir.|Demand for electric cars is driven largely by fuel prices.|Elektr avtomobillarga talab asosan yoqilg'i narxlari tufayli yuzaga keladi.
bring about a change|phrase|/brɪŋ əˈbaʊt ə tʃeɪndʒ/|o'zgarishga sabab bo'lmoq, o'zgarish yasamoq|To make a change happen.|cause a change · produce a shift · transform|biror narsada o'zgarish yuz berishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|The invention of the printing press brought about a change in how knowledge spread.|Bosmaxona ixtirosi bilimning tarqalish usulida o'zgarishga sabab bo'ldi.|Printing transformed the way information was shared.|Kitob bosish ma'lumot almashish usulini tubdan o'zgartirdi.|Campaigners hope the new law will bring about a change in public attitudes.|Faollar yangi qonun jamoatchilik qarashlarida o'zgarish yasashiga umid qilmoqda.
trace its origins back to|phrase|/treɪs ɪts ˈɒrɪdʒɪnz bæk tuː/|kelib chiqishi ... ga borib taqalmoq|To show where or when something first began.|date back to · originate in · have its roots in|biror narsaning ilk boshlanish joyi yoki vaqtini ko'rsatmoq.|The modern Olympic Games trace their origins back to ancient Greece.|Zamonaviy Olimpiya o'yinlarining kelib chiqishi qadimgi Yunonistonga borib taqaladi.|The Olympics first began in Greek antiquity.|Olimpiada ilk bor qadimgi Yunonistonda boshlangan.|This recipe traces its origins back to a small village in Italy.|Bu retseptning kelib chiqishi Italiyadagi kichik bir qishloqqa borib taqaladi.
fuel demand for|phrase|/ˈfjuːəl dɪˈmɑːnd fɔː/|... ga talabni oshirmoq|To make people want more of something.|increase demand for · boost demand for · drive up demand for|biror narsaga bo'lgan ehtiyoj yoki talabni kuchaytirmoq.|The rise of online shopping has fuelled demand for delivery drivers.|Onlayn xaridlarning ko'payishi yetkazib beruvchi haydovchilarga talabni oshirdi.|More people now need couriers because of internet shopping.|Internet orqali xaridlar sababli kuryerlarga ehtiyoj ortdi.|Rapid population growth is fuelling demand for new housing.|Aholining tez o'sishi yangi uy-joyga talabni oshirmoqda.
set in motion|phrase|/set ɪn ˈməʊʃn/|harakatga keltirmoq, boshlab bermoq|To start a process or series of events.|start · trigger · initiate · launch|biror jarayon yoki voqealar zanjirini boshlab bermoq.|The discovery of gold set in motion a wave of migration to the west.|Oltin topilishi g'arbga ommaviy ko'chish to'lqinini boshlab berdi.|Finding gold caused large numbers of people to move west.|Oltin topilishi ko'p odamlarning g'arbga ko'chishiga sabab bo'ldi.|The minister's speech set in motion a long debate about tax reform.|Vazirning nutqi soliq islohoti haqida uzoq bahsni boshlab berdi.
consequence|noun|/ˈkɒnsɪkwəns/|oqibat, natija|A result of an action or situation, often a bad one.|result · outcome · effect · repercussion|biror harakat yoki vaziyatning natijasi, ko'pincha salbiy.|One consequence of deforestation is the loss of fertile soil.|O'rmonlarni kesishning oqibatlaridan biri unumdor tuproqning yo'qolishidir.|Cutting down forests results in poorer soil.|O'rmonlarni kesish tuproqning kambag'allashishiga olib keladi.|Few people considered the long-term consequences of the decision.|Kam odam bu qarorning uzoq muddatli oqibatlari haqida o'ylab ko'rdi.
trigger|verb|/ˈtrɪɡə/|qo'zg'atmoq, boshlab yubormoq|To make something happen suddenly.|cause · set off · spark · provoke|biror narsaning to'satdan yuz berishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|Strong winds can trigger avalanches in the mountains.|Kuchli shamollar tog'larda qor ko'chkisini qo'zg'atishi mumkin.|Avalanches may be set off by high winds.|Qor ko'chkilari kuchli shamol tufayli boshlanishi mumkin.|Certain foods can trigger an allergic reaction.|Ba'zi oziq-ovqatlar allergik reaksiyani qo'zg'atishi mumkin.
origin|noun|/ˈɒrɪdʒɪn/|kelib chiqish, manba|The point or place where something begins.|source · beginning · root · starting point|biror narsaning boshlanish nuqtasi yoki joyi.|The origin of the disease remains a mystery to scientists.|Kasallikning kelib chiqishi olimlar uchun hamon sir bo'lib qolmoqda.|Researchers still do not know where the illness came from.|Tadqiqotchilar kasallik qayerdan paydo bo'lganini hali bilishmaydi.|Many English words have their origin in Latin.|Ko'plab inglizcha so'zlarning kelib chiqishi lotin tiliga borib taqaladi.
play a crucial role in|phrase|/pleɪ ə ˈkruːʃl rəʊl ɪn/|... da hal qiluvchi rol o'ynamoq|To be very important in a process or situation.|be vital to · be essential for · be central to|biror jarayon yoki vaziyatda juda muhim ahamiyatga ega bo'lmoq.|Bees play a crucial role in pollinating food crops.|Asalarilar oziq-ovqat ekinlarini changlatishda hal qiluvchi rol o'ynaydi.|Without bees, many crops could not be pollinated.|Asalarilarsiz ko'plab ekinlarni changlatib bo'lmas edi.|Parents play a crucial role in a child's early development.|Ota-onalar bolaning ilk rivojlanishida hal qiluvchi rol o'ynaydi.
be closely linked to|phrase|/bi ˈkləʊsli lɪŋkt tuː/|... bilan chambarchas bog'liq bo'lmoq|To be strongly connected with something.|be strongly associated with · be tied to · correlate with|biror narsa bilan kuchli bog'liqlikka ega bo'lmoq.|Lack of sleep is closely linked to poor academic performance.|Uyqu yetishmasligi past o'zlashtirish bilan chambarchas bog'liq.|Students who sleep less tend to get lower grades.|Kam uxlaydigan talabalar odatda pastroq baho olishadi.|The town's prosperity was closely linked to the river trade.|Shaharning farovonligi daryo savdosi bilan chambarchas bog'liq edi.
# Dalil, isbot va tadqiqot|Dalil va tadqiqot
conduct research|phrase|/kənˈdʌkt rɪˈsɜːtʃ/|tadqiqot olib bormoq|To carry out a careful study in order to discover new facts.|carry out a study · investigate · do research|yangi faktlarni aniqlash uchun puxta o'rganish olib bormoq.|The team conducted research into the sleeping habits of teenagers.|Jamoa o'smirlarning uyqu odatlari bo'yicha tadqiqot olib bordi.|Scientists studied how young people sleep.|Olimlar yoshlarning qanday uxlashini o'rganishdi.|Universities need more funding to conduct research on rare diseases.|Universitetlarga kam uchraydigan kasalliklar bo'yicha tadqiqot olib borish uchun ko'proq mablag' kerak.
carry out an experiment|phrase|/ˈkæri aʊt ən ɪkˈsperɪmənt/|tajriba o'tkazmoq|To do a scientific test to find out something.|conduct a test · run a trial · perform an experiment|biror narsani aniqlash uchun ilmiy sinov o'tkazmoq.|The students carried out an experiment to measure how fast plants grow in the dark.|Talabalar o'simliklar qorong'ida qanchalik tez o'sishini o'lchash uchun tajriba o'tkazishdi.|A test was done to see the growth rate of plants without light.|Yorug'liksiz o'simliklarning o'sish tezligini ko'rish uchun sinov o'tkazildi.|Before carrying out an experiment, researchers must get ethical approval.|Tajriba o'tkazishdan oldin tadqiqotchilar axloqiy ruxsat olishlari kerak.
a recent study|phrase|/ə ˈriːsnt ˈstʌdi/|yaqinda o'tkazilgan tadqiqot|A piece of research carried out a short time ago.|new research · the latest findings · a new investigation|yaqin vaqtda o'tkazilgan ilmiy izlanish.|A recent study found that walking every day reduces the risk of heart disease.|Yaqinda o'tkazilgan tadqiqot har kuni piyoda yurish yurak kasalliklari xavfini kamaytirishini aniqladi.|New research shows daily walks are good for the heart.|Yangi tadqiqotlar kundalik sayr yurak uchun foydali ekanini ko'rsatmoqda.|According to a recent study, most people check their phones within minutes of waking.|Yaqindagi tadqiqotga ko'ra, ko'pchilik uyg'ongach bir necha daqiqa ichida telefonini tekshiradi.
evidence suggests that|phrase|/ˈevɪdəns səˈdʒests ðæt/|dalillar shuni ko'rsatadiki|There are facts or signs which indicate that something is probably true.|research indicates that · data shows that · it appears that|mavjud faktlar biror narsa ehtimol to'g'ri ekanini ko'rsatadi.|Evidence suggests that early humans used fire over a million years ago.|Dalillar shuni ko'rsatadiki, ilk odamlar million yildan ko'proq vaqt oldin olovdan foydalanishgan.|It seems likely that fire was used by humans very early in history.|Odamlar olovdan tarixning juda erta davrida foydalangani ehtimoldan yiroq emas.|Evidence suggests that bilingual children are better at switching between tasks.|Dalillar ikki tilli bolalar vazifalar orasida yaxshiroq almasha olishini ko'rsatadi.
the results show that|phrase|/ðə rɪˈzʌlts ʃəʊ ðæt/|natijalar shuni ko'rsatadiki|The outcome of a study or test makes it clear that something is true.|the findings reveal that · the data indicates that|tadqiqot yoki sinov natijasi biror narsani aniq ko'rsatib beradi.|The results show that students who revise in short sessions remember more.|Natijalar qisqa mashg'ulotlarda takrorlaydigan talabalar ko'proq eslab qolishini ko'rsatadi.|Short study periods were found to improve memory.|Qisqa o'qish vaqtlari xotirani yaxshilashi aniqlandi.|The results show that the new drug is safe for children.|Natijalar yangi dori bolalar uchun xavfsiz ekanini ko'rsatadi.
back up a claim|phrase|/bæk ʌp ə kleɪm/|da'voni asoslamoq, tasdiqlamoq|To provide evidence that a statement is true.|support a claim · confirm · substantiate · prove|biror fikr to'g'ri ekanini dalil bilan ko'rsatmoq.|The company could not back up its claim that the product was eco-friendly.|Kompaniya mahsulot ekologik toza degan da'vosini asoslay olmadi.|There was no evidence to prove the product was green.|Mahsulot ekologik ekanini isbotlovchi dalil yo'q edi.|Scientists need reliable data to back up a claim like this.|Olimlarga bunday da'voni asoslash uchun ishonchli ma'lumotlar kerak.
cite evidence|phrase|/saɪt ˈevɪdəns/|dalil keltirmoq|To mention facts or examples in order to support an argument.|refer to evidence · quote data · give examples|fikrni qo'llab-quvvatlash uchun fakt yoki misollarni keltirmoq.|The author cites evidence from several surveys to support her argument.|Muallif o'z fikrini qo'llab-quvvatlash uchun bir nechta so'rovnomadan dalil keltiradi.|She refers to survey data to strengthen her case.|U o'z pozitsiyasini kuchaytirish uchun so'rovnoma ma'lumotlariga tayanadi.|Critics were asked to cite evidence that the policy had failed.|Tanqidchilardan siyosat muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'lganiga dalil keltirish so'raldi.
gather data|phrase|/ˈɡæðə ˈdeɪtə/|ma'lumot to'plamoq|To collect facts or numbers for study.|collect information · compile data · record figures|o'rganish uchun fakt yoki raqamlarni yig'moq.|Researchers used satellites to gather data on melting ice.|Tadqiqotchilar erayotgan muz haqida ma'lumot to'plash uchun sun'iy yo'ldoshlardan foydalanishdi.|Information about the ice was collected from space.|Muz haqidagi ma'lumotlar koinotdan yig'ildi.|The app gathers data about how long users spend reading.|Ilova foydalanuvchilar o'qishga qancha vaqt sarflashi haqida ma'lumot to'playdi.
test a hypothesis|phrase|/test ə haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/|farazni sinovdan o'tkazmoq|To check by experiment whether an idea is correct.|check a theory · examine an idea · put a theory to the test|biror g'oyaning to'g'riligini tajriba orqali tekshirmoq.|The scientists designed a simple experiment to test the hypothesis.|Olimlar farazni sinash uchun oddiy tajriba ishlab chiqishdi.|A test was created to see if the idea was correct.|G'oya to'g'rimi yoki yo'qligini bilish uchun sinov yaratildi.|To test this hypothesis, birds were moved to a new island.|Bu farazni sinab ko'rish uchun qushlar yangi orolga ko'chirildi.
yield promising results|phrase|/jiːld ˈprɒmɪsɪŋ rɪˈzʌlts/|umidli natijalar bermoq|To produce outcomes that suggest future success.|show encouraging results · look hopeful · be promising|kelajakda muvaffaqiyatga umid beruvchi natijalar ko'rsatmoq.|Early trials of the vaccine have yielded promising results.|Vaksinaning dastlabki sinovlari umidli natijalar berdi.|The first tests of the vaccine look encouraging.|Vaksinaning birinchi sinovlari umid uyg'otmoqda.|The new teaching method has yielded promising results in rural schools.|Yangi o'qitish usuli qishloq maktablarida umidli natijalar berdi.
verify the accuracy of|phrase|/ˈverɪfaɪ ði ˈækjərəsi əv/|aniqligini tekshirmoq|To check that something is correct and true.|check · confirm · make sure · validate|biror narsa to'g'ri va haqiqiy ekanini tekshirib ko'rmoq.|Journalists must verify the accuracy of their sources before publishing.|Jurnalistlar nashr qilishdan oldin manbalarining aniqligini tekshirishlari shart.|Reporters need to check that their information is correct.|Muxbirlar o'z ma'lumotlari to'g'ri ekanini tekshirishlari kerak.|A second team was asked to verify the accuracy of the measurements.|O'lchovlarning aniqligini tekshirish ikkinchi jamoaga topshirildi.
establish a link|phrase|/ɪˈstæblɪʃ ə lɪŋk/|bog'liqlikni aniqlamoq|To prove that there is a connection between two things.|find a connection · show a relationship · prove a link|ikki narsa o'rtasida bog'liqlik borligini isbotlamoq.|Doctors first established a link between smoking and lung cancer in the 1950s.|Shifokorlar chekish va o'pka saratoni o'rtasidagi bog'liqlikni ilk bor 1950-yillarda aniqlashgan.|In the 1950s, smoking was shown to be connected to cancer.|1950-yillarda chekish saraton bilan bog'liq ekani ko'rsatib berildi.|No study has yet established a link between the chemical and the illness.|Hali biror tadqiqot kimyoviy modda va kasallik o'rtasidagi bog'liqlikni aniqlamagan.
a sample of|phrase|/ə ˈsɑːmpl əv/|... dan tanlanma, namuna|A small group or amount taken to represent a larger whole.|a selection of · a group of · a cross-section of|kattaroq butunni ifodalash uchun olingan kichik guruh yoki miqdor.|The survey was based on a sample of 2,000 adults.|So'rovnoma 2 000 nafar kattalardan iborat tanlanmaga asoslangan.|Two thousand grown-ups took part in the survey.|So'rovnomada ikki ming nafar katta yoshli kishi ishtirok etdi.|Scientists took a sample of water from each lake.|Olimlar har bir ko'ldan suv namunasini olishdi.
findings|noun|/ˈfaɪndɪŋz/|xulosalar, aniqlangan natijalar|The information discovered as a result of research.|results · conclusions · discoveries · data|tadqiqot natijasida aniqlangan ma'lumotlar.|The findings were published in a leading medical journal.|Natijalar yetakchi tibbiyot jurnalida chop etildi.|The results of the study appeared in a respected journal.|Tadqiqot natijalari nufuzli jurnalda e'lon qilindi.|These findings challenge the traditional view of how memory works.|Bu xulosalar xotira qanday ishlashi haqidagi an'anaviy qarashni shubha ostiga qo'yadi.
participant|noun|/pɑːˈtɪsɪpənt/|ishtirokchi|A person who takes part in a study or activity.|subject · volunteer · member · contributor|tadqiqot yoki tadbirda qatnashuvchi shaxs.|Each participant was asked to keep a diary for two weeks.|Har bir ishtirokchidan ikki hafta davomida kundalik yuritish so'raldi.|Everyone in the study had to write a daily record.|Tadqiqotdagi har bir kishi kundalik yozuv olib borishi kerak edi.|Participants who exercised regularly reported lower stress levels.|Muntazam jismoniy mashq qilgan ishtirokchilar stress darajasi pastroq ekanini bildirishdi.
reveal|verb|/rɪˈviːl/|oshkor qilmoq, ko'rsatmoq|To make known something that was secret or unknown.|show · uncover · disclose · bring to light|yashirin yoki noma'lum narsani ma'lum qilmoq.|The survey revealed that most workers would prefer a four-day week.|So'rovnoma ko'pchilik ishchilar to'rt kunlik ish haftasini afzal ko'rishini ko'rsatdi.|Most employees said they wanted a shorter working week.|Ko'pchilik xodimlar qisqaroq ish haftasini xohlashlarini aytishdi.|X-rays revealed a hidden painting beneath the portrait.|Rentgen nurlari portret ostidagi yashirin rasmni ko'rsatib berdi.
confirm|verb|/kənˈfɜːm/|tasdiqlamoq|To show that something is definitely true.|prove · verify · support · back up|biror narsa haqiqatan to'g'ri ekanini ko'rsatmoq.|New tests confirmed that the bones were over 3,000 years old.|Yangi sinovlar suyaklar 3 000 yildan qadimiyroq ekanini tasdiqladi.|The bones were shown to be more than three millennia old.|Suyaklar uch ming yildan qadimiyroq ekani ko'rsatildi.|The data confirmed what many teachers had suspected for years.|Ma'lumotlar ko'plab o'qituvchilar yillar davomida gumon qilgan narsani tasdiqladi.
# Qarama-qarshilik va nozik farqlar|Qarama-qarshilik
in contrast to|phrase|/ɪn ˈkɒntrɑːst tuː/|... dan farqli o'laroq|Used to show how two things are different.|unlike · as opposed to · compared with · whereas|ikki narsa bir-biridan qanday farq qilishini ko'rsatish uchun ishlatiladi.|In contrast to their parents, young people today rarely buy newspapers.|Ota-onalaridan farqli o'laroq, bugungi yoshlar kamdan-kam gazeta sotib oladi.|Unlike older generations, the young seldom purchase printed news.|Katta avloddan farqli ravishda yoshlar bosma yangiliklarni kam xarid qiladi.|In contrast to the dry north, the south receives heavy rainfall.|Qurg'oqchil shimoldan farqli o'laroq, janubga kuchli yog'ingarchilik tushadi.
