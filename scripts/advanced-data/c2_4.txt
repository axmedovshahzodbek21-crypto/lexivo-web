# Ta'lim va mahorat|Ta'lim
credential inflation|noun|/krəˈdenʃl ɪnˈfleɪʃn/|diplomlar qadrsizlanishi|The rising level of qualifications needed for jobs that once required less.|degree inflation · qualification creep|ilgari kamroq talab qilingan ishlar uchun kerakli malaka darajasining oshishi.|Credential inflation means many jobs now require a degree.|Diplomlar qadrsizlanishi tufayli endi ko'plab ishlar oliy ma'lumotni talab qiladi.|Jobs that once needed school certificates now ask for university degrees.|Ilgari maktab attestati yetarli bo'lgan ishlar endi oliy ma'lumot so'raydi.|Critics say credential inflation wastes students' time and money.|Tanqidchilar diplomlar qadrsizlanishi talabalarning vaqti va pulini isrof qiladi deyishadi.
epistemic curiosity|noun|/ˌepɪˈstiːmɪk ˌkjʊəriˈɒsəti/|bilimga chanqoqlik|The desire to gain new knowledge and understanding.|intellectual curiosity · thirst for knowledge|yangi bilim va tushuncha olishga intilish.|Good questions spark epistemic curiosity in learners.|Yaxshi savollar o'rganuvchilarda bilimga chanqoqlik uyg'otadi.|Clever questions make students want to find out more.|Aqlli savollar talabalarda ko'proq bilish istagini uyg'otadi.|Epistemic curiosity is linked to better long-term memory.|Bilimga chanqoqlik yaxshiroq uzoq muddatli xotira bilan bog'liq.
autonomous learners|phrase|/ɔːˈtɒnəməs ˈlɜːnəz/|mustaqil o'rganuvchilar|Students who take responsibility for their own learning.|independent learners · self-directed students|o'z ta'limi uchun mas'uliyatni o'z zimmasiga oladigan o'quvchilar.|The course aims to produce autonomous learners.|Kurs mustaqil o'rganuvchilarni tayyorlashni maqsad qiladi.|Students are taught to manage their own studies.|Talabalarga o'z o'qishlarini boshqarish o'rgatiladi.|Autonomous learners set their own goals.|Mustaqil o'rganuvchilar o'z maqsadlarini o'zlari belgilaydi.
Socratic questioning|noun|/səˈkrætɪk ˈkwestʃənɪŋ/|Suqrotona savol berish|Asking a series of questions to help someone think deeply and examine ideas.|probing questions · guided inquiry|kimgadir chuqur o'ylash va g'oyalarni tekshirishga yordam berish uchun bir qator savollar berish.|The teacher used Socratic questioning to challenge assumptions.|O'qituvchi taxminlarga e'tiroz bildirish uchun Suqrotona savol berishdan foydalandi.|By asking question after question, she made students rethink their views.|Ketma-ket savollar berib, u o'quvchilarni o'z qarashlarini qayta ko'rib chiqishga majbur qildi.|Socratic questioning encourages critical thinking.|Suqrotona savol berish tanqidiy fikrlashni rag'batlantiradi.
didactic instruction|noun|/daɪˈdæktɪk ɪnˈstrʌkʃn/|didaktik o'qitish|Teaching in which the teacher talks and students listen.|teacher-led instruction · lecturing · direct teaching|o'qituvchi gapirib, o'quvchilar tinglaydigan o'qitish.|Didactic instruction is still common in many schools.|Didaktik o'qitish ko'plab maktablarda hamon keng tarqalgan.|In many classrooms, the teacher mainly talks and pupils listen.|Ko'plab sinflarda asosan o'qituvchi gapiradi, o'quvchilar tinglaydi.|Critics argue that didactic instruction limits creativity.|Tanqidchilar didaktik o'qitish ijodkorlikni cheklaydi deb ta'kidlashadi.
cultivate analytical acumen|phrase|/ˈkʌltɪveɪt ˌænəˈlɪtɪkl ˈækjʊmən/|tahliliy ziyraklikni rivojlantirmoq|To develop the ability to analyse and judge things sharply.|develop sharp analytical skills · sharpen judgement|narsalarni o'tkir tahlil qilish va baholash qobiliyatini rivojlantirmoq.|Case studies help business students cultivate analytical acumen.|Amaliy holatlar biznes talabalariga tahliliy ziyraklikni rivojlantirishga yordam beradi.|Examining real cases sharpens students' ability to analyse.|Haqiqiy holatlarni o'rganish talabalarning tahlil qilish qobiliyatini o'tkirlashtiradi.|Chess can cultivate analytical acumen.|Shaxmat tahliliy ziyraklikni rivojlantirishi mumkin.
the hidden curriculum|noun|/ðə ˈhɪdn kəˈrɪkjələm/|yashirin o'quv dasturi|The values and behaviour students learn at school without being formally taught.|unwritten lessons · implicit learning at school|o'quvchilar maktabda rasman o'rgatilmasdan o'rganadigan qadriyat va xatti-harakatlar.|Punctuality is part of the hidden curriculum.|Vaqtga rioya qilish yashirin o'quv dasturining bir qismi.|Pupils learn to be on time even though it is not a subject.|O'quvchilar bu fan bo'lmasa ham vaqtida kelishni o'rganishadi.|The hidden curriculum can reinforce social inequality.|Yashirin o'quv dasturi ijtimoiy tengsizlikni kuchaytirishi mumkin.
a sink-or-swim approach|phrase|/ə ˌsɪŋk ɔː ˈswɪm əˈprəʊtʃ/|"yo cho'k, yo suz" yondashuvi|A method in which people must succeed alone without help.|being left to cope alone · trial by fire|odamlar yordamisiz o'zlari muvaffaqiyatga erishishlari kerak bo'lgan usul.|New employees were given a sink-or-swim approach to training.|Yangi xodimlarga "yo cho'k, yo suz" tarzida o'qitish berildi.|New staff had to learn on their own without support.|Yangi xodimlar yordamisiz o'zlari o'rganishlari kerak edi.|A sink-or-swim approach does not suit every student.|"Yo cho'k, yo suz" yondashuvi har bir talabaga mos kelmaydi.
learn by osmosis|phrase|/lɜːn baɪ ɒzˈməʊsɪs/|beixtiyor o'zlashtirmoq|To learn gradually, without deliberate effort, by being around something.|pick up naturally · absorb gradually · learn through exposure|biror narsa atrofida bo'lish orqali, maxsus harakatsiz asta-sekin o'rganmoq.|Children often learn a second language by osmosis.|Bolalar ko'pincha ikkinchi tilni beixtiyor o'zlashtiradi.|Kids pick up languages simply by hearing them around them.|Bolalar tillarni atrofida eshitish orqali o'zlashtiradi.|You cannot learn grammar by osmosis alone.|Grammatikani faqat beixtiyor o'zlashtirib bo'lmaydi.
a solid grounding in|phrase|/ə ˈsɒlɪd ˈɡraʊndɪŋ ɪn/|... bo'yicha mustahkam asos|A good basic knowledge of a subject.|a good foundation in · a firm basis in · basic training in|biror fan bo'yicha yaxshi asosiy bilim.|The course gives students a solid grounding in statistics.|Kurs talabalarga statistika bo'yicha mustahkam asos beradi.|Students finish the course with a good basic knowledge of statistics.|Talabalar kursni statistika bo'yicha yaxshi asosiy bilim bilan tugatadi.|A solid grounding in maths helps in many careers.|Matematika bo'yicha mustahkam asos ko'plab kasblarda yordam beradi.
be steeped in|phrase|/bi stiːpt ɪn/|... ga chuqur singgan bo'lmoq|To be full of a particular quality or influenced by it deeply.|be immersed in · be rich in · be full of|biror xususiyatga to'la yoki undan chuqur ta'sirlangan bo'lmoq.|Bukhara is steeped in history.|Buxoro tarixga chuqur singgan.|The city is full of historical buildings and stories.|Shahar tarixiy binolar va hikoyalarga to'la.|She was steeped in classical music from childhood.|U bolaligidan klassik musiqaga chuqur singib o'sgan.
spoon-feed students|phrase|/ˈspuːn fiːd ˈstjuːdnts/|talabalarga tayyor bilim bermoq|To give students so much help that they do not have to think for themselves.|over-help learners · give ready-made answers|talabalarga shunchalik ko'p yordam berishki, ular o'zlari o'ylamasligi.|Universities complain that schools spoon-feed students.|Universitetlar maktablar o'quvchilarga tayyor bilim beradi deb shikoyat qiladi.|Pupils are given all the answers instead of working them out.|O'quvchilarga javoblarni o'zlari topish o'rniga hammasi tayyor beriladi.|If you spoon-feed students, they cannot work independently.|Talabalarga tayyor bilim bersangiz, ular mustaqil ishlay olmaydi.
the ivory tower|noun|/ði ˈaɪvəri ˈtaʊə/|"fil suyagi minorasi", hayotdan uzilgan akademik muhit|A situation in which academics are separated from the practical problems of ordinary life.|academic isolation · detachment from reality|akademiklar oddiy hayotning amaliy muammolaridan uzilgan vaziyat.|Critics say economists live in an ivory tower.|Tanqidchilar iqtisodchilar hayotdan uzilgan muhitda yashaydi deyishadi.|Some believe academics do not understand real people's problems.|Ba'zilar akademiklar oddiy odamlarning muammolarini tushunmaydi deb hisoblaydi.|The professor left the ivory tower to work with farmers.|Professor akademik muhitni tark etib, dehqonlar bilan ishlay boshladi.
teach to the test|phrase|/tiːtʃ tə ðə test/|faqat imtihonga tayyorlab o'qitmoq|To focus teaching only on what will be examined.|focus only on exam content · exam-driven teaching|o'qitishni faqat imtihonda so'raladigan narsalarga qaratmoq.|Many teachers feel pressure to teach to the test.|Ko'plab o'qituvchilar faqat imtihonga tayyorlab o'qitish bosimini his qiladi.|Teachers often concentrate only on what exams will ask.|O'qituvchilar ko'pincha faqat imtihonda nima so'ralishiga e'tibor qaratadi.|If schools teach to the test, deeper learning suffers.|Maktablar faqat imtihonga tayyorlasa, chuqur o'rganish zarar ko'radi.
erudite|adjective|/ˈerudaɪt/|bilimdon, chuqur bilimli|Having or showing great knowledge from study.|learned · scholarly · well-read · knowledgeable|o'qish orqali olingan katta bilimga ega.|The professor gave an erudite lecture on Persian poetry.|Professor fors she'riyati haqida chuqur bilimli ma'ruza qildi.|The talk showed the speaker's deep knowledge.|Ma'ruza notiqning chuqur bilimini ko'rsatdi.|He was an erudite man who spoke six languages.|U olti tilda gaplasha oladigan bilimdon inson edi.
pedantic|adjective|/pɪˈdæntɪk/|mayda-chuyda narsalarga yopishuvchi|Too concerned with small details or rules.|overly precise · nit-picking · fussy · hair-splitting|mayda tafsilotlar yoki qoidalarga haddan tashqari e'tibor beradigan.|It may sound pedantic, but the date in the report is wrong.|Mayda gap bo'lib tuyulishi mumkin, lekin hisobotdagi sana noto'g'ri.|This is a small point, but the detail is incorrect.|Bu kichik masala, lekin tafsilot noto'g'ri.|Students found the teacher pedantic about spelling.|O'quvchilar o'qituvchini imlo masalasida mayda-chuydaga yopishuvchi deb bilishdi.
polymath|noun|/ˈpɒlimæθ/|qomusiy olim|A person who knows a lot about many different subjects.|all-round scholar · universal genius · Renaissance person|ko'plab turli fanlar bo'yicha ko'p narsa biladigan inson.|Ibn Sina was a polymath who wrote on medicine and philosophy.|Ibn Sino tibbiyot va falsafa haqida yozgan qomusiy olim edi.|He was an expert in many fields, from medicine to philosophy.|U tibbiyotdan falsafagacha ko'plab sohalarda mutaxassis edi.|Leonardo da Vinci is the best-known polymath in history.|Leonardo da Vinchi tarixdagi eng mashhur qomusiy olimdir.
autodidact|noun|/ˈɔːtəʊdaɪdækt/|o'zini o'zi o'qitgan inson|A person who has taught themselves rather than receiving formal education.|self-taught person · self-educated learner|rasmiy ta'lim olmasdan o'zini o'zi o'qitgan inson.|The famous inventor was an autodidact who left school at twelve.|Mashhur ixtirochi o'n ikki yoshida maktabni tashlab, o'zini o'zi o'qitgan.|He taught himself almost everything he knew.|U bilgan deyarli hamma narsani o'zi o'rgangan.|The internet has created a new generation of autodidacts.|Internet o'zini o'zi o'qitgan odamlarning yangi avlodini yaratdi.
precocious|adjective|/prɪˈkəʊʃəs/|erta ulg'aygan, yoshiga nisbatan iqtidorli|Having developed abilities at an unusually early age.|advanced for one's age · gifted · mature beyond one's years|g'ayrioddiy erta yoshda qobiliyatlarni rivojlantirgan.|Mozart was a precocious child who composed music at five.|Motsart besh yoshida musiqa bastalagan iqtidorli bola edi.|He was writing music when most children are just learning to read.|U ko'pchilik bolalar o'qishni endigina o'rganayotgan yoshda musiqa yozgan.|The precocious student finished university at sixteen.|Iqtidorli talaba o'n olti yoshida universitetni tugatdi.
dilettante|noun|/ˌdɪləˈtænti/|havaskor, yuzaki biluvchi|A person who is interested in a subject but does not study it seriously.|amateur · dabbler · non-professional · trifler|biror fanga qiziqadigan, lekin uni jiddiy o'rganmaydigan inson.|Critics dismissed him as a dilettante rather than a true scholar.|Tanqidchilar uni haqiqiy olim emas, yuzaki biluvchi deb rad etishdi.|They said he only played at the subject without real expertise.|Ular u haqiqiy bilimsiz faqat qiziqish uchun shug'ullanadi deyishdi.|She was no dilettante; she had studied the subject for decades.|U havaskor emas edi; bu fanni o'n yilliklar davomida o'rgangan.
be well versed in|phrase|/bi wel vɜːst ɪn/|... ni yaxshi bilmoq|To know a lot about a subject.|be knowledgeable about · be experienced in · be familiar with|biror fan haqida ko'p narsa bilmoq.|The new manager is well versed in international law.|Yangi menejer xalqaro huquqni yaxshi biladi.|She has a deep knowledge of law between countries.|U davlatlararo huquqni chuqur biladi.|Guides must be well versed in local history.|Gidlar mahalliy tarixni yaxshi bilishi kerak.

# Qonun va davlat boshqaruvi|Qonun va davlat
impunity|noun|/ɪmˈpjuːnəti/|jazosizlik|Freedom from punishment.|exemption from punishment · freedom from consequences|jazodan ozod bo'lish.|Corrupt officials acted with impunity for years.|Korrupsiyachi mansabdorlar yillar davomida jazosiz harakat qilishdi.|For a long time, dishonest officials were never punished.|Uzoq vaqt davomida nohalol mansabdorlar hech qachon jazolanmadi.|Strong courts help to end impunity.|Kuchli sudlar jazosizlikka barham berishga yordam beradi.
regulatory capture|noun|/ˈreɡjələtəri ˈkæptʃə/|nazoratchining egallab olinishi|A situation in which a regulator serves the interests of the industry it controls.|industry control of regulators · captured oversight|nazoratchi organ o'zi nazorat qiladigan soha manfaatlariga xizmat qiladigan vaziyat.|Critics accused the agency of regulatory capture by oil companies.|Tanqidchilar agentlikni neft kompaniyalari tomonidan egallab olinganlikda ayblashdi.|The regulator seemed to protect oil firms rather than the public.|Nazoratchi jamoatchilikni emas, neft firmalarini himoya qilgandek tuyuldi.|Regulatory capture weakens public trust.|Nazoratchining egallab olinishi jamoatchilik ishonchini zaiflashtiradi.
executive overreach|noun|/ɪɡˈzekjətɪv ˈəʊvəriːtʃ/|ijroiya hokimiyatning vakolatdan oshishi|A government using more power than the law allows.|abuse of executive power · exceeding authority|hukumatning qonun ruxsat berganidan ko'proq hokimiyatdan foydalanishi.|The court ruled that the decree was executive overreach.|Sud farmonni ijroiya hokimiyatning vakolatdan oshishi deb topdi.|Judges said the government had gone beyond its legal powers.|Sudyalar hukumat qonuniy vakolatlaridan oshib ketganini aytishdi.|Parliament must guard against executive overreach.|Parlament ijroiya hokimiyatning vakolatdan oshishiga qarshi turishi kerak.
legislative gridlock|noun|/ˈledʒɪslətɪv ˈɡrɪdlɒk/|qonunchilikdagi turg'unlik|A situation in which lawmakers cannot agree, so no laws are passed.|political deadlock · parliamentary stalemate|qonun chiqaruvchilar kelisha olmagani uchun hech qanday qonun qabul qilinmaydigan vaziyat.|Legislative gridlock has delayed the budget for months.|Qonunchilikdagi turg'unlik byudjetni oylab kechiktirdi.|Lawmakers could not agree, so the budget was not passed.|Qonun chiqaruvchilar kelisha olmadi va byudjet qabul qilinmadi.|Voters are frustrated by legislative gridlock.|Saylovchilar qonunchilikdagi turg'unlikdan norozi.
retroactive legislation|noun|/ˌretrəʊˈæktɪv ˌledʒɪsˈleɪʃn/|orqaga qaytuvchi kuchga ega qonun|A law that applies to actions taken before it was passed.|backdated law · retrospective legislation|qabul qilinishidan oldin qilingan harakatlarga ham tatbiq etiladigan qonun.|Retroactive legislation is seen as unfair in most legal systems.|Orqaga qaytuvchi kuchga ega qonun ko'pchilik huquq tizimlarida adolatsiz hisoblanadi.|Punishing people for acts that were legal at the time seems unjust.|O'sha paytda qonuniy bo'lgan harakatlar uchun jazolash adolatsiz tuyuladi.|The tax change was introduced as retroactive legislation.|Soliq o'zgarishi orqaga qaytuvchi kuchga ega qonun sifatida joriy etildi.
a fiduciary duty|phrase|/ə fɪˈduːʃəri ˈdjuːti/|ishonchga asoslangan majburiyat|A legal duty to act in the best interests of another person.|a duty of trust · a legal obligation to act faithfully|boshqa shaxsning manfaatlari yo'lida harakat qilish huquqiy majburiyati.|Company directors have a fiduciary duty to shareholders.|Kompaniya direktorlari aksiyadorlar oldida ishonchga asoslangan majburiyatga ega.|Directors must act in the best interests of the owners.|Direktorlar egalar manfaati yo'lida harakat qilishlari shart.|The lawyer breached his fiduciary duty.|Advokat ishonchga asoslangan majburiyatini buzdi.
a precedent-setting ruling|phrase|/ə ˈpresɪdənt ˌsetɪŋ ˈruːlɪŋ/|pretsedent yaratuvchi qaror|A court decision that will guide future cases.|a landmark judgement · a groundbreaking decision|kelajakdagi ishlar uchun yo'l ko'rsatadigan sud qarori.|The court issued a precedent-setting ruling on data privacy.|Sud shaxsiy ma'lumotlar maxfiyligi bo'yicha pretsedent yaratuvchi qaror chiqardi.|Future cases about personal data will follow this decision.|Shaxsiy ma'lumotlar haqidagi kelajakdagi ishlar shu qarorga amal qiladi.|Lawyers called it a precedent-setting ruling.|Advokatlar buni pretsedent yaratuvchi qaror deb atashdi.
codify customary law|phrase|/ˈkəʊdɪfaɪ ˈkʌstəməri lɔː/|odat huquqini kodlashtirmoq|To write traditional unwritten rules into a formal legal code.|write down traditional law · formalise customs|an'anaviy yozilmagan qoidalarni rasmiy huquqiy kodeksga kiritmoq.|Colonial rulers tried to codify customary law.|Mustamlakachi hukmdorlar odat huquqini kodlashtirishga harakat qilishgan.|They wrote down local traditions as official laws.|Ular mahalliy an'analarni rasmiy qonunlar sifatida yozib olishgan.|Codifying customary law can make it less flexible.|Odat huquqini kodlashtirish uni kamroq moslashuvchan qilishi mumkin.
due process|noun|/djuː ˈprəʊses/|qonuniy tartib|The fair legal procedures that must be followed before someone is punished.|fair legal procedure · the right to a fair hearing|kimdir jazolanishidan oldin rioya qilinishi kerak bo'lgan adolatli huquqiy tartib.|Everyone has the right to due process.|Har kim qonuniy tartibga bo'lgan huquqqa ega.|Nobody should be punished without a fair legal process.|Hech kim adolatli huquqiy jarayonsiz jazolanmasligi kerak.|The prisoners were held without due process.|Mahbuslar qonuniy tartibsiz ushlab turildi.
a legal grey area|phrase|/ə ˈliːɡl ɡreɪ ˈeəriə/|qonunda noaniq soha|A situation where it is unclear what the law allows.|an uncertain area of law · a legal loophole|qonun nimaga ruxsat berishi noaniq bo'lgan vaziyat.|Flying drones over private homes is a legal grey area.|Shaxsiy uylar ustidan dron uchirish qonunda noaniq soha.|The law does not clearly say whether this is allowed.|Qonun bunga ruxsat berilganmi yoki yo'qligini aniq aytmaydi.|New technologies often operate in a legal grey area.|Yangi texnologiyalar ko'pincha qonunda noaniq sohada ishlaydi.

jurisprudence|noun|/ˌdʒʊərɪsˈpruːdns/|huquqshunoslik|The study and theory of law.|legal theory · philosophy of law · legal science|huquqni o'rganish va uning nazariyasi.|She studied jurisprudence at Oxford.|U Oksfordda huquqshunoslikni o'rgangan.|She researched legal theory at university.|U universitetda huquq nazariyasini o'rgangan.|Islamic jurisprudence has a long history.|Islom huquqshunosligi uzoq tarixga ega.
