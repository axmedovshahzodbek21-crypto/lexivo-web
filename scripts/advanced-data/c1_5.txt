# Xavf, inqiroz va bardoshlilik|Xavf va inqiroz
bear the brunt of|phrase|/beə ðə brʌnt əv/|... ning eng og'ir zarbasini olmoq|To suffer the worst part of something unpleasant.|suffer most from · take the full force of · be hit hardest by|yoqimsiz narsaning eng og'ir qismini boshdan kechirmoq.|Coastal villages bore the brunt of the storm.|Qirg'oq bo'yidagi qishloqlar bo'ronning eng og'ir zarbasini olishdi.|The seaside villages were hit hardest by the storm.|Dengiz bo'yidagi qishloqlar bo'rondan eng ko'p zarar ko'rdi.|The poor usually bear the brunt of rising prices.|Narxlar oshishining eng og'ir zarbasini odatda kambag'allar oladi.
assess the severity of|phrase|/əˈses ðə sɪˈverəti əv/|... ning jiddiyligini baholamoq|To judge how serious something is.|evaluate how serious · gauge the extent of · measure the seriousness of|biror narsa qanchalik jiddiy ekanini aniqlamoq.|Doctors must quickly assess the severity of each injury.|Shifokorlar har bir jarohatning jiddiyligini tez baholashi kerak.|Medical staff need to decide fast how serious each wound is.|Tibbiyot xodimlari har bir yara qanchalik jiddiy ekanini tez hal qilishi kerak.|Engineers were sent to assess the severity of the damage.|Zararning jiddiyligini baholash uchun muhandislar yuborildi.
vulnerable to external shocks|phrase|/ˈvʌlnərəbl tʊ ɪkˈstɜːnl ʃɒks/|tashqi zarbalarga zaif|Easily harmed by sudden events coming from outside.|exposed to outside crises · fragile in the face of global events|tashqaridan keladigan to'satdan hodisalardan osongina zarar ko'radigan.|Small economies are vulnerable to external shocks such as oil price rises.|Kichik iqtisodiyotlar neft narxining oshishi kabi tashqi zarbalarga zaif.|Countries with small economies are easily hurt by global events.|Kichik iqtisodiyotli davlatlar global voqealardan osongina zarar ko'radi.|Relying on one export makes a country vulnerable to external shocks.|Bitta eksportga tayanish mamlakatni tashqi zarbalarga zaif qiladi.
cascading failures|phrase|/kæˈskeɪdɪŋ ˈfeɪljəz/|zanjirli nosozliklar|A series of failures in which one causes the next.|a chain reaction · knock-on breakdowns · domino failures|biri ikkinchisiga sabab bo'ladigan nosozliklar ketma-ketligi.|One broken power line caused cascading failures across the grid.|Bitta uzilgan elektr liniyasi butun tarmoqda zanjirli nosozliklarga sabab bo'ldi.|A single fault spread until the whole power network went down.|Bitta nosozlik butun elektr tarmog'i o'chguncha tarqaldi.|Engineers design systems to prevent cascading failures.|Muhandislar zanjirli nosozliklarning oldini oladigan tizimlar loyihalaydi.
contain the fallout|phrase|/kənˈteɪn ðə ˈfɔːlaʊt/|salbiy oqibatlarni cheklamoq|To limit the bad results of an event.|limit the damage · control the consequences · minimise the impact|voqeaning yomon oqibatlarini cheklamoq.|The company hired experts to contain the fallout from the scandal.|Kompaniya janjal oqibatlarini cheklash uchun mutaxassislar yolladi.|Specialists were brought in to reduce the damage caused by the scandal.|Janjal yetkazgan zararni kamaytirish uchun mutaxassislar jalb qilindi.|Governments acted quickly to contain the fallout of the crisis.|Hukumatlar inqiroz oqibatlarini cheklash uchun tez harakat qilishdi.
pose an existential threat|phrase|/pəʊz ən ˌeɡzɪˈstenʃl θret/|mavjudlikka tahdid solmoq|To be a danger to the continued existence of something.|threaten survival · endanger the very existence of|biror narsaning mavjud bo'lib qolishiga xavf tug'dirmoq.|Rising seas pose an existential threat to small island nations.|Dengiz sathining ko'tarilishi kichik orol davlatlarining mavjudligiga tahdid soladi.|Some island countries may disappear completely under water.|Ba'zi orol davlatlari butunlay suv ostida qolib ketishi mumkin.|Some experts believe AI could pose an existential threat.|Ba'zi mutaxassislar sun'iy intellekt mavjudlikka tahdid solishi mumkin deb hisoblaydi.
reach a tipping point|phrase|/riːtʃ ə ˈtɪpɪŋ pɔɪnt/|burilish nuqtasiga yetmoq|To reach the moment when a small change causes a big, often irreversible, effect.|reach a critical point · cross a threshold · reach the point of no return|kichik o'zgarish katta, ko'pincha qaytarib bo'lmas ta'sirga olib keladigan paytga yetmoq.|Scientists warn that the rainforest may reach a tipping point.|Olimlar tropik o'rmon burilish nuqtasiga yetishi mumkinligidan ogohlantirmoqda.|At some point, the forest could suddenly turn into grassland.|Bir paytda o'rmon to'satdan o'tloqqa aylanib qolishi mumkin.|Public anger reached a tipping point after the price rise.|Narx oshganidan keyin jamoatchilik g'azabi burilish nuqtasiga yetdi.
expose systemic flaws|phrase|/ɪkˈspəʊz sɪˈstemɪk flɔːz/|tizimdagi kamchiliklarni fosh qilmoq|To reveal weaknesses in a whole system rather than in one part.|reveal structural weaknesses · uncover deep problems|bitta qismda emas, butun tizimdagi zaif tomonlarni ochib bermoq.|The crisis exposed systemic flaws in the banking sector.|Inqiroz bank sohasidagi tizimli kamchiliklarni fosh qildi.|The emergency showed that the whole banking system had weaknesses.|Favqulodda vaziyat butun bank tizimida kamchiliklar borligini ko'rsatdi.|The inquiry exposed systemic flaws in how hospitals report errors.|Tergov shifoxonalarning xatolar haqida xabar berish tartibidagi tizimli kamchiliklarni fosh qildi.
collateral damage|noun|/kəˈlætərəl ˈdæmɪdʒ/|yondosh zarar|Harm caused to people or things that were not the intended target.|unintended harm · side effects · accidental damage|nishon bo'lmagan odamlar yoki narsalarga yetkazilgan zarar.|Small businesses became collateral damage in the trade war.|Kichik bizneslar savdo urushida yondosh zararga uchradi.|Small firms were hurt even though they were not the target.|Kichik firmalar nishon bo'lmasa ham zarar ko'rdi.|Pesticides cause collateral damage to harmless insects.|Pestitsidlar zararsiz hasharotlarga ham yondosh zarar yetkazadi.
the precautionary principle|noun|/ðə prɪˈkɔːʃənəri ˈprɪnsəpl/|ehtiyot tamoyili|The idea that action should be taken to prevent harm even before there is full proof.|better safe than sorry · erring on the side of caution|to'liq isbot bo'lmasa ham, zararning oldini olish uchun chora ko'rish kerak degan g'oya.|The ban was based on the precautionary principle.|Taqiq ehtiyot tamoyiliga asoslangan edi.|The product was banned because it might be harmful, even without proof.|Mahsulot isbot bo'lmasa ham zararli bo'lishi mumkinligi sababli taqiqlandi.|Environmental law often follows the precautionary principle.|Ekologik qonunchilik ko'pincha ehtiyot tamoyiliga amal qiladi.
a black swan event|phrase|/ə blæk swɒn ɪˈvent/|kutilmagan noyob hodisa|A rare and unpredictable event with very serious consequences.|an unforeseeable crisis · a shock event · a rare catastrophe|juda jiddiy oqibatlarga ega noyob va oldindan aytib bo'lmaydigan hodisa.|The pandemic was described as a black swan event.|Pandemiya kutilmagan noyob hodisa sifatida ta'riflandi.|Hardly anyone predicted the pandemic or its enormous impact.|Pandemiyani va uning ulkan ta'sirini deyarli hech kim oldindan ko'rmagan.|Investors cannot plan for every black swan event.|Investorlar har bir kutilmagan noyob hodisaga tayyorlana olmaydi.
adaptive capacity|noun|/əˈdæptɪv kəˈpæsəti/|moslashish qobiliyati|The ability of a system or community to adjust to change.|ability to adapt · flexibility · resilience|tizim yoki jamoaning o'zgarishlarga moslasha olish qobiliyati.|Poorer regions have less adaptive capacity to cope with climate change.|Kambag'alroq mintaqalarning iqlim o'zgarishiga moslashish qobiliyati pastroq.|Poor areas find it harder to adjust to a changing climate.|Kambag'al hududlarga o'zgarayotgan iqlimga moslashish qiyinroq.|Diverse ecosystems tend to have greater adaptive capacity.|Xilma-xil ekotizimlar odatda kattaroq moslashish qobiliyatiga ega.
avert impending collapse|phrase|/əˈvɜːt ɪmˈpendɪŋ kəˈlæps/|yaqinlashib kelayotgan inqirozning oldini olmoq|To prevent a failure that is about to happen.|prevent imminent failure · stop a looming breakdown|yuz berish arafasidagi barbodlikning oldini olmoq.|An emergency loan helped to avert impending collapse of the bank.|Favqulodda qarz bankning yaqinlashib kelayotgan barbod bo'lishining oldini olishga yordam berdi.|The bank was about to fail until it received urgent funding.|Bank shoshilinch mablag' olguncha barbod bo'lish arafasida edi.|Strict fishing limits were introduced to avert impending collapse of fish stocks.|Baliq zaxiralarining yaqinlashib kelayotgan barbod bo'lishining oldini olish uchun qat'iy ov cheklovlari joriy qilindi.
a volatile environment|phrase|/ə ˈvɒlətaɪl ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/|beqaror muhit|A situation that can change suddenly and unpredictably.|an unstable situation · an unpredictable climate · a turbulent setting|to'satdan va oldindan aytib bo'lmaydigan tarzda o'zgarishi mumkin bo'lgan vaziyat.|Companies struggle to plan in such a volatile environment.|Kompaniyalar bunday beqaror muhitda rejalashtirishda qiynaladi.|Firms find it hard to plan when conditions keep changing suddenly.|Sharoitlar doimo to'satdan o'zgarsa, firmalarga rejalashtirish qiyin.|Journalists often work in a volatile environment.|Jurnalistlar ko'pincha beqaror muhitda ishlaydi.
stress-test infrastructure|phrase|/ˈstres test ˈɪnfrəstrʌktʃə/|infratuzilmani bardoshlilikka sinamoq|To check whether roads, power systems or buildings can cope with extreme conditions.|test resilience · check systems under pressure|yo'llar, energiya tizimlari yoki binolar o'ta og'ir sharoitlarga bardosh bera olishini tekshirmoq.|Engineers stress-test infrastructure to prepare for floods.|Muhandislar suv toshqinlariga tayyorlanish uchun infratuzilmani bardoshlilikka sinaydi.|Experts check whether systems would survive extreme flooding.|Mutaxassislar tizimlar kuchli suv toshqiniga bardosh berishini tekshiradi.|Cities are beginning to stress-test infrastructure against heatwaves.|Shaharlar infratuzilmani issiq to'lqinlariga bardoshlilikka sinay boshlamoqda.
catastrophic|adjective|/ˌkætəˈstrɒfɪk/|halokatli|Causing great damage or suffering.|disastrous · devastating · calamitous · ruinous|katta zarar yoki azob-uqubatga sabab bo'ladigan.|The earthquake had catastrophic consequences.|Zilzila halokatli oqibatlarga olib keldi.|The earthquake caused enormous destruction and loss of life.|Zilzila ulkan vayronagarchilik va qurbonlarga sabab bo'ldi.|A small error could have catastrophic results.|Kichik xato halokatli natijalarga olib kelishi mumkin.
fragility|noun|/frəˈdʒɪləti/|mo'rtlik, zaiflik|The quality of being easily broken or damaged.|weakness · delicacy · vulnerability · frailty|osongina sinadigan yoki shikastlanadigan bo'lish sifati.|The pandemic revealed the fragility of global supply chains.|Pandemiya global ta'minot zanjirlarining zaifligini ochib berdi.|The crisis showed how easily world trade could break down.|Inqiroz jahon savdosi qanchalik oson buzilishi mumkinligini ko'rsatdi.|Museum staff handle the vase carefully because of its fragility.|Muzey xodimlari vazaning mo'rtligi tufayli unga ehtiyotkorlik bilan munosabatda bo'lishadi.
redundancy|noun|/rɪˈdʌndənsi/|zaxira (tizimdagi); ishdan bo'shatish|Extra parts that keep a system working if one fails; or job loss.|backup · spare capacity · duplication · lay-off|biri ishdan chiqsa ham tizimni ishlatadigan qo'shimcha qismlar; yoki ishdan bo'shatish.|Aircraft are built with redundancy so a single failure is not fatal.|Samolyotlar bitta nosozlik halokatli bo'lmasligi uchun zaxira tizimlar bilan quriladi.|Planes have backup systems in case one part breaks.|Samolyotlarda biror qism buzilsa ishlaydigan zaxira tizimlar bor.|The factory closure led to 300 redundancies.|Zavodning yopilishi 300 kishining ishdan bo'shatilishiga olib keldi.
imminent|adjective|/ˈɪmɪnənt/|yaqinlashib kelayotgan, muqarrar|About to happen very soon.|impending · approaching · forthcoming · on the horizon|juda tez orada yuz beradigan.|Residents were warned of an imminent flood.|Aholiga yaqinlashib kelayotgan suv toshqini haqida ogohlantirish berildi.|People were told the water would rise very soon.|Odamlarga suv tez orada ko'tarilishi aytildi.|There is no sign of an imminent recovery.|Tez orada tiklanish belgilari yo'q.
safeguard|verb|/ˈseɪfɡɑːd/|himoya qilmoq|To protect something from harm.|protect · preserve · secure · defend|biror narsani zarardan himoya qilmoq.|New laws aim to safeguard children online.|Yangi qonunlar bolalarni internetda himoya qilishga qaratilgan.|The rules are meant to keep young people safe on the internet.|Qoidalar yoshlarni internetda xavfsiz saqlashga mo'ljallangan.|We must safeguard water supplies for future generations.|Biz kelajak avlodlar uchun suv manbalarini himoya qilishimiz kerak.
# Baholash, daraja va qamrov|Baholash va qamrov

a disproportionate share|phrase|/ə ˌdɪsprəˈpɔːʃənət ʃeə/|nomutanosib ulush|An amount that is too large or small compared with what is fair.|an unfair proportion · more than its fair share|adolatli miqdorga nisbatan juda katta yoki kichik ulush.|The richest 1% own a disproportionate share of the wealth.|Eng boy 1% boylikning nomutanosib ulushiga egalik qiladi.|A tiny group of people owns far more than their fair share.|Kichik bir guruh odamlar adolatli ulushidan ancha ko'proq narsaga egalik qiladi.|Women do a disproportionate share of unpaid work.|Ayollar haq to'lanmaydigan ishning nomutanosib ulushini bajaradi.

# Dalillash va muallif pozitsiyasi|Muallif pozitsiyasi
dismiss a theory as flawed|phrase|/dɪsˈmɪs ə ˈθɪəri æz flɔːd/|nazariyani noto'g'ri deb rad etmoq|To reject an idea because you think it has serious mistakes.|reject a theory · write off an idea · discount a hypothesis|jiddiy xatolari bor deb biror g'oyani rad etmoq.|Many scientists dismissed the theory as flawed.|Ko'plab olimlar nazariyani noto'g'ri deb rad etishdi.|Lots of researchers rejected the idea as mistaken.|Ko'plab tadqiqotchilar g'oyani xato deb rad etishdi.|It is too early to dismiss the theory as flawed.|Nazariyani noto'g'ri deb rad etishga hali erta.
lend weight to|phrase|/lend weɪt tuː/|... ga salmoq qo'shmoq, ... ni kuchaytirmoq|To make an argument or idea seem more likely to be true.|support · strengthen · reinforce · add credibility to|dalil yoki fikrni haqiqatga yaqinroq qilib ko'rsatmoq.|The new data lend weight to the argument for a sugar tax.|Yangi ma'lumotlar shakar solig'i foydasidagi dalilga salmoq qo'shadi.|The figures strengthen the case for taxing sugar.|Raqamlar shakarga soliq solish foydasidagi pozitsiyani kuchaytiradi.|Her experience lends weight to her opinion.|Uning tajribasi fikriga salmoq qo'shadi.
adopt a cautious stance|phrase|/əˈdɒpt ə ˈkɔːʃəs stæns/|ehtiyotkor pozitsiyani egallamoq|To take a careful position and avoid strong claims.|be careful · take a conservative position · tread carefully|ehtiyotkor pozitsiyani tutib, keskin da'volardan qochmoq.|The author adopts a cautious stance on the benefits of the drug.|Muallif dorining foydalari masalasida ehtiyotkor pozitsiyani egallaydi.|The writer is careful not to overstate how helpful the medicine is.|Yozuvchi dori qanchalik foydali ekanini oshirib yubormaslikka harakat qiladi.|The bank adopted a cautious stance on lending.|Bank kredit berishda ehtiyotkor pozitsiyani egalladi.
refute an argument|phrase|/rɪˈfjuːt ən ˈɑːɡjumənt/|dalilni rad etmoq|To prove that an argument is wrong.|disprove · counter · rebut · contradict|dalil noto'g'ri ekanini isbotlamoq.|The study refutes the argument that video games cause violence.|Tadqiqot video o'yinlar zo'ravonlikka sabab bo'ladi degan dalilni rad etadi.|Research shows that games do not make players violent.|Tadqiqot o'yinlar o'yinchilarni zo'ravon qilmasligini ko'rsatadi.|She used statistics to refute the argument.|U dalilni rad etish uchun statistikadan foydalandi.
concede a point|phrase|/kənˈsiːd ə pɔɪnt/|biror fikrni tan olmoq|To admit that something an opponent says is true.|admit · acknowledge · accept · grant|raqib aytgan narsa to'g'ri ekanini tan olmoq.|The minister conceded the point that costs had risen.|Vazir xarajatlar oshganini tan oldi.|The minister admitted that the price had gone up.|Vazir narx oshganini tan oldi.|A good debater knows when to concede a point.|Yaxshi munozarachi qachon biror fikrni tan olishni biladi.
underscore the need for|phrase|/ˌʌndəˈskɔː ðə niːd fɔː/|... zarurligini ta'kidlamoq|To emphasise that something is necessary.|highlight the need for · stress the importance of · emphasise|biror narsa zarur ekanini alohida ta'kidlamoq.|The floods underscore the need for better drainage.|Suv toshqinlari yaxshiroq kanalizatsiya zarurligini ta'kidlaydi.|The flooding shows clearly that drains must be improved.|Toshqin zovurlarni yaxshilash kerakligini aniq ko'rsatadi.|The report underscores the need for more teachers.|Hisobot ko'proq o'qituvchi zarurligini ta'kidlaydi.
draw a flawed conclusion|phrase|/drɔː ə flɔːd kənˈkluːʒn/|noto'g'ri xulosa chiqarmoq|To reach a judgement that is wrong because of faulty reasoning.|reach the wrong conclusion · misinterpret the evidence|noto'g'ri mulohaza tufayli xato hukmga kelmoq.|The journalist drew a flawed conclusion from limited data.|Jurnalist cheklangan ma'lumotlardan noto'g'ri xulosa chiqardi.|With so little information, the reporter's judgement was mistaken.|Bunchalik kam ma'lumot bilan muxbirning xulosasi xato edi.|It is easy to draw a flawed conclusion from a small sample.|Kichik tanlanmadan noto'g'ri xulosa chiqarish oson.
champion a cause|phrase|/ˈtʃæmpiən ə kɔːz/|biror g'oya uchun kurashmoq|To publicly support and fight for a belief or aim.|campaign for · fight for · promote · advocate|biror e'tiqod yoki maqsadni ochiq qo'llab-quvvatlab, u uchun kurashmoq.|She has championed the cause of girls' education for years.|U yillar davomida qizlar ta'limi uchun kurashib keladi.|She has long campaigned for girls to go to school.|U uzoq vaqtdan beri qizlarning maktabga borishi uchun kurashadi.|Famous people often champion a cause they care about.|Mashhur odamlar ko'pincha o'zlari qayg'uradigan g'oya uchun kurashadi.
gloss over|phrasal verb|/ɡlɒs ˈəʊvə/|ko'z yummoq, yuzaki o'tib ketmoq|To avoid discussing something properly, especially a problem.|skate over · ignore · play down · brush aside|biror narsani, ayniqsa muammoni, to'g'ri muhokama qilishdan qochmoq.|The report glosses over the environmental costs of the project.|Hisobot loyihaning ekologik xarajatlariga ko'z yumadi.|The report does not deal properly with the harm to nature.|Hisobot tabiatga yetkaziladigan zararni to'g'ri ko'rib chiqmaydi.|Politicians tend to gloss over their mistakes.|Siyosatchilar o'z xatolariga ko'z yumishga moyil.
substantiate a claim|phrase|/səbˈstænʃieɪt ə kleɪm/|da'voni asoslamoq|To provide evidence that a statement is true.|prove · support · back up · verify|biror fikr to'g'ri ekaniga dalil keltirmoq.|The company could not substantiate its claim that the cream works.|Kompaniya krem ishlaydi degan da'vosini asoslay olmadi.|There was no evidence that the product did what was promised.|Mahsulot va'da qilingan natijani berishiga dalil yo'q edi.|Scientists need data to substantiate a claim.|Olimlarga da'voni asoslash uchun ma'lumotlar kerak.
overstate the case|phrase|/ˌəʊvəˈsteɪt ðə keɪs/|oshirib yubormoq|To make something seem more important or extreme than it really is.|exaggerate · overstate · overplay · blow out of proportion|biror narsani aslidan muhimroq yoki keskinroq qilib ko'rsatmoq.|Critics say the author overstates the case against social media.|Tanqidchilar muallif ijtimoiy tarmoqlarga qarshi fikrni oshirib yuboradi deyishadi.|The writer may exaggerate how harmful social media is.|Yozuvchi ijtimoiy tarmoqlar qanchalik zararli ekanini oshirib yuborayotgan bo'lishi mumkin.|It would be overstating the case to call it a crisis.|Buni inqiroz deyish oshirib yuborish bo'lardi.
harbour doubts about|phrase|/ˈhɑːbə daʊts əˈbaʊt/|... haqida shubha saqlamoq|To keep feeling uncertain about something.|have reservations about · be sceptical of · question|biror narsa haqida noaniqlikni his qilishda davom etmoq.|Many experts still harbour doubts about the plan.|Ko'plab mutaxassislar hamon rejaga nisbatan shubha saqlaydi.|Lots of specialists remain unsure whether the plan will work.|Ko'plab mutaxassislar reja ishlashiga hali ishonchi komil emas.|She harboured doubts about her decision for years.|U yillar davomida qaroriga nisbatan shubha saqladi.
debunk a myth|phrase|/diːˈbʌŋk ə mɪθ/|afsonani fosh qilmoq|To show that a widely held belief is false.|disprove a myth · expose a falsehood · dispel a misconception|keng tarqalgan e'tiqod noto'g'ri ekanini ko'rsatmoq.|The study debunks the myth that we only use 10% of our brains.|Tadqiqot biz miyamizning faqat 10 foizidan foydalanamiz degan afsonani fosh qiladi.|Research shows that we actually use all of our brain.|Tadqiqot biz aslida miyamizning hammasidan foydalanishimizni ko'rsatadi.|Scientists work hard to debunk a myth once it spreads.|Afsona tarqalgach, olimlar uni fosh qilish uchun ko'p mehnat qiladi.
undermine the credibility of|phrase|/ˌʌndəˈmaɪn ðə ˌkredəˈbɪləti əv/|... ning ishonchliligiga putur yetkazmoq|To make people trust someone or something less.|damage the reputation of · weaken trust in · discredit|odamlarning kimgadir yoki biror narsaga ishonchini kamaytirmoq.|The errors undermine the credibility of the report.|Xatolar hisobotning ishonchliligiga putur yetkazadi.|Because of the mistakes, readers trust the report less.|Xatolar tufayli o'quvchilar hisobotga kamroq ishonadi.|Fake reviews undermine the credibility of online shops.|Soxta sharhlar onlayn do'konlarning ishonchliligiga putur yetkazadi.
cogent|adjective|/ˈkəʊdʒənt/|asosli, ishonarli|Clear, logical and convincing.|convincing · persuasive · compelling · well-argued|aniq, mantiqiy va ishonarli.|She presented a cogent argument for reform.|U islohot foydasiga ishonarli dalil keltirdi.|Her case for change was clear and convincing.|Uning o'zgarish foydasidagi fikri aniq va ishonarli edi.|The report offers no cogent reason for the delay.|Hisobot kechikish uchun asosli sabab keltirmaydi.
espouse|verb|/ɪˈspaʊz/|qo'llab-quvvatlamoq, tarafdori bo'lmoq|To support an idea or belief.|support · adopt · embrace · advocate|biror g'oya yoki e'tiqodni qo'llab-quvvatlamoq.|The party espouses free-market economics.|Partiya erkin bozor iqtisodiyotini qo'llab-quvvatlaydi.|The party believes in leaving the economy to the market.|Partiya iqtisodiyotni bozorga topshirish tarafdori.|He espoused the idea of universal education.|U umumiy ta'lim g'oyasini qo'llab-quvvatladi.
rebut|verb|/rɪˈbʌt/|rad javob bermoq|To argue that a criticism or claim is false.|refute · counter · deny · contradict|tanqid yoki da'vo noto'g'ri ekanini isbotlamoq.|The company rebutted claims that its products were unsafe.|Kompaniya mahsulotlari xavfli degan da'volarni rad etdi.|The firm argued that its goods were perfectly safe.|Firma o'z mahsulotlari mutlaqo xavfsiz ekanini ta'kidladi.|She rebutted every point her opponent made.|U raqibining har bir fikriga rad javob berdi.
tenuous|adjective|/ˈtenjuəs/|zaif, ishonchsiz|Very weak or slight.|weak · flimsy · slight · shaky|juda zaif yoki arzimas.|The link between the two events is tenuous at best.|Ikki voqea o'rtasidagi bog'liqlik juda zaif.|There is hardly any real connection between the events.|Voqealar o'rtasida deyarli haqiqiy bog'liqlik yo'q.|The evidence for his theory is tenuous.|Uning nazariyasi uchun dalillar zaif.
unsubstantiated|adjective|/ˌʌnsəbˈstænʃieɪtɪd/|asossiz|Not supported by evidence.|unproven · unsupported · unfounded · baseless|dalil bilan tasdiqlanmagan.|The article makes unsubstantiated claims about the vaccine.|Maqolada vaksina haqida asossiz da'volar bor.|The writer's statements about the vaccine have no evidence behind them.|Muallifning vaksina haqidagi bayonotlari dalilsiz.|Police said the rumours were unsubstantiated.|Politsiya mish-mishlar asossiz ekanini aytdi.
contentious|adjective|/kənˈtenʃəs/|bahsli|Likely to cause disagreement.|controversial · disputed · debatable · divisive|kelishmovchilikka sabab bo'lishi mumkin bo'lgan.|Immigration remains a contentious issue.|Immigratsiya bahsli masala bo'lib qolmoqda.|People continue to disagree strongly about immigration.|Odamlar immigratsiya bo'yicha qattiq kelishmay kelmoqda.|The plan to build a new airport is highly contentious.|Yangi aeroport qurish rejasi juda bahsli.
# Vaqt va davrlar|Vaqt va davrlar
a transient phenomenon|phrase|/ə ˈtrænziənt fəˈnɒmɪnən/|o'tkinchi hodisa|Something that exists or happens for only a short time.|a passing trend · a temporary occurrence · a short-lived event|qisqa vaqt mavjud bo'ladigan yoki sodir bo'ladigan narsa.|Some economists believe the price rise is a transient phenomenon.|Ba'zi iqtisodchilar narx oshishi o'tkinchi hodisa deb hisoblaydi.|They think prices will soon return to normal.|Ular narxlar tez orada odatiy holiga qaytadi deb o'ylashadi.|The craze proved to be a transient phenomenon.|Bu qiziqish o'tkinchi hodisa bo'lib chiqdi.
perpetuate a cycle|phrase|/pəˈpetʃueɪt ə ˈsaɪkl/|aylanma jarayonni davom ettirmoq|To make a repeated pattern, usually a bad one, continue.|keep a pattern going · maintain a vicious circle|odatda yomon takroriy holatni davom ettirmoq.|Poverty can perpetuate a cycle of poor health.|Qashshoqlik yomon sog'liqning aylanma jarayonini davom ettirishi mumkin.|Being poor leads to illness, which leads to more poverty.|Kambag'allik kasallikka, kasallik esa yanada kambag'allikka olib keladi.|Punishment alone may perpetuate a cycle of crime.|Faqat jazolash jinoyatchilik aylanmasini davom ettirishi mumkin.
culminate in|phrasal verb|/ˈkʌlmɪneɪt ɪn/|... bilan yakunlanmoq|To end with a particular result, often after a long process.|end in · lead to · result in · reach a climax in|ko'pincha uzoq jarayondan keyin ma'lum natija bilan tugamoq.|Years of protest culminated in a change of government.|Yillar davom etgan noroziliklar hukumat almashinuvi bilan yakunlandi.|The long protests finally led to a new government.|Uzoq davom etgan noroziliklar nihoyat yangi hukumatga olib keldi.|The festival culminates in a firework display.|Festival mushakbozlik bilan yakunlanadi.
herald the arrival of|phrase|/ˈherəld ði əˈraɪvl əv/|... kelishidan darak bermoq|To be a sign that something is about to happen or begin.|signal the start of · announce · mark the coming of|biror narsa boshlanish arafasida ekanining belgisi bo'lmoq.|The first flowers herald the arrival of spring.|Ilk gullar bahor kelishidan darak beradi.|When the first flowers appear, spring is coming.|Ilk gullar paydo bo'lganda bahor kelmoqda bo'ladi.|The new phone heralded the arrival of the mobile internet age.|Yangi telefon mobil internet davri kelishidan darak berdi.
protracted negotiations|phrase|/prəˈtræktɪd nɪˌɡəʊʃiˈeɪʃnz/|cho'zilib ketgan muzokaralar|Discussions that last much longer than expected.|lengthy talks · drawn-out discussions · prolonged bargaining|kutilganidan ancha uzoq davom etgan muhokamalar.|The deal was signed after protracted negotiations.|Kelishuv cho'zilib ketgan muzokaralardan keyin imzolandi.|Talks went on for a very long time before agreement was reached.|Kelishuvga erishilgunga qadar muzokaralar juda uzoq davom etdi.|Protracted negotiations delayed the start of construction.|Cho'zilib ketgan muzokaralar qurilish boshlanishini kechiktirdi.
enduring appeal|phrase|/ɪnˈdjʊərɪŋ əˈpiːl/|o'chmas jozibadorlik|Attraction that lasts for a very long time.|lasting popularity · timeless attraction · continuing charm|juda uzoq vaqt davom etadigan jozibadorlik.|The enduring appeal of fairy tales lies in their simple messages.|Ertaklarning o'chmas jozibadorligi ularning oddiy g'oyalarida.|Fairy tales stay popular because their lessons are simple.|Ertaklar sabaqlari oddiy bo'lgani uchun mashhurligicha qolmoqda.|The enduring appeal of the Silk Road attracts tourists today.|Ipak yo'lining o'chmas jozibadorligi bugun ham sayyohlarni jalb qiladi.
a watershed moment|phrase|/ə ˈwɔːtəʃed ˈməʊmənt/|tarixiy burilish lahzasi|A time when an important change happens.|a turning point · a landmark · a defining moment|muhim o'zgarish yuz beradigan vaqt.|The first moon landing was a watershed moment in history.|Oyga birinchi qo'nish tarixdagi burilish lahzasi bo'ldi.|Landing on the moon changed how people saw space travel.|Oyga qo'nish odamlarning koinot sayohatiga qarashini o'zgartirdi.|The court case was a watershed moment for women's rights.|Sud ishi ayollar huquqlari uchun burilish lahzasi bo'ldi.
a chronological sequence|phrase|/ə ˌkrɒnəˈlɒdʒɪkl ˈsiːkwəns/|xronologik ketma-ketlik|An order based on when things happened.|time order · order of events · sequence by date|narsalar qachon sodir bo'lganiga asoslangan tartib.|The exhibition presents the paintings in a chronological sequence.|Ko'rgazma rasmlarni xronologik ketma-ketlikda taqdim etadi.|The pictures are arranged in the order they were painted.|Rasmlar chizilgan tartibida joylashtirilgan.|Put the events in a chronological sequence.|Voqealarni xronologik ketma-ketlikda joylashtiring.
prefigure later developments|phrase|/priːˈfɪɡə ˈleɪtə dɪˈveləpmənts/|keyingi o'zgarishlarni oldindan ko'rsatmoq|To show early signs of something that happened later.|anticipate later changes · foreshadow · point ahead to|keyinroq sodir bo'lgan narsaning ilk belgilarini ko'rsatmoq.|Her early sketches prefigure later developments in modern art.|Uning ilk eskizlari zamonaviy san'atdagi keyingi o'zgarishlarni oldindan ko'rsatadi.|Her first drawings already show ideas that modern artists later used.|Uning ilk chizmalarida zamonaviy rassomlar keyinchalik qo'llagan g'oyalar allaqachon ko'rinadi.|The old machine prefigured later developments in computing.|Eski mashina hisoblash texnikasidagi keyingi o'zgarishlarni oldindan ko'rsatgan.
a perennial challenge|phrase|/ə pəˈreniəl ˈtʃælɪndʒ/|doimiy muammo|A problem that keeps returning year after year.|a recurring problem · a long-standing issue · a persistent difficulty|yildan-yilga qaytib keladigan muammo.|Traffic congestion is a perennial challenge for the city.|Transport tirbandligi shahar uchun doimiy muammo.|The city has struggled with traffic for many years.|Shahar ko'p yillardan beri tirbandlik bilan kurashib kelmoqda.|Recruiting teachers is a perennial challenge in rural areas.|Qishloq joylarda o'qituvchi yollash doimiy muammodir.
concurrently with|phrase|/kənˈkʌrəntli wɪð/|... bilan bir vaqtda|At the same time as something else.|simultaneously with · at the same time as · alongside|boshqa narsa bilan bir vaqtning o'zida.|The drug was tested concurrently with the existing treatment.|Dori mavjud davolash usuli bilan bir vaqtda sinab ko'rildi.|Both treatments were tried at the same time.|Ikkala davolash usuli bir vaqtda sinab ko'rildi.|The two projects are running concurrently with each other.|Ikki loyiha bir-biri bilan bir vaqtda amalga oshirilmoqda.
pre-date|verb|/ˌpriːˈdeɪt/|... dan oldinroq bo'lmoq|To exist or happen earlier than something else.|come before · precede · be older than · antedate|boshqa narsadan oldinroq mavjud bo'lmoq yoki sodir bo'lmoq.|These cave paintings pre-date the pyramids by thousands of years.|Bu g'or rasmlari piramidalardan ming yillar oldinroq chizilgan.|The paintings are much older than the pyramids.|Rasmlar piramidalardan ancha qadimiyroq.|The tradition pre-dates the arrival of Islam in the region.|Bu an'ana mintaqaga islom kirib kelishidan oldin paydo bo'lgan.
anachronism|noun|/əˈnækrənɪzəm/|anaxronizm, zamonaviy bo'lmagan narsa|Something that seems to belong to a different time period.|something out of date · a relic of the past · a misplaced detail|boshqa davrga tegishli bo'lib tuyuladigan narsa.|Some see the royal ceremony as an anachronism.|Ba'zilar qirollik marosimini anaxronizm deb bilishadi.|Certain people think the ceremony belongs to the past.|Ayrim odamlar bu marosim o'tmishga tegishli deb o'ylashadi.|A wristwatch in a film about ancient Rome is an anachronism.|Qadimgi Rim haqidagi filmdagi qo'l soati anaxronizmdir.
epoch|noun|/ˈiːpɒk/|davr, epoxa|A long period of time in history marked by particular events.|era · age · period · time|muayyan voqealar bilan ajralib turadigan uzoq tarixiy davr.|The invention of writing began a new epoch in human history.|Yozuvning ixtiro qilinishi insoniyat tarixida yangi davrni boshlab berdi.|Writing started a completely new stage of history.|Yozuv tarixning butunlay yangi bosqichini boshlab berdi.|Scientists debate whether we are living in a new geological epoch.|Olimlar biz yangi geologik davrda yashayapmizmi, degan masalada bahslashadi.
fleeting|adjective|/ˈfliːtɪŋ/|o'tkinchi, qisqa|Lasting for a very short time.|brief · momentary · short-lived · passing|juda qisqa vaqt davom etadigan.|Fame on social media is often fleeting.|Ijtimoiy tarmoqlardagi shuhrat ko'pincha o'tkinchi.|Online popularity usually lasts only a short time.|Onlayn mashhurlik odatda qisqa vaqt davom etadi.|We caught a fleeting glimpse of the rare bird.|Biz noyob qushni bir lahza ko'rib qoldik.
incipient|adjective|/ɪnˈsɪpiənt/|boshlang'ich, endigina paydo bo'layotgan|Beginning to happen or develop.|beginning · emerging · developing · early|endigina yuz bera boshlagan yoki rivojlanayotgan.|Doctors detected signs of incipient disease.|Shifokorlar boshlang'ich kasallik belgilarini aniqlashdi.|The illness was found at a very early stage.|Kasallik juda erta bosqichda aniqlandi.|The country's incipient democracy faced many challenges.|Mamlakatning endigina shakllanayotgan demokratiyasi ko'p qiyinchiliklarga duch keldi.
interim|adjective|/ˈɪntərɪm/|oraliq, vaqtinchalik|Intended to last only until something permanent is arranged.|temporary · provisional · stopgap · transitional|doimiysi tashkil etilguncha davom etishi mo'ljallangan.|An interim government was formed until elections could be held.|Saylovlar o'tkazilgunga qadar vaqtinchalik hukumat tuzildi.|A temporary government ran the country before the vote.|Saylovgacha mamlakatni vaqtinchalik hukumat boshqardi.|The committee published an interim report.|Qo'mita oraliq hisobot e'lon qildi.
in the wake of|phrase|/ɪn ðə weɪk əv/|... dan keyin, ... natijasida|After something, and often as a result of it.|following · after · as a result of · in the aftermath of|biror narsadan keyin va ko'pincha uning natijasida.|In the wake of the scandal, the director resigned.|Janjaldan keyin direktor iste'foga chiqdi.|The director left his job following the scandal.|Direktor janjaldan keyin ishdan ketdi.|New safety laws were introduced in the wake of the disaster.|Falokatdan keyin yangi xavfsizlik qonunlari joriy etildi.
