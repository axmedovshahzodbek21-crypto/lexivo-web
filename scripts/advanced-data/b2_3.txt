# Til, madaniyat va muloqot|Til va muloqot
body language|noun|/ˈbɒdi ˌlæŋɡwɪdʒ/|tana tili|The way people show feelings through movements and posture rather than words.|non-verbal communication · gestures and posture|odamlarning so'z emas, harakat va holat orqali his-tuyg'ularini ifodalashi.|Interviewers pay close attention to a candidate's body language.|Suhbat o'tkazuvchilar nomzodning tana tiliga katta e'tibor berishadi.|Employers notice how applicants sit, move and look.|Ish beruvchilar nomzodlarning qanday o'tirishi, harakatlanishi va qarashini payqashadi.|Crossed arms are often seen as defensive body language.|Qo'llarni chalishtirish ko'pincha himoyaviy tana tili sifatida qabul qilinadi.
non-verbal cues|phrase|/ˌnɒn ˈvɜːbl kjuːz/|og'zaki bo'lmagan ishoralar|Signals such as facial expressions and tone of voice that communicate meaning.|body signals · unspoken signs · facial expressions|ma'no ifodalovchi yuz ifodasi va ovoz ohangi kabi signallar.|Babies respond to non-verbal cues long before they understand words.|Chaqaloqlar so'zlarni tushunishdan ancha oldin og'zaki bo'lmagan ishoralarga javob beradi.|Infants react to faces and tone of voice before they know language.|Go'daklar tilni bilishdan oldin yuz va ovoz ohangiga munosabat bildiradi.|Online meetings make it harder to read non-verbal cues.|Onlayn uchrashuvlarda og'zaki bo'lmagan ishoralarni tushunish qiyinroq.
convey complex ideas|phrase|/kənˈveɪ ˈkɒmpleks aɪˈdɪəz/|murakkab fikrlarni yetkazmoq|To communicate difficult thoughts clearly.|express difficult concepts · communicate complicated thoughts|qiyin fikrlarni aniq yetkazib bermoq.|Good teachers can convey complex ideas in simple language.|Yaxshi o'qituvchilar murakkab fikrlarni oddiy til bilan yetkaza oladi.|Skilled teachers explain difficult concepts clearly.|Mohir o'qituvchilar qiyin tushunchalarni aniq tushuntiradi.|Diagrams help scientists convey complex ideas to the public.|Diagrammalar olimlarga murakkab fikrlarni jamoatchilikka yetkazishga yordam beradi.
lost in translation|phrase|/lɒst ɪn trænzˈleɪʃn/|tarjimada yo'qolgan|Not fully understood when expressed in another language.|not translated accurately · missing in another language|boshqa tilga o'girilganda to'liq tushunilmagan.|Jokes are often lost in translation.|Hazillar ko'pincha tarjimada yo'qolib ketadi.|Humour rarely works as well in another language.|Hazil boshqa tilda kamdan-kam shunday yaxshi ishlaydi.|Some of the poem's beauty is inevitably lost in translation.|She'rning go'zalligining bir qismi muqarrar ravishda tarjimada yo'qoladi.
a native speaker|phrase|/ə ˈneɪtɪv ˈspiːkə/|ona tili sohibi|A person who learned a language from birth.|a first-language user · someone born speaking the language|tilni tug'ilgandan o'rgangan kishi.|The recordings were checked by a native speaker of Japanese.|Yozuvlar yapon tili sohibi tomonidan tekshirildi.|Someone who grew up speaking Japanese reviewed the audio.|Yapon tilida gapirib ulg'aygan kishi audioni ko'rib chiqdi.|Even a native speaker can make grammar mistakes.|Hatto ona tili sohibi ham grammatik xato qilishi mumkin.
a second language|phrase|/ə ˈsekənd ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ/|ikkinchi til|A language learned in addition to your first language.|a foreign language · an additional language|ona tilidan tashqari o'rganilgan til.|English is spoken as a second language by millions of people.|Ingliz tilida millionlab odamlar ikkinchi til sifatida gaplashadi.|Many people learn English after their mother tongue.|Ko'pchilik ingliz tilini ona tilidan keyin o'rganadi.|Learning a second language can delay memory loss in old age.|Ikkinchi tilni o'rganish keksalikda xotira zaiflashuvini kechiktirishi mumkin.
colloquial expressions|phrase|/kəˈləʊkwiəl ɪkˈspreʃnz/|so'zlashuv iboralari|Informal words and phrases used in everyday conversation.|informal language · everyday phrases · slang|kundalik suhbatda ishlatiladigan norasmiy so'z va iboralar.|Textbooks rarely teach the colloquial expressions people actually use.|Darsliklar odamlar aslida ishlatadigan so'zlashuv iboralarini kamdan-kam o'rgatadi.|Course books seldom include everyday informal phrases.|O'quv kitoblarida kundalik norasmiy iboralar kam uchraydi.|Avoid colloquial expressions in academic essays.|Akademik inshoda so'zlashuv iboralaridan qoching.
carry negative connotations|phrase|/ˈkæri ˈneɡətɪv ˌkɒnəˈteɪʃnz/|salbiy ma'no tusiga ega bo'lmoq|To suggest bad feelings or ideas in addition to the basic meaning.|have a negative sense · sound critical · imply something bad|asosiy ma'nodan tashqari yomon his yoki fikrni anglatmoq.|The word "cheap" can carry negative connotations of poor quality.|"Cheap" so'zi past sifat degan salbiy ma'no tusiga ega bo'lishi mumkin.|Calling something cheap may suggest it is badly made.|Biror narsani arzon deyish uning sifatsizligini anglatishi mumkin.|In some cultures, certain colours carry negative connotations.|Ba'zi madaniyatlarda ayrim ranglar salbiy ma'noga ega.
command of a language|phrase|/kəˈmɑːnd əv ə ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ/|tilni yaxshi bilish|The ability to use a language well.|fluency · mastery of a language · proficiency|tildan yaxshi foydalana olish qobiliyati.|The job requires an excellent command of English.|Bu ish ingliz tilini a'lo darajada bilishni talab qiladi.|Applicants must speak and write English extremely well.|Nomzodlar ingliz tilida juda yaxshi gapirishi va yozishi kerak.|Her command of the language impressed the examiners.|Uning tilni yaxshi bilishi imtihon oluvchilarni hayratda qoldirdi.
coin a term|phrase|/kɔɪn ə tɜːm/|yangi atama o'ylab topmoq|To invent a new word or expression.|create a new word · invent an expression · introduce a phrase|yangi so'z yoki ibora ixtiro qilmoq.|The scientist coined the term "global warming" in the 1970s.|Olim "global isish" atamasini 1970-yillarda o'ylab topgan.|The phrase was first used by a scientist in the 1970s.|Bu ibora ilk bor 1970-yillarda bir olim tomonidan ishlatilgan.|Writers often coin a term to describe a new social trend.|Yozuvchilar ko'pincha yangi ijtimoiy tendensiyani tasvirlash uchun yangi atama o'ylab topadi.
evolve over centuries|phrase|/ɪˈvɒlv ˈəʊvə ˈsentʃəriz/|asrlar davomida rivojlanmoq|To change and develop slowly over hundreds of years.|develop gradually · change over time · transform slowly|yuzlab yillar davomida sekin o'zgarib rivojlanmoq.|The English language has evolved over centuries.|Ingliz tili asrlar davomida rivojlanib kelgan.|English has slowly changed across hundreds of years.|Ingliz tili yuzlab yillar davomida asta-sekin o'zgargan.|These farming methods evolved over centuries of trial and error.|Bu dehqonchilik usullari asrlar davomidagi sinov va xatolar orqali rivojlangan.
endangered languages|phrase|/ɪnˈdeɪndʒəd ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒɪz/|yo'qolib borayotgan tillar|Languages that may soon have no speakers left.|dying languages · languages at risk · disappearing tongues|tez orada so'zlashuvchisi qolmasligi mumkin bo'lgan tillar.|Linguists are recording endangered languages before they disappear.|Tilshunoslar yo'qolib borayotgan tillarni yo'qolmasidan oldin yozib olishmoqda.|Experts are saving languages that few people still speak.|Mutaxassislar kam odam gaplashadigan tillarni saqlab qolmoqda.|About half of the world's languages are endangered languages.|Dunyodagi tillarning qariyb yarmi yo'qolib borayotgan tillardir.
a barrier to communication|phrase|/ə ˈbæriə tə kəˌmjuːnɪˈkeɪʃn/|muloqotga to'siq|Something that makes it difficult for people to understand each other.|an obstacle to understanding · a communication problem|odamlarning bir-birini tushunishini qiyinlashtiradigan narsa.|Language differences can be a barrier to communication in hospitals.|Til farqlari shifoxonalarda muloqotga to'siq bo'lishi mumkin.|Doctors and patients may not understand each other if they speak different languages.|Shifokor va bemor turli tillarda gaplashsa, bir-birini tushunmasligi mumkin.|Technical jargon is often a barrier to communication.|Texnik atamalar ko'pincha muloqotga to'siq bo'ladi.
linguist|noun|/ˈlɪŋɡwɪst/|tilshunos|A person who studies languages scientifically.|language expert · language scientist · philologist|tillarni ilmiy o'rganuvchi shaxs.|Linguists believe all human languages share certain features.|Tilshunoslar barcha inson tillari ba'zi umumiy xususiyatlarga ega deb hisoblashadi.|Language experts think every language has some things in common.|Til mutaxassislari har bir tilda umumiy jihatlar bor deb o'ylashadi.|The linguist spent ten years studying dialects of the region.|Tilshunos mintaqa shevalarini o'n yil davomida o'rgandi.
fluency|noun|/ˈfluːənsi/|ravonlik, erkin gapirish|The ability to speak or write a language easily and well.|ease · proficiency · command · smoothness|tilda oson va yaxshi gapirish yoki yozish qobiliyati.|Daily conversation practice is the fastest way to improve fluency.|Kundalik suhbat mashqi ravonlikni oshirishning eng tez yo'li.|Speaking every day helps learners talk more easily.|Har kuni gapirish o'rganuvchilarga osonroq so'zlashishga yordam beradi.|Fluency in two languages is an advantage in many careers.|Ikki tilda erkin gapirish ko'plab kasblarda afzallik hisoblanadi.
literacy|noun|/ˈlɪtərəsi/|savodxonlik|The ability to read and write.|reading and writing skills · education · basic skills|o'qish va yozish qobiliyati.|Literacy rates have risen sharply over the last century.|So'nggi asrda savodxonlik darajasi keskin oshdi.|Far more people can now read and write than a hundred years ago.|Hozir yuz yil oldingiga qaraganda ancha ko'p odam o'qish va yozishni biladi.|The charity runs literacy classes for adults.|Xayriya tashkiloti kattalar uchun savodxonlik darslarini o'tkazadi.
gesture|noun|/ˈdʒestʃə/|imo-ishora|A movement of the hands or head to express meaning.|sign · signal · movement · motion|ma'no ifodalash uchun qo'l yoki boshning harakati.|A thumbs-up gesture is rude in some countries.|Bosh barmoqni ko'tarish ishorasi ba'zi mamlakatlarda qo'pol hisoblanadi.|In certain places, raising your thumb is considered offensive.|Ba'zi joylarda bosh barmoqni ko'tarish haqoratli hisoblanadi.|She made a gesture to show that she wanted to speak.|U gapirmoqchi ekanini bildirish uchun ishora qildi.
accent|noun|/ˈæksent/|talaffuz uslubi, aksent|A way of pronouncing a language that shows where someone is from.|pronunciation · way of speaking · intonation|odamning qayerdan ekanligini ko'rsatadigan talaffuz tarzi.|Her accent showed that she was from Scotland.|Uning aksenti Shotlandiyadan ekanini ko'rsatdi.|You could tell from her pronunciation that she was Scottish.|Talaffuzidan uning shotlandiyalik ekanini bilish mumkin edi.|Having an accent does not make your English less correct.|Aksent borligi ingliz tilingizni kamroq to'g'ri qilmaydi.
a figure of speech|phrase|/ə ˈfɪɡə əv spiːtʃ/|ko'chma ma'nodagi ifoda|An expression that uses words in a non-literal way.|an idiom · a metaphor · an expression · not meant literally|so'zlarni to'g'ridan-to'g'ri bo'lmagan ma'noda ishlatuvchi ifoda.|"It's raining cats and dogs" is just a figure of speech.|"Mushuk va it yog'yapti" shunchaki ko'chma ma'nodagi ifoda.|The phrase is not meant to be taken literally.|Bu iborani so'zma-so'z tushunmaslik kerak.|Children often misunderstand a figure of speech.|Bolalar ko'pincha ko'chma ma'nodagi ifodalarni noto'g'ri tushunishadi.
# Salomatlik va tibbiyot|Salomatlik
chronic illnesses|phrase|/ˈkrɒnɪk ˈɪlnəsɪz/|surunkali kasalliklar|Diseases that last for a long time or keep coming back.|long-term conditions · persistent diseases · lasting illnesses|uzoq davom etadigan yoki qayta-qayta qaytadigan kasalliklar.|Chronic illnesses such as diabetes are becoming more common.|Diabet kabi surunkali kasalliklar tobora keng tarqalmoqda.|Long-term conditions like diabetes are on the rise.|Diabet kabi uzoq davom etadigan kasalliklar ko'paymoqda.|Many older people live with one or more chronic illnesses.|Ko'plab keksalar bir yoki bir nechta surunkali kasallik bilan yashaydi.
boost the immune system|phrase|/buːst ði ɪˈmjuːn ˈsɪstəm/|immunitetni mustahkamlamoq|To make the body better at fighting infections.|strengthen immunity · improve resistance to disease|organizmni infeksiyalarga qarshi kurashishda kuchliroq qilmoq.|Regular sleep helps to boost the immune system.|Muntazam uyqu immunitetni mustahkamlashga yordam beradi.|Sleeping well makes the body better at fighting illness.|Yaxshi uxlash organizmni kasallikka qarshi kurashishda kuchliroq qiladi.|Some people believe vitamin C can boost the immune system.|Ba'zilar C vitamini immunitetni mustahkamlashi mumkin deb hisoblaydi.
adverse side effects|phrase|/ˈædvɜːs saɪd ɪˈfekts/|nojo'ya ta'sirlar|Unwanted and harmful effects of a medicine or treatment.|harmful reactions · negative effects · unwanted consequences|dori yoki davolashning istalmagan va zararli ta'sirlari.|The drug was withdrawn after reports of adverse side effects.|Nojo'ya ta'sirlar haqida xabarlar kelgach dori sotuvdan olib tashlandi.|The medicine was removed because it was harming some patients.|Dori ba'zi bemorlarga zarar yetkazgani uchun olib tashlandi.|Patients should report any adverse side effects to their doctor.|Bemorlar har qanday nojo'ya ta'sir haqida shifokorga xabar berishi kerak.
physical activity|phrase|/ˈfɪzɪkl ækˈtɪvəti/|jismoniy faollik|Movement of the body that uses energy, such as walking or sport.|exercise · physical exercise · being active|piyoda yurish yoki sport kabi energiya sarflaydigan tana harakati.|Adults should do at least 150 minutes of physical activity a week.|Kattalar haftasiga kamida 150 daqiqa jismoniy faollik bilan shug'ullanishi kerak.|Grown-ups are advised to exercise for two and a half hours weekly.|Kattalarga haftasiga ikki yarim soat mashq qilish tavsiya etiladi.|Lack of physical activity is linked to heart disease.|Jismoniy faollik yetishmasligi yurak kasalliklari bilan bog'liq.
infectious diseases|phrase|/ɪnˈfekʃəs dɪˈziːzɪz/|yuqumli kasalliklar|Illnesses that can be passed from one person to another.|contagious illnesses · communicable diseases · infections|bir odamdan boshqasiga yuqishi mumkin bo'lgan kasalliklar.|Clean water has reduced the spread of infectious diseases.|Toza suv yuqumli kasalliklar tarqalishini kamaytirdi.|Safe drinking water means fewer contagious illnesses.|Xavfsiz ichimlik suvi yuqumli kasalliklarni kamaytiradi.|Vaccines protect children against many infectious diseases.|Vaksinalar bolalarni ko'plab yuqumli kasalliklardan himoya qiladi.
alleviate symptoms|phrase|/əˈliːvieɪt ˈsɪmptəmz/|alomatlarni yengillashtirmoq|To make the signs of an illness less severe.|relieve symptoms · ease pain · reduce discomfort|kasallik belgilarini kamroq og'ir qilmoq.|This medicine can alleviate symptoms but does not cure the disease.|Bu dori alomatlarni yengillashtirishi mumkin, lekin kasallikni davolamaydi.|The drug makes patients feel better without curing them.|Dori bemorlarni davolamasdan ularning ahvolini yaxshilaydi.|Drinking warm liquids can alleviate symptoms of a cold.|Iliq ichimliklar shamollash alomatlarini yengillashtirishi mumkin.
diagnose at an early stage|phrase|/ˈdaɪəɡnəʊz æt ən ˈɜːli steɪdʒ/|erta bosqichda tashxis qo'ymoq|To identify a disease soon after it starts.|detect early · spot in its early phase · identify promptly|kasallikni boshlangandan keyin tez orada aniqlamoq.|Most cancers can be treated if they are diagnosed at an early stage.|Saraton turlarining aksariyati erta bosqichda tashxis qo'yilsa davolanishi mumkin.|Many cancers are curable when detected early.|Ko'plab saraton turlari erta aniqlansa davolanadi.|New tests help doctors diagnose heart problems at an early stage.|Yangi tahlillar shifokorlarga yurak muammolarini erta bosqichda aniqlashga yordam beradi.
preventative healthcare|phrase|/prɪˈventətɪv ˈhelθkeə/|profilaktik tibbiyot|Medical care that aims to stop illness before it starts.|preventive medicine · disease prevention · health screening|kasallikni boshlanmasidan oldin to'xtatishga qaratilgan tibbiy yordam.|Investing in preventative healthcare saves money in the long run.|Profilaktik tibbiyotga sarmoya kiritish uzoq muddatda pulni tejaydi.|Stopping illness before it starts is cheaper than treating it later.|Kasallikni boshlanmasidan to'xtatish keyin davolashdan arzonroq.|Regular check-ups are a key part of preventative healthcare.|Muntazam tibbiy ko'riklar profilaktik tibbiyotning asosiy qismidir.
nutritional deficiencies|phrase|/njuˈtrɪʃənl dɪˈfɪʃnsiz/|ozuqa moddalari yetishmovchiligi|A lack of the vitamins or minerals the body needs.|a lack of vitamins · poor nutrition · dietary shortfalls|organizmga kerakli vitamin yoki minerallarning yetishmasligi.|Nutritional deficiencies can slow children's growth.|Ozuqa moddalari yetishmovchiligi bolalarning o'sishini sekinlashtirishi mumkin.|Children who lack vitamins may not grow properly.|Vitamin yetishmaydigan bolalar to'g'ri o'smasligi mumkin.|Doctors tested the patients for nutritional deficiencies.|Shifokorlar bemorlarni ozuqa moddalari yetishmovchiligiga tekshirishdi.
vaccine|noun|/ˈvæksiːn/|vaksina, emlash dori|A substance given to protect people from a disease.|immunisation · inoculation · jab|odamlarni kasallikdan himoya qilish uchun beriladigan modda.|The vaccine was developed in less than a year.|Vaksina bir yildan kamroq vaqt ichida ishlab chiqildi.|Scientists created the protection against the disease very quickly.|Olimlar kasallikka qarshi himoyani juda tez yaratishdi.|A new vaccine could save millions of lives.|Yangi vaksina millionlab hayotni saqlab qolishi mumkin.
# Ta'lim va mahorat|Ta'lim

academic performance|phrase|/ˌækəˈdemɪk pəˈfɔːməns/|o'zlashtirish, akademik natijalar|How well a student does in studies and exams.|school results · grades · educational achievement|o'quvchining o'qish va imtihonlardagi natijalari.|Breakfast has been shown to improve academic performance.|Nonushta o'zlashtirishni yaxshilashi isbotlangan.|Children who eat in the morning tend to get better grades.|Ertalab ovqatlanadigan bolalar odatda yaxshiroq baho olishadi.|Stress at home can affect a child's academic performance.|Uydagi stress bolaning o'zlashtirishiga ta'sir qilishi mumkin.
a steep learning curve|phrase|/ə stiːp ˈlɜːnɪŋ kɜːv/|tez va qiyin o'rganish jarayoni|A situation in which you must learn a lot very quickly.|a lot to learn quickly · a challenging start · rapid learning|juda ko'p narsani juda tez o'rganishga to'g'ri keladigan vaziyat.|Starting a new job abroad involved a steep learning curve.|Chet elda yangi ishni boshlash tez va qiyin o'rganishni talab qildi.|She had to learn a great deal very fast in her new role abroad.|U chet eldagi yangi lavozimida juda ko'p narsani tez o'rganishi kerak edi.|Coding has a steep learning curve for beginners.|Dasturlash yangi boshlovchilar uchun qiyin o'rganish jarayoniga ega.
master the fundamentals|phrase|/ˈmɑːstə ðə ˌfʌndəˈmentlz/|asoslarni mukammal o'zlashtirmoq|To learn the basic principles of something thoroughly.|learn the basics well · grasp the essentials|biror narsaning asosiy tamoyillarini puxta o'rganmoq.|Musicians must master the fundamentals before they can improvise.|Musiqachilar improvizatsiya qilishdan oldin asoslarni mukammal o'zlashtirishi kerak.|Players need to learn the basics thoroughly first.|Ijrochilar avval asoslarni puxta o'rganishlari kerak.|Once you master the fundamentals, advanced grammar becomes easier.|Asoslarni o'zlashtirganingizdan keyin murakkab grammatika osonlashadi.
