# Atrof-muhit va ekotizimlar|Atrof-muhit
the sixth mass extinction|noun|/ðə sɪksθ mæs ɪkˈstɪŋkʃn/|oltinchi ommaviy qirilish|The current period in which species are dying out at a very fast rate because of humans.|the current wave of extinctions · biodiversity collapse|inson faoliyati tufayli turlar juda tez yo'qolib borayotgan hozirgi davr.|Many biologists believe we are living through the sixth mass extinction.|Ko'plab biologlar biz oltinchi ommaviy qirilish davrida yashayapmiz deb hisoblaydi.|Scientists think species are disappearing faster than at almost any time in history.|Olimlar turlar tarixdagi deyarli har qanday davrdan tezroq yo'qolmoqda deb o'ylashadi.|Habitat loss is the main driver of the sixth mass extinction.|Yashash muhitining yo'qolishi oltinchi ommaviy qirilishning asosiy sababidir.
ecological overshoot|noun|/ˌiːkəˈlɒdʒɪkl ˈəʊvəʃuːt/|ekologik me'yordan oshish|Using natural resources faster than the earth can replace them.|living beyond the planet's means · overconsumption of nature|tabiiy resurslardan yer ularni tiklay olganidan tezroq foydalanish.|Humanity has been in ecological overshoot since the 1970s.|Insoniyat 1970-yillardan beri ekologik me'yordan oshib kelmoqda.|People have used more than nature can renew for decades.|Odamlar o'n yilliklardan beri tabiat tiklay olganidan ko'proq foydalanib kelmoqda.|Ecological overshoot will eventually lead to shortages.|Ekologik me'yordan oshish oxir-oqibat tanqisliklarga olib keladi.
the Anthropocene epoch|noun|/ði ˈænθrəpəsiːn ˈiːpɒk/|antropotsen davri|A proposed name for the present period, when human activity shapes the planet.|the age of humans · the human-dominated era|inson faoliyati sayyorani shakllantiradigan hozirgi davr uchun taklif qilingan nom.|Some geologists argue that we have entered the Anthropocene epoch.|Ba'zi geologlar biz antropotsen davriga kirdik deb ta'kidlaydi.|They say humans are now the main force changing the earth.|Ular endi yerni o'zgartiruvchi asosiy kuch odamlar ekanini aytishadi.|Plastic layers in rock may mark the Anthropocene epoch.|Tog' jinslaridagi plastik qatlamlar antropotsen davrini belgilashi mumkin.
a trophic cascade|phrase|/ə ˈtrɒfɪk kæˈskeɪd/|trofik kaskad, oziq zanjiridagi zanjirli ta'sir|A chain of effects through a food web when a top predator is added or removed.|a food-chain ripple effect · knock-on effects in an ecosystem|eng yuqori yirtqich qo'shilganda yoki yo'qolganda oziq zanjiri bo'ylab yuzaga keladigan ta'sirlar zanjiri.|Returning wolves to the park triggered a trophic cascade.|Bo'rilarni bog'ga qaytarish trofik kaskadni boshlab yubordi.|The wolves changed deer behaviour, which allowed trees and rivers to recover.|Bo'rilar bug'ular xulqini o'zgartirdi va bu daraxtlar hamda daryolarning tiklanishiga imkon berdi.|Overfishing sharks can cause a trophic cascade.|Akulalarni haddan tashqari ovlash trofik kaskadga sabab bo'lishi mumkin.
the albedo effect|noun|/ði ælˈbiːdəʊ ɪˈfekt/|albedo effekti|The way light-coloured surfaces such as ice reflect sunlight and keep the planet cooler.|reflectivity of surfaces · sunlight reflection|muz kabi och rangli yuzalarning quyosh nurini qaytarib, sayyorani sovuqroq saqlashi.|Melting ice weakens the albedo effect and speeds up warming.|Muzning erishi albedo effektini kuchsizlantirib, isishni tezlashtiradi.|When white ice melts, darker sea absorbs more heat.|Oq muz eriganda, qoraroq dengiz ko'proq issiqlik yutadi.|White roofs use the albedo effect to cool buildings.|Oq tomlar binolarni sovutish uchun albedo effektidan foydalanadi.
permafrost thaw|noun|/ˈpɜːməfrɒst θɔː/|abadiy muzloqning erishi|The melting of ground that has been frozen for a very long time.|melting frozen ground · thawing tundra|juda uzoq vaqtdan beri muzlagan yerning erishi.|Permafrost thaw releases large amounts of methane.|Abadiy muzloqning erishi katta miqdorda metan chiqaradi.|As frozen soil melts, it lets out greenhouse gases.|Muzlagan tuproq eriganda issiqxona gazlarini chiqaradi.|Roads and buildings are cracking because of permafrost thaw.|Abadiy muzloqning erishi tufayli yo'llar va binolar yorilmoqda.
ocean acidification|noun|/ˈəʊʃn əˌsɪdɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/|okean kislotaliligining ortishi|The process by which seawater becomes more acidic as it absorbs carbon dioxide.|seas becoming more acidic · falling ocean pH|dengiz suvi karbonat angidridni yutib, kislotaliroq bo'lish jarayoni.|Ocean acidification makes it harder for corals to build reefs.|Okean kislotaliligining ortishi marjonlarga rif qurishni qiyinlashtiradi.|More acidic water damages the skeletons of corals.|Kislotaliroq suv marjonlarning skeletiga zarar yetkazadi.|Shellfish farms are suffering from ocean acidification.|Mollyuska fermalari okean kislotaliligining ortishidan aziyat chekmoqda.
rewilding initiatives|phrase|/riːˈwaɪldɪŋ ɪˈnɪʃətɪvz/|tabiatni yovvoyi holiga qaytarish tashabbuslari|Projects that return land to its natural state and reintroduce wild animals.|restoring wild nature · nature restoration projects|yerni tabiiy holatiga qaytarib, yovvoyi hayvonlarni qayta olib kiradigan loyihalar.|Rewilding initiatives have brought beavers back to Britain.|Tabiatni yovvoyi holiga qaytarish tashabbuslari Britaniyaga qunduzlarni qaytardi.|Projects to restore nature have reintroduced beavers.|Tabiatni tiklash loyihalari qunduzlarni qayta olib keldi.|Farmers are divided over rewilding initiatives.|Fermerlar tabiatni yovvoyi holiga qaytarish tashabbuslari bo'yicha turlicha fikrda.
an ecological niche|phrase|/ən ˌiːkəˈlɒdʒɪkl niːʃ/|ekologik o'rin|The particular role and position of a species in its environment.|a species' role · a habitat role · a place in the ecosystem|turning o'z muhitidagi alohida roli va o'rni.|Each species of finch occupies a different ecological niche.|Har bir qizilishton turi alohida ekologik o'rinni egallaydi.|The birds survive together because each eats different food.|Qushlar birga yashay oladi, chunki har biri turli ozuqa yeydi.|Invasive species can take over an ecological niche.|Bosqinchi turlar ekologik o'rinni egallab olishi mumkin.
endemic flora and fauna|phrase|/enˈdemɪk ˈflɔːrə ənd ˈfɔːnə/|endemik o'simlik va hayvonot dunyosi|Plants and animals found naturally in only one place.|native species found nowhere else · unique local wildlife|tabiiy ravishda faqat bir joyda uchraydigan o'simlik va hayvonlar.|Madagascar is famous for its endemic flora and fauna.|Madagaskar endemik o'simlik va hayvonot dunyosi bilan mashhur.|Many of Madagascar's plants and animals exist nowhere else.|Madagaskarning ko'plab o'simlik va hayvonlari boshqa hech qayerda yo'q.|Islands often have rich endemic flora and fauna.|Orollarda ko'pincha boy endemik o'simlik va hayvonot dunyosi bo'ladi.
a carbon sink|phrase|/ə ˈkɑːbən sɪŋk/|uglerod yutgichi|A natural area such as a forest or ocean that absorbs more carbon than it releases.|a carbon store · a natural absorber of CO2|chiqaradiganidan ko'proq uglerod yutadigan o'rmon yoki okean kabi tabiiy hudud.|The Amazon rainforest acts as a huge carbon sink.|Amazonka tropik o'rmoni ulkan uglerod yutgichi vazifasini bajaradi.|The forest absorbs vast amounts of carbon dioxide.|O'rmon juda katta miqdorda karbonat angidrid yutadi.|Peat bogs are an important carbon sink.|Torf botqoqliklari muhim uglerod yutgichidir.
carrying capacity|noun|/ˈkæriɪŋ kəˈpæsəti/|sig'im, ko'tara olish qobiliyati|The largest population an environment can support.|maximum sustainable population · environmental limit|muhit qo'llab-quvvatlay oladigan eng katta populyatsiya.|The island has exceeded its carrying capacity for goats.|Orol echkilar uchun o'z sig'imidan oshib ketdi.|There are now more goats than the island can feed.|Orolda endi u boqa olganidan ko'proq echki bor.|Some scientists debate the earth's carrying capacity for humans.|Ba'zi olimlar yerning odamlar uchun sig'imi haqida bahslashadi.
habitat fragmentation|noun|/ˈhæbɪtæt ˌfræɡmenˈteɪʃn/|yashash muhitining bo'linib ketishi|The breaking up of natural areas into small, separate pieces.|division of habitats · splitting of natural areas|tabiiy hududlarning kichik, alohida qismlarga bo'linishi.|Roads cause habitat fragmentation for many animals.|Yo'llar ko'plab hayvonlar uchun yashash muhitining bo'linib ketishiga sabab bo'ladi.|New roads cut wild areas into small isolated pieces.|Yangi yo'llar yovvoyi hududlarni kichik ajralgan bo'laklarga bo'ladi.|Wildlife corridors help to reduce habitat fragmentation.|Yovvoyi tabiat yo'laklari yashash muhitining bo'linishini kamaytiradi.
ecocide|noun|/ˈiːkəʊsaɪd/|ekotsid|The large-scale destruction of the natural environment.|environmental destruction · devastation of nature|tabiiy muhitning keng ko'lamli yo'q qilinishi.|Campaigners want ecocide to be recognised as an international crime.|Faollar ekotsid xalqaro jinoyat sifatida tan olinishini xohlashadi.|Activists believe destroying nature on a huge scale should be punished in court.|Faollar tabiatni katta miqyosda yo'q qilish sudda jazolanishi kerak deb hisoblaydi.|The oil spill was described as ecocide.|Neft to'kilishi ekotsid deb ta'riflandi.
