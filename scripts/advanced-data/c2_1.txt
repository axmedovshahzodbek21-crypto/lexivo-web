# Sabab, oqibat va kelib chiqish|Sabab va oqibat
be a harbinger of|phrase|/bi ə ˈhɑːbɪndʒər əv/|... ning darakchisi bo'lmoq|To be a sign that something, often bad, is going to happen.|signal · foreshadow · be an early sign of · herald|biror narsa, ko'pincha yomon narsa, yuz berishining belgisi bo'lmoq.|The sudden fall in bee numbers may be a harbinger of wider ecological collapse.|Asalarilar sonining to'satdan kamayishi kengroq ekologik inqirozning darakchisi bo'lishi mumkin.|Losing bees could be an early warning that whole ecosystems are failing.|Asalarilarning yo'qolishi butun ekotizimlar buzilayotganining ilk ogohlantirishi bo'lishi mumkin.|Rising debt is often a harbinger of economic trouble.|Qarzning o'sishi ko'pincha iqtisodiy muammolarning darakchisi bo'ladi.
set in train|phrase|/set ɪn treɪn/|boshlab yubormoq, harakatga keltirmoq|To start a process or series of events.|set in motion · initiate · trigger · get under way|jarayon yoki voqealar zanjirini boshlab bermoq.|The minister's resignation set in train a series of political crises.|Vazirning iste'fosi bir qator siyosiy inqirozlarni boshlab yubordi.|One resignation led to crisis after crisis in government.|Bitta iste'fo hukumatda ketma-ket inqirozlarga olib keldi.|The report set in train a full review of safety rules.|Hisobot xavfsizlik qoidalarini to'liq qayta ko'rib chiqishni boshlab berdi.
be contingent upon|phrase|/bi kənˈtɪndʒənt əˈpɒn/|... ga bog'liq bo'lmoq|To depend on something else happening.|depend on · be conditional on · hinge on · rest on|boshqa narsa yuz berishiga bog'liq bo'lmoq.|Further funding is contingent upon the success of the first trial.|Qo'shimcha mablag' birinchi sinov muvaffaqiyatiga bog'liq.|More money will only be given if the first test works.|Birinchi sinov ish bersagina ko'proq pul beriladi.|The offer is contingent upon a satisfactory medical report.|Taklif qoniqarli tibbiy xulosaga bog'liq.
be born of necessity|phrase|/bi bɔːn əv nəˈsesəti/|zaruratdan tug'ilmoq|To come into existence because it was badly needed.|arise from need · emerge out of necessity|qattiq ehtiyoj tufayli paydo bo'lmoq.|Many inventions were born of necessity during the war.|Urush davrida ko'plab ixtirolar zaruratdan tug'ilgan.|Wartime shortages forced people to create new devices.|Urush davridagi tanqisliklar odamlarni yangi qurilmalar yaratishga majbur qildi.|Their friendship was born of necessity rather than choice.|Ularning do'stligi tanlovdan emas, zaruratdan tug'ilgan.
reverberate through|phrasal verb|/rɪˈvɜːbəreɪt θruː/|... bo'ylab aks-sado bermoq|To have a strong effect that spreads widely over a period of time.|spread through · echo across · have repercussions throughout|keng tarqaladigan kuchli ta'sirga ega bo'lmoq.|The bank's collapse reverberated through the global economy.|Bankning qulashi jahon iqtisodiyoti bo'ylab aks-sado berdi.|The failure of one bank affected economies around the world.|Bitta bankning barbod bo'lishi butun dunyo iqtisodiyotiga ta'sir qildi.|The decision will reverberate through the industry for years.|Qaror yillar davomida soha bo'ylab aks-sado beradi.
compound the predicament|phrase|/kəmˈpaʊnd ðə prɪˈdɪkəmənt/|og'ir ahvolni yanada chigallashtirmoq|To make a difficult situation even worse.|worsen the situation · exacerbate the problem · add to the difficulty|qiyin vaziyatni yanada yomonlashtirmoq.|A second drought compounded the farmers' predicament.|Ikkinchi qurg'oqchilik fermerlarning og'ir ahvolini yanada chigallashtirdi.|Another dry year made things even harder for the farmers.|Yana bir qurg'oqchil yil fermerlarga vaziyatni yanada qiyinlashtirdi.|Rising interest rates compounded the predicament of indebted families.|Foiz stavkalarining oshishi qarzdor oilalarning ahvolini yanada og'irlashtirdi.
a self-fulfilling prophecy|phrase|/ə ˌself fʊlˈfɪlɪŋ ˈprɒfəsi/|o'z-o'zini ro'yobga chiqaradigan bashorat|A prediction that comes true because people believe it.|a prediction that causes itself · an expectation that creates its outcome|odamlar ishongani uchun amalga oshadigan bashorat.|Fear of a bank collapse can become a self-fulfilling prophecy.|Bank qulashidan qo'rquv o'z-o'zini ro'yobga chiqaradigan bashoratga aylanishi mumkin.|If everyone expects a bank to fail and withdraws money, it really does fail.|Hamma bank qulaydi deb pulini olsa, u haqiqatan ham quladi.|Low expectations of pupils can be a self-fulfilling prophecy.|O'quvchilardan kam kutish o'z-o'zini ro'yobga chiqaradigan bashorat bo'lishi mumkin.
be intertwined with|phrase|/bi ˌɪntəˈtwaɪnd wɪð/|... bilan chambarchas bog'langan bo'lmoq|To be closely connected so that one affects the other.|be closely linked with · be bound up with · be interwoven with|biri ikkinchisiga ta'sir qiladigan darajada yaqin bog'liq bo'lmoq.|The city's history is intertwined with the river.|Shahar tarixi daryo bilan chambarchas bog'langan.|You cannot separate the city's past from the river's story.|Shahar o'tmishini daryo tarixidan ajratib bo'lmaydi.|Economic and environmental problems are deeply intertwined.|Iqtisodiy va ekologik muammolar bir-biri bilan chuqur bog'langan.
blunt the impact of|phrase|/blʌnt ði ˈɪmpækt əv/|... ta'sirini yumshatmoq|To make the effect of something less strong.|soften the effect of · cushion · lessen · dampen|biror narsaning ta'sirini kamroq kuchli qilmoq.|Government subsidies helped blunt the impact of rising fuel prices.|Davlat subsidiyalari yoqilg'i narxlari oshishi ta'sirini yumshatishga yordam berdi.|State payments made the higher fuel costs easier to bear.|Davlat to'lovlari yoqilg'ining qimmatlashuvini ko'tarishni osonlashtirdi.|Sea walls can blunt the impact of storms.|Dengiz to'siqlari bo'ronlar ta'sirini yumshatishi mumkin.
unleash a torrent of|phrase|/ʌnˈliːʃ ə ˈtɒrənt əv/|... sel kabi oqimini qo'zg'atmoq|To suddenly cause a large amount of something, such as criticism.|provoke a flood of · trigger a wave of · release a stream of|to'satdan juda ko'p narsaga, masalan tanqidga, sabab bo'lmoq.|The announcement unleashed a torrent of criticism online.|E'lon internetda sel kabi tanqidlar oqimini qo'zg'atdi.|After the news, huge numbers of people complained online.|Xabardan keyin juda ko'p odam internetda shikoyat qildi.|The film unleashed a torrent of debate about privacy.|Film shaxsiy hayot haqida kuchli bahslar to'lqinini qo'zg'atdi.
act as an incubator for|phrase|/ækt æz ən ˈɪŋkjubeɪtər fɔː/|... ning beshigi bo'lmoq, rivojlanishiga sharoit yaratmoq|To provide the conditions in which something new can develop.|nurture · foster · be a breeding ground for · cultivate|yangi narsa rivojlanishi mumkin bo'lgan sharoitni ta'minlamoq.|Universities often act as an incubator for new businesses.|Universitetlar ko'pincha yangi bizneslar uchun beshik vazifasini bajaradi.|Many start-ups first develop inside universities.|Ko'plab startaplar dastlab universitetlar ichida rivojlanadi.|Coffee houses acted as an incubator for political ideas.|Qahvaxonalar siyosiy g'oyalarning beshigi bo'lgan.
a knock-on effect|phrase|/ə ˈnɒk ɒn ɪˌfekt/|zanjirli ta'sir|An indirect result of something that causes further effects.|a ripple effect · a chain reaction · a secondary consequence|keyingi oqibatlarga sabab bo'ladigan bilvosita natija.|The strike had a knock-on effect on other industries.|Ish tashlash boshqa tarmoqlarga zanjirli ta'sir ko'rsatdi.|Other businesses also suffered because of the strike.|Ish tashlash tufayli boshqa bizneslar ham zarar ko'rdi.|Higher fuel costs have a knock-on effect on food prices.|Yoqilg'i narxining oshishi oziq-ovqat narxlariga zanjirli ta'sir qiladi.
precipitate a crisis|phrase|/prɪˈsɪpɪteɪt ə ˈkraɪsɪs/|inqirozni keltirib chiqarmoq|To make a crisis happen suddenly or sooner than expected.|trigger a crisis · bring about a crisis · spark an emergency|inqirozning to'satdan yoki kutilganidan oldin yuz berishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|The sudden rise in oil prices precipitated a crisis.|Neft narxining to'satdan oshishi inqirozni keltirib chiqardi.|The crisis began when oil suddenly became much more expensive.|Inqiroz neft to'satdan ancha qimmatlashganda boshlandi.|A single mistake could precipitate a crisis.|Birgina xato inqirozni keltirib chiqarishi mumkin.
ramifications|noun|/ˌræmɪfɪˈkeɪʃnz/|oqibatlar|The complex and often unexpected results of an action.|consequences · implications · repercussions · effects|harakatning murakkab va ko'pincha kutilmagan natijalari.|Few people understood the ramifications of the new law.|Kam odam yangi qonunning oqibatlarini tushundi.|Most people did not realise how much the law would change.|Ko'pchilik qonun qanchalik ko'p narsani o'zgartirishini anglamadi.|The decision has serious ramifications for the economy.|Qaror iqtisodiyot uchun jiddiy oqibatlarga ega.
repercussions|noun|/ˌriːpəˈkʌʃnz/|salbiy oqibatlar|Unintended bad effects of an event or action.|consequences · after-effects · fallout · backlash|voqea yoki harakatning ko'zda tutilmagan yomon ta'sirlari.|The scandal had repercussions for the whole government.|Janjal butun hukumat uchun salbiy oqibatlarga olib keldi.|The whole government suffered because of the scandal.|Janjal tufayli butun hukumat zarar ko'rdi.|Workers feared repercussions if they complained.|Ishchilar shikoyat qilsa, salbiy oqibatlar bo'lishidan qo'rqishdi.
engender|verb|/ɪnˈdʒendə/|keltirib chiqarmoq, tug'dirmoq|To cause a feeling or situation to exist.|cause · produce · create · give rise to|his yoki vaziyatning yuzaga kelishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|The policy engendered distrust among local people.|Siyosat mahalliy aholi orasida ishonchsizlik tug'dirdi.|Local people stopped trusting the authorities because of the policy.|Siyosat tufayli mahalliy aholi hokimiyatga ishonmay qo'ydi.|Good teachers engender a love of learning.|Yaxshi o'qituvchilar ilmga muhabbat uyg'otadi.
induce|verb|/ɪnˈdjuːs/|qo'zg'atmoq, sabab bo'lmoq|To cause something, especially a physical effect.|cause · bring on · produce · trigger|biror narsaga, ayniqsa jismoniy ta'sirga, sabab bo'lmoq.|Some medicines can induce drowsiness.|Ba'zi dorilar uyquchanlikni qo'zg'atishi mumkin.|Certain drugs may make you feel sleepy.|Ayrim dorilar sizni uyquchan qilishi mumkin.|Nothing could induce him to change his mind.|Hech narsa uni fikrini o'zgartirishga undamas edi.
progenitor|noun|/prəʊˈdʒenɪtə/|asoschi, ajdod|A person or thing from which something later develops.|forerunner · ancestor · originator · precursor|keyinchalik biror narsa rivojlangan shaxs yoki narsa.|The wolf is the progenitor of the domestic dog.|Bo'ri uy itining ajdodidir.|Pet dogs are descended from wolves.|Uy itlari bo'rilardan kelib chiqqan.|He is seen as the progenitor of modern jazz.|U zamonaviy jazning asoschisi sifatida qaraladi.
impetus|noun|/ˈɪmpɪtəs/|turtki, harakatlantiruvchi kuch|Something that encourages a process to happen faster.|stimulus · driving force · momentum · incentive|jarayonning tezroq yuz berishiga undaydigan narsa.|The disaster gave new impetus to safety reform.|Falokat xavfsizlik islohotiga yangi turtki berdi.|After the disaster, efforts to improve safety sped up.|Falokatdan keyin xavfsizlikni yaxshilash harakatlari tezlashdi.|Cheaper solar panels provided the impetus for change.|Arzonroq quyosh panellari o'zgarishga turtki bo'ldi.
a confluence of factors|phrase|/ə ˈkɒnfluəns əv ˈfæktəz/|omillar uyg'unligi|Several causes coming together to produce a result.|a combination of causes · several factors together|bir natijani keltirib chiqargan bir necha sababning birlashuvi.|The empire fell because of a confluence of factors.|Imperiya omillar uyg'unligi tufayli quladi.|Many different causes together led to the empire's collapse.|Ko'plab turli sabablar birgalikda imperiyaning qulashiga olib keldi.|A confluence of factors made the harvest unusually poor.|Omillar uyg'unligi hosilni g'ayrioddiy darajada kam qildi.
be symptomatic of|phrase|/bi ˌsɪmptəˈmætɪk əv/|... ning belgisi bo'lmoq|To be a sign of a wider problem.|be a sign of · indicate · reflect · point to|kengroq muammoning belgisi bo'lmoq.|Long hospital queues are symptomatic of underfunding.|Shifoxonalardagi uzun navbatlar mablag' yetishmasligining belgisidir.|Long waits show that hospitals do not have enough money.|Uzoq kutish shifoxonalarda pul yetishmasligini ko'rsatadi.|His anger was symptomatic of deeper stress.|Uning jahli chuqurroq stressning belgisi edi.
be a by-product of|phrase|/bi ə ˈbaɪ prɒdʌkt əv/|... ning qo'shimcha mahsuli bo'lmoq|To be an extra result that happens as a side effect of something else.|be a side effect of · result incidentally from · be a spin-off of|boshqa narsaning yon ta'siri sifatida yuz beradigan qo'shimcha natija bo'lmoq.|Heat is a by-product of electricity generation.|Issiqlik elektr energiyasi ishlab chiqarishning qo'shimcha mahsulidir.|Producing electricity also creates unwanted heat.|Elektr ishlab chiqarish keraksiz issiqlikni ham hosil qiladi.|Better health was a by-product of the cycling scheme.|Sog'liqning yaxshilanishi velosiped dasturining qo'shimcha natijasi bo'ldi.
# Dalil, isbot va tadqiqot|Dalil va tadqiqot
winnow fact from conjecture|phrase|/ˈwɪnəʊ fækt frəm kənˈdʒektʃə/|faktni taxmindan ajratmoq|To separate what is proven from what is only guessed.|separate truth from speculation · distinguish fact from guesswork|isbotlangan narsani faqat taxmin qilingandan ajratmoq.|Historians must winnow fact from conjecture in ancient sources.|Tarixchilar qadimiy manbalarda faktni taxmindan ajratishlari kerak.|Researchers have to decide what in old texts is true and what is guesswork.|Tadqiqotchilar eski matnlardagi nima haqiqat va nima taxmin ekanini hal qilishi kerak.|Good journalists winnow fact from conjecture.|Yaxshi jurnalistlar faktni taxmindan ajratadi.
be a litmus test for|phrase|/bi ə ˈlɪtməs test fɔː/|... uchun hal qiluvchi sinov bo'lmoq|To be a clear test that shows the true nature of something.|be a decisive test of · serve as an indicator of · be a touchstone for|biror narsaning haqiqiy mohiyatini ko'rsatadigan aniq sinov bo'lmoq.|The election will be a litmus test for the new government.|Saylov yangi hukumat uchun hal qiluvchi sinov bo'ladi.|The vote will show clearly how popular the government is.|Ovoz berish hukumat qanchalik mashhur ekanini aniq ko'rsatadi.|Climate policy is a litmus test for political leadership.|Iqlim siyosati siyosiy yetakchilik uchun hal qiluvchi sinovdir.
incontrovertible proof|phrase|/ˌɪnkɒntrəˈvɜːtəbl pruːf/|rad etib bo'lmaydigan dalil|Evidence so strong that nobody can doubt it.|undeniable evidence · conclusive proof · irrefutable evidence|hech kim shubha qila olmaydigan darajada kuchli dalil.|DNA tests provided incontrovertible proof of his innocence.|DNK tahlillari uning aybsizligiga rad etib bo'lmaydigan dalil bo'ldi.|Genetic evidence showed beyond any doubt that he was innocent.|Genetik dalillar uning aybsiz ekanini hech qanday shubhasiz ko'rsatdi.|There is still no incontrovertible proof that the drug works.|Dori ishlashiga hali rad etib bo'lmaydigan dalil yo'q.
cherry-pick data|phrase|/ˈtʃeri pɪk ˈdeɪtə/|ma'lumotlarni tanlab olmoq|To choose only the information that supports your view and ignore the rest.|select data selectively · present one-sided evidence|faqat o'z fikringizni quvvatlaydigan ma'lumotni tanlab, qolganini e'tiborsiz qoldirmoq.|Critics accused the company of cherry-picking data.|Tanqidchilar kompaniyani ma'lumotlarni tanlab olishda aybladi.|The firm was said to show only the figures that suited it.|Firma faqat o'ziga ma'qul raqamlarni ko'rsatgan deyildi.|Honest researchers do not cherry-pick data.|Halol tadqiqotchilar ma'lumotlarni tanlab olmaydi.
stand up to scrutiny|phrase|/stænd ʌp tə ˈskruːtəni/|sinchkov tekshiruvga bardosh bermoq|To remain convincing when examined carefully.|survive close examination · hold up · withstand analysis|puxta tekshirilganda ham ishonarliligicha qolmoq.|The theory does not stand up to scrutiny.|Nazariya sinchkov tekshiruvga bardosh bermaydi.|When examined closely, the idea falls apart.|Yaqindan tekshirilganda g'oya puchga chiqadi.|Her account of events stood up to scrutiny in court.|Uning voqealar haqidagi bayoni sudda sinchkov tekshiruvga bardosh berdi.
an artefact of measurement|phrase|/ən ˈɑːtɪfækt əv ˈmeʒəmənt/|o'lchash usulidan kelib chiqqan xato|A result that appears only because of how something was measured, not because it is real.|a measurement error · a methodological illusion · a false signal|haqiqiy emas, faqat o'lchash usuli tufayli paydo bo'lgan natija.|The apparent rise in crime may be an artefact of measurement.|Jinoyatchilikning ko'rinadigan o'sishi o'lchash usulidan kelib chiqqan xato bo'lishi mumkin.|Crime may only seem higher because it is now recorded differently.|Jinoyatchilik endi boshqacha qayd etilgani uchun ko'proq bo'lib ko'rinishi mumkin.|Scientists checked that the result was not an artefact of measurement.|Olimlar natija o'lchash xatosi emasligini tekshirishdi.
tease apart causal threads|phrase|/tiːz əˈpɑːt ˈkɔːzl θredz/|sabab-oqibat bog'lanishlarini ajratib olmoq|To carefully separate the different causes that are mixed together.|separate the causes · untangle the factors · disentangle causes|aralashib ketgan turli sabablarni ehtiyotkorlik bilan ajratmoq.|Researchers tried to tease apart causal threads linking diet and disease.|Tadqiqotchilar ovqatlanish va kasallikni bog'lovchi sabab-oqibat bog'lanishlarini ajratishga harakat qilishdi.|Scientists worked out which aspects of diet actually cause illness.|Olimlar ovqatlanishning qaysi jihatlari aslida kasallikka sabab bo'lishini aniqlashdi.|It is hard to tease apart causal threads in complex systems.|Murakkab tizimlarda sabab-oqibat bog'lanishlarini ajratish qiyin.
cast a long shadow over|phrase|/kɑːst ə lɒŋ ˈʃædəʊ ˈəʊvə/|... ga uzoq vaqt salbiy ta'sir ko'rsatmoq|To have a negative effect on something for a long time.|have a lasting negative effect on · overshadow · haunt|uzoq vaqt davomida biror narsaga salbiy ta'sir qilmoq.|The war cast a long shadow over the country's development.|Urush mamlakat taraqqiyotiga uzoq vaqt salbiy ta'sir ko'rsatdi.|The country's progress was affected by the war for many years.|Mamlakat taraqqiyotiga urush ko'p yillar davomida ta'sir qildi.|The scandal continues to cast a long shadow over the company.|Janjal kompaniyaga uzoq vaqtdan beri salbiy ta'sir ko'rsatmoqda.
glean insights from|phrase|/ɡliːn ˈɪnsaɪts frɒm/|... dan tushunchalar to'plamoq|To gradually gather useful knowledge from something.|gather understanding from · learn from · extract knowledge from|biror narsadan asta-sekin foydali bilim yig'moq.|Researchers gleaned insights from thousands of patient records.|Tadqiqotchilar minglab bemor yozuvlaridan tushunchalar to'plashdi.|Scientists learned a great deal by studying many medical files.|Olimlar ko'plab tibbiy hujjatlarni o'rganib, ko'p narsa bilib olishdi.|We can glean insights from how other countries solved the problem.|Boshqa mamlakatlar muammoni qanday hal qilganidan tushunchalar olishimiz mumkin.
a spurious correlation|phrase|/ə ˈspjʊəriəs ˌkɒrəˈleɪʃn/|soxta bog'liqlik|A connection between two things that seems real but is caused by chance or another factor.|a false link · a coincidental connection · a misleading correlation|haqiqiy bo'lib ko'rinadigan, lekin tasodif yoki boshqa omil tufayli yuzaga kelgan bog'liqlik.|The link between ice cream sales and drownings is a spurious correlation.|Muzqaymoq savdosi va cho'kishlar o'rtasidagi bog'liqlik soxta bog'liqlikdir.|Both rise in hot weather, but one does not cause the other.|Ikkalasi ham issiqda oshadi, lekin biri ikkinchisiga sabab bo'lmaydi.|Large data sets often produce spurious correlations.|Katta ma'lumotlar to'plamlari ko'pincha soxta bog'liqliklarni yuzaga keltiradi.
a corpus of scholarship|phrase|/ə ˈkɔːpəs əv ˈskɒləʃɪp/|ilmiy asarlar majmuasi|A large collection of academic writing on a subject.|a body of research · academic literature · a collection of studies|biror mavzu bo'yicha akademik asarlarning katta to'plami.|There is a large corpus of scholarship on the Silk Road.|Ipak yo'li haqida katta ilmiy asarlar majmuasi bor.|Many academics have written about the Silk Road.|Ko'plab olimlar Ipak yo'li haqida yozgan.|Her work added significantly to the corpus of scholarship.|Uning ishi ilmiy asarlar majmuasiga sezilarli hissa qo'shdi.
beyond a shadow of a doubt|phrase|/bɪˈjɒnd ə ˈʃædəʊ əv ə daʊt/|hech qanday shubhasiz|With complete certainty.|without any doubt · with absolute certainty · conclusively|to'liq ishonch bilan.|The tests proved beyond a shadow of a doubt that the painting was a fake.|Tahlillar rasm soxta ekanini hech qanday shubhasiz isbotladi.|It is now completely certain that the picture is not genuine.|Rasm haqiqiy emasligi endi mutlaqo aniq.|We cannot say beyond a shadow of a doubt what caused the fire.|Yong'inga nima sabab bo'lganini hech qanday shubhasiz ayta olmaymiz.
the falsifiability criterion|noun|/ðə ˌfɔːlsɪfaɪəˈbɪləti kraɪˈtɪəriən/|soxtalashtirish mezoni|The principle that a scientific theory must be testable and possible to prove wrong.|testability · the principle that theories must be disprovable|ilmiy nazariya sinovdan o'tkazilishi va noto'g'riligi isbotlanishi mumkin bo'lishi kerak degan tamoyil.|Astrology fails the falsifiability criterion.|Munajjimlik soxtalashtirish mezoniga javob bermaydi.|Astrology cannot be tested in a way that could prove it wrong.|Munajjimlikni noto'g'riligini isbotlay oladigan tarzda sinab bo'lmaydi.|Popper introduced the falsifiability criterion to define science.|Popper fanni ta'riflash uchun soxtalashtirish mezonini kiritgan.
extrapolate|verb|/ɪkˈstræpəleɪt/|ekstrapolyatsiya qilmoq, mavjud ma'lumotdan taxmin qilmoq|To use known facts to estimate something unknown, such as future trends.|project · predict from data · infer · estimate|ma'lum faktlardan noma'lum narsani, masalan kelajak tendensiyalarini, taxmin qilmoq.|It is risky to extrapolate from such a small sample.|Bunday kichik tanlanmadan xulosa chiqarish xavfli.|You cannot reliably predict much from so few cases.|Juda kam holatdan ishonchli bashorat qilib bo'lmaydi.|Scientists extrapolated future sea levels from current data.|Olimlar hozirgi ma'lumotlardan kelajakdagi dengiz sathini taxmin qilishdi.
