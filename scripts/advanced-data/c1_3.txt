# Iqtisodiyot va savdo|Iqtisodiyot
a lucrative market|phrase|/ə ˈluːkrətɪv ˈmɑːkɪt/|daromadli bozor|A market in which a lot of money can be made.|a profitable market · a money-making sector · a rewarding business area|ko'p pul topish mumkin bo'lgan bozor.|Health foods have become a lucrative market.|Sog'lom oziq-ovqatlar daromadli bozorga aylandi.|Companies earn a great deal of money from health foods.|Kompaniyalar sog'lom oziq-ovqatlardan ko'p pul topadi.|China is a lucrative market for luxury brands.|Xitoy hashamatli brendlar uchun daromadli bozor.
weather an economic downturn|phrase|/ˈweðər ən ˌiːkəˈnɒmɪk ˈdaʊntɜːn/|iqtisodiy tanazzuldan omon chiqmoq|To survive a period when the economy is doing badly.|survive a recession · get through hard times · ride out a slump|iqtisodiyot yomon ahvolda bo'lgan davrdan omon o'tmoq.|Firms with savings were better able to weather the economic downturn.|Jamg'armasi bor firmalar iqtisodiy tanazzuldan yaxshiroq omon chiqdi.|Companies with money set aside survived the recession more easily.|Pul jamg'argan kompaniyalar turg'unlikdan osonroq omon qoldi.|Diversified economies tend to weather an economic downturn better.|Diversifikatsiyalangan iqtisodiyotlar odatda tanazzuldan yaxshiroq omon chiqadi.
impose tariffs on|phrase|/ɪmˈpəʊz ˈtærɪfs ɒn/|... ga boj solmoq|To put a tax on goods coming into a country.|tax imports · introduce import duties · levy charges on|mamlakatga kirib keladigan tovarlarga soliq solmoq.|The government imposed tariffs on imported steel.|Hukumat import qilinadigan po'latga boj soldi.|Steel from abroad now costs more because of a new tax.|Yangi soliq tufayli chet el po'lati endi qimmatroq.|Imposing tariffs on food can raise prices for consumers.|Oziq-ovqatga boj solish iste'molchilar uchun narxlarni oshirishi mumkin.
a scarcity of raw materials|phrase|/ə ˈskeəsəti əv rɔː məˈtɪəriəlz/|xomashyo tanqisligi|A situation in which basic materials are hard to find.|a shortage of resources · a lack of materials · limited supply|asosiy materiallarni topish qiyin bo'lgan vaziyat.|A scarcity of raw materials slowed production.|Xomashyo tanqisligi ishlab chiqarishni sekinlashtirdi.|Factories produced less because basic materials were hard to get.|Asosiy materiallarni olish qiyin bo'lgani uchun zavodlar kamroq ishlab chiqardi.|Japan's scarcity of raw materials made it dependent on trade.|Yaponiyadagi xomashyo tanqisligi uni savdoga qaram qilib qo'ydi.
diversify the economy|phrase|/daɪˈvɜːsɪfaɪ ði ɪˈkɒnəmi/|iqtisodiyotni diversifikatsiya qilmoq|To develop a wider range of industries instead of depending on one.|broaden the economic base · reduce dependence on one sector|bitta sohaga bog'liq bo'lmasdan, ko'proq tarmoqlarni rivojlantirmoq.|Oil-rich nations are trying to diversify the economy.|Neftga boy davlatlar iqtisodiyotni diversifikatsiya qilishga harakat qilmoqda.|These countries want to rely less on oil.|Bu davlatlar neftga kamroq tayanmoqchi.|Tourism is one way to diversify the economy.|Turizm iqtisodiyotni diversifikatsiya qilish usullaridan biri.
fluctuating exchange rates|phrase|/ˈflʌktʃueɪtɪŋ ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ reɪts/|o'zgaruvchan valyuta kurslari|Values of currencies that keep changing against each other.|unstable currency values · volatile exchange rates|valyutalarning bir-biriga nisbatan doimiy o'zgarib turadigan qiymati.|Fluctuating exchange rates make planning difficult for exporters.|O'zgaruvchan valyuta kurslari eksportchilar uchun rejalashtirishni qiyinlashtiradi.|Exporters struggle to plan when currency values keep changing.|Valyuta qiymati doimo o'zgarsa, eksportchilar rejalashtirishda qiynaladi.|Tourists were affected by fluctuating exchange rates.|Sayyohlarga o'zgaruvchan valyuta kurslari ta'sir qildi.
subsidise local industries|phrase|/ˈsʌbsɪdaɪz ˈləʊkl ˈɪndəstriz/|mahalliy sanoatni subsidiyalamoq|To give money to local businesses to help them survive.|fund domestic businesses · support local producers financially|mahalliy bizneslarga omon qolishi uchun pul bermoq.|The state subsidises local industries to protect jobs.|Davlat ish o'rinlarini himoya qilish uchun mahalliy sanoatni subsidiyalaydi.|The government pays local firms so they can keep employing people.|Hukumat mahalliy firmalarga odamlarni ishda saqlab qolishlari uchun pul to'laydi.|Critics say subsidising local industries discourages innovation.|Tanqidchilar mahalliy sanoatni subsidiyalash innovatsiyaga to'sqinlik qiladi deyishadi.
saturate the market|phrase|/ˈsætʃəreɪt ðə ˈmɑːkɪt/|bozorni to'ldirib yubormoq|To supply so much of a product that there is more than people want.|flood the market · oversupply · overfill the market|odamlar xohlaganidan ko'proq mahsulot yetkazib bermoq.|Cheap imports have saturated the market.|Arzon import bozorni to'ldirib yubordi.|There are now more cheap foreign goods than people want to buy.|Endi odamlar sotib olmoqchi bo'lganidan ko'proq arzon chet el tovarlari bor.|Too many new apps saturate the market and few succeed.|Juda ko'p yangi ilovalar bozorni to'ldirib yuboradi va ozi muvaffaqiyatga erishadi.
allocate resources efficiently|phrase|/ˈæləkeɪt rɪˈzɔːsɪz ɪˈfɪʃntli/|resurslarni samarali taqsimlamoq|To share out money, people or materials in the most effective way.|use resources wisely · distribute funds effectively|pul, odamlar yoki materiallarni eng samarali tarzda taqsimlamoq.|Good managers allocate resources efficiently.|Yaxshi rahbarlar resurslarni samarali taqsimlaydi.|Effective leaders make the best use of the money and staff available.|Samarali rahbarlar mavjud pul va xodimlardan eng yaxshi foydalanadi.|Hospitals must allocate resources efficiently during a crisis.|Inqiroz paytida shifoxonalar resurslarni samarali taqsimlashi kerak.
curb inflation|phrase|/kɜːb ɪnˈfleɪʃn/|inflyatsiyani jilovlamoq|To control the rise in prices.|control price rises · bring inflation down · rein in prices|narxlarning oshishini nazorat qilmoq.|The central bank raised interest rates to curb inflation.|Markaziy bank inflyatsiyani jilovlash uchun foiz stavkalarini oshirdi.|Interest rates went up to stop prices rising so fast.|Narxlar tez oshmasligi uchun foiz stavkalari oshirildi.|Curbing inflation is the government's main economic aim.|Inflyatsiyani jilovlash hukumatning asosiy iqtisodiy maqsadi.
economies of scale|phrase|/ɪˈkɒnəmiz əv skeɪl/|miqyos samarasi|The cost advantages a business gets by producing more.|lower costs from larger output · savings from mass production|ko'proq ishlab chiqarish orqali biznes oladigan xarajat afzalliklari.|Large supermarkets benefit from economies of scale.|Yirik supermarketlar miqyos samarasidan foyda ko'radi.|Big stores pay less per item because they buy in huge quantities.|Katta do'konlar juda ko'p miqdorda sotib olgani uchun har bir mahsulotga kamroq to'laydi.|Small farms cannot achieve the same economies of scale.|Kichik fermalar xuddi shunday miqyos samarasiga erisha olmaydi.
comparative advantage|noun|/kəmˈpærətɪv ədˈvɑːntɪdʒ/|qiyosiy ustunlik|The ability of a country to produce something more cheaply than others.|competitive strength · relative efficiency · economic edge|mamlakatning biror narsani boshqalarga qaraganda arzonroq ishlab chiqara olishi.|Sunny countries have a comparative advantage in growing fruit.|Quyoshli mamlakatlar meva yetishtirishda qiyosiy ustunlikka ega.|Warm climates make it cheaper to produce fruit there.|Iliq iqlim u yerda meva yetishtirishni arzonroq qiladi.|Each country should focus on its comparative advantage.|Har bir mamlakat o'zining qiyosiy ustunligiga e'tibor qaratishi kerak.
yield high returns|phrase|/jiːld haɪ rɪˈtɜːnz/|yuqori daromad keltirmoq|To produce a lot of profit.|be very profitable · pay off well · generate large profits|ko'p foyda keltirmoq.|Investing in education can yield high returns.|Ta'limga sarmoya kiritish yuqori daromad keltirishi mumkin.|Money spent on schooling often pays back many times over.|Ta'limga sarflangan pul ko'pincha bir necha barobar qaytadi.|Risky investments may yield high returns but can also fail.|Xavfli sarmoyalar yuqori daromad keltirishi, lekin muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'lishi ham mumkin.
protectionist measures|phrase|/prəˈtekʃənɪst ˈmeʒəz/|protektsionistik choralar|Actions taken to protect a country's industries from foreign competition.|trade barriers · import restrictions · tariffs and quotas|mamlakat sanoatini xorijiy raqobatdan himoya qilish choralari.|Protectionist measures can lead to trade wars.|Protektsionistik choralar savdo urushlariga olib kelishi mumkin.|Barriers against foreign goods may cause other countries to retaliate.|Chet el tovarlariga qo'yilgan to'siqlar boshqa davlatlarning javob choralariga sabab bo'lishi mumkin.|The country introduced protectionist measures to save its car industry.|Mamlakat avtomobil sanoatini saqlab qolish uchun protektsionistik choralar joriy qildi.
the circular economy|noun|/ðə ˈsɜːkjələr ɪˈkɒnəmi/|aylanma iqtisodiyot|An economic system that reuses and recycles materials instead of throwing them away.|a zero-waste economy · reuse and recycling system|materiallarni tashlab yubormasdan qayta ishlatadigan iqtisodiy tizim.|The circular economy aims to eliminate waste.|Aylanma iqtisodiyot chiqindilarni yo'q qilishni maqsad qiladi.|This approach tries to keep materials in use for as long as possible.|Bu yondashuv materiallardan imkon qadar uzoq foydalanishga harakat qiladi.|Repairing old phones is part of the circular economy.|Eski telefonlarni ta'mirlash aylanma iqtisodiyotning bir qismi.
recession|noun|/rɪˈseʃn/|iqtisodiy turg'unlik, retsessiya|A period when a country's economy shrinks and unemployment rises.|downturn · slump · depression · economic decline|mamlakat iqtisodiyoti qisqarib, ishsizlik ortadigan davr.|Millions lost their jobs during the recession.|Retsessiya davrida millionlab odamlar ishini yo'qotdi.|Many people became unemployed when the economy shrank.|Iqtisodiyot qisqarganda ko'p odam ishsiz qoldi.|Economists fear the country is heading into recession.|Iqtisodchilar mamlakat turg'unlikka yuz tutayotganidan qo'rqishmoqda.
commodity|noun|/kəˈmɒdəti/|tovar, xomashyo mahsuloti|A raw material or product that can be bought and sold.|product · goods · raw material · resource|sotib olish va sotish mumkin bo'lgan xomashyo yoki mahsulot.|Coffee is one of the most traded commodities in the world.|Qahva dunyoda eng ko'p savdo qilinadigan tovarlardan biri.|Coffee is bought and sold internationally in huge amounts.|Qahva xalqaro miqyosda juda katta hajmda sotib olinadi va sotiladi.|In the desert, water is a precious commodity.|Cho'lda suv qimmatli tovardir.
expenditure|noun|/ɪkˈspendɪtʃə/|xarajat|The amount of money spent.|spending · costs · outlay · expenses|sarflangan pul miqdori.|Government expenditure on health has doubled.|Hukumatning sog'liqni saqlashga xarajati ikki baravar oshdi.|The state now spends twice as much on healthcare.|Davlat endi sog'liqni saqlashga ikki baravar ko'p pul sarflaydi.|Families are cutting their expenditure on luxuries.|Oilalar hashamatli narsalarga xarajatlarini qisqartirmoqda.
monopoly|noun|/məˈnɒpəli/|monopoliya|Complete control of a market by one company.|exclusive control · market dominance · sole supplier|bozorni bitta kompaniya to'liq nazorat qilishi.|The company had a monopoly on rail travel for decades.|Kompaniya o'n yilliklar davomida temir yo'l qatnovida monopoliyaga ega edi.|For many years, only one firm ran the trains.|Ko'p yillar davomida poyezdlarni faqat bitta firma boshqargan.|Monopolies can lead to higher prices and poor service.|Monopoliyalar yuqori narx va yomon xizmatga olib kelishi mumkin.
austerity|noun|/ɒˈsterəti/|tejamkorlik siyosati|Government policy of cutting public spending to reduce debt.|spending cuts · budget cuts · financial restraint|qarzni kamaytirish uchun davlat xarajatlarini qisqartirish siyosati.|Years of austerity led to cuts in public services.|Ko'p yillik tejamkorlik siyosati davlat xizmatlarining qisqarishiga olib keldi.|Reduced government spending meant fewer public services.|Davlat xarajatlarining kamayishi davlat xizmatlarining kamayishini anglatardi.|Protesters marched against austerity.|Namoyishchilar tejamkorlik siyosatiga qarshi yurish qilishdi.
# Psixologiya va xulq-atvor|Psixologiya
delayed gratification|noun|/dɪˈleɪd ˌɡrætɪfɪˈkeɪʃn/|lazzatni kechiktirish|Choosing to wait for a bigger reward instead of taking a smaller one now.|self-control · patience · resisting temptation|hozirgi kichik mukofot o'rniga kattaroq mukofotni kutishni tanlash.|Children who can practise delayed gratification often do well later.|Lazzatni kechiktira oladigan bolalar ko'pincha keyinchalik muvaffaqiyatga erishadi.|Kids who are able to wait for rewards tend to succeed in life.|Mukofotni kuta oladigan bolalar hayotda muvaffaqiyatga erishishga moyil.|Saving money requires delayed gratification.|Pul jamg'arish lazzatni kechiktirishni talab qiladi.
intrinsic motivation|noun|/ɪnˈtrɪnzɪk ˌməʊtɪˈveɪʃn/|ichki motivatsiya|The desire to do something because you enjoy it, not for a reward.|inner drive · self-motivation · genuine interest|mukofot uchun emas, yoqqani uchun biror ishni qilish istagi.|Intrinsic motivation is stronger than the promise of money.|Ichki motivatsiya pul va'dasidan kuchliroq.|People work harder when they enjoy the task itself.|Odamlar vazifaning o'zi yoqqanda qattiqroq ishlaydi.|Good teachers try to build intrinsic motivation in their students.|Yaxshi o'qituvchilar o'quvchilarida ichki motivatsiyani shakllantirishga harakat qiladi.
cognitive bias|noun|/ˈkɒɡnətɪv ˈbaɪəs/|kognitiv xato, fikrlashdagi og'ish|A common mistake in the way people think that affects their judgements.|thinking error · mental shortcut · distorted judgement|odamlarning hukmlariga ta'sir qiladigan fikrlashdagi keng tarqalgan xato.|Cognitive bias can lead investors to make poor decisions.|Kognitiv xato investorlarni noto'g'ri qarorlar qabul qilishga olib kelishi mumkin.|Errors in thinking cause many people to invest badly.|Fikrlashdagi xatolar ko'p odamni noto'g'ri sarmoya kiritishga undaydi.|We all have cognitive biases we are not aware of.|Barchamizda o'zimiz bilmaydigan kognitiv xatolar bor.
shape human perception|phrase|/ʃeɪp ˈhjuːmən pəˈsepʃn/|inson idrokini shakllantirmoq|To influence how people see and understand the world.|influence how we see · affect our understanding · mould our view|odamlarning dunyoni qanday ko'rishi va tushunishiga ta'sir qilmoq.|Language may shape human perception of colour.|Til insonning rangni idrok etishini shakllantirishi mumkin.|The words we have can affect how we see colours.|Bizdagi so'zlar ranglarni qanday ko'rishimizga ta'sir qilishi mumkin.|Media images shape human perception of beauty.|Ommaviy axborot tasvirlari insonning go'zallik haqidagi idrokini shakllantiradi.

a subconscious habit|phrase|/ə ˌsʌbˈkɒnʃəs ˈhæbɪt/|ongsiz odat|Something you do regularly without realising it.|an automatic behaviour · an unconscious routine|o'zingiz sezmagan holda muntazam qiladigan narsa.|Biting your nails is often a subconscious habit.|Tirnoq tishlash ko'pincha ongsiz odat bo'ladi.|Many people bite their nails without noticing.|Ko'p odam buni sezmagan holda tirnog'ini tishlaydi.|Checking the phone has become a subconscious habit for many.|Telefonni tekshirish ko'pchilik uchun ongsiz odatga aylandi.
susceptible to manipulation|phrase|/səˈseptəbl tə məˌnɪpjuˈleɪʃn/|manipulyatsiyaga moyil|Easily influenced or controlled by others in a dishonest way.|easily influenced · vulnerable to persuasion · easily misled|boshqalar tomonidan osongina noto'g'ri yo'l bilan boshqariladigan.|Lonely people may be more susceptible to manipulation.|Yolg'iz odamlar manipulyatsiyaga ko'proq moyil bo'lishi mumkin.|Isolated people can be influenced more easily by others.|Yolg'izlanib qolgan odamlarga boshqalar osonroq ta'sir o'tkaza oladi.|Children are especially susceptible to manipulation by advertisers.|Bolalar ayniqsa reklama beruvchilarning manipulyatsiyasiga moyil.
a predisposition to|phrase|/ə ˌpriːdɪspəˈzɪʃn tuː/|... ga moyillik|A natural tendency to develop something or behave in a certain way.|a tendency towards · a susceptibility to · an inclination to|biror narsaga tabiiy moyillik.|Some people have a genetic predisposition to heart disease.|Ba'zi odamlarda yurak kasalligiga genetik moyillik bor.|Certain people inherit a higher risk of heart problems.|Ayrim odamlarga yurak muammolari xavfi irsiy o'tadi.|She has a predisposition to worry.|Uning xavotirlanishga moyilligi bor.
