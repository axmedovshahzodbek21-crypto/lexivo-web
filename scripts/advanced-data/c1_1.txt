# Sabab, oqibat va kelib chiqish|Sabab va oqibat
stem from|phrasal verb|/stem frɒm/|... dan kelib chiqmoq|To be caused by or come from something.|result from · arise from · originate in · be caused by|biror narsa sababli yuzaga kelmoq yoki undan kelib chiqmoq.|Many health problems stem from a lack of sleep.|Ko'plab sog'liq muammolari uyqu yetishmasligidan kelib chiqadi.|Insufficient rest is the root cause of numerous illnesses.|Yetarlicha dam olmaslik ko'plab kasalliklarning asl sababidir.|The conflict stems from a dispute over water rights.|Nizo suv huquqlari bo'yicha kelishmovchilikdan kelib chiqqan.
pave the way for|phrase|/peɪv ðə weɪ fɔː/|... ga yo'l ochmoq|To create the conditions that make something possible.|make possible · prepare the ground for · open the door to|biror narsa amalga oshishi uchun sharoit yaratmoq.|Early experiments with gliders paved the way for powered flight.|Planyorlar bilan dastlabki tajribalar motorli parvozga yo'l ochdi.|Powered aircraft were made possible by earlier glider tests.|Motorli samolyotlar avvalgi planyor sinovlari tufayli yaratildi.|The agreement could pave the way for lasting peace.|Kelishuv barqaror tinchlikka yo'l ochishi mumkin.
be rooted in|phrase|/bi ˈruːtɪd ɪn/|... ga asoslangan bo'lmoq, ildizi ... da bo'lmoq|To have developed from something and be strongly influenced by it.|originate in · be based on · stem from · have its roots in|biror narsadan kelib chiqqan va unga chuqur bog'liq bo'lmoq.|The festival is rooted in ancient farming traditions.|Bayramning ildizi qadimiy dehqonchilik an'analariga borib taqaladi.|The celebration originally developed from old agricultural customs.|Bayram dastlab eski dehqonchilik odatlaridan kelib chiqqan.|Her fear of water was rooted in a childhood accident.|Uning suvdan qo'rqishi bolalikdagi baxtsiz hodisaga borib taqalardi.
attribute X to Y|phrase|/əˈtrɪbjuːt ... tuː/|X ni Y ga bog'lamoq, X ni Y bilan izohlamoq|To say that something is caused by, or was created by, a particular person or thing.|put X down to Y · credit X to Y · blame X on Y · ascribe|biror narsani ma'lum sabab yoki shaxs bilan bog'lab izohlamoq.|Scientists attribute the decline in frogs to a fungal disease.|Olimlar qurbaqalar kamayishini zamburug' kasalligi bilan bog'lashadi.|Researchers believe a fungus is responsible for falling frog numbers.|Tadqiqotchilar qurbaqalar sonining kamayishiga zamburug' sabab deb hisoblaydi.|The poem is often attributed to a fourteenth-century monk.|She'r ko'pincha XIV asrda yashagan rohibga nisbat beriladi.
act as a catalyst for|phrase|/ækt æz ə ˈkætəlɪst fɔː/|... ga turtki bo'lmoq|To cause an important change or event to happen more quickly.|trigger · spur · stimulate · be the impetus for|muhim o'zgarish yoki voqeaning tezroq yuz berishiga sabab bo'lmoq.|The crisis acted as a catalyst for reform of the banking system.|Inqiroz bank tizimini isloh qilishga turtki bo'ldi.|The emergency speeded up changes to how banks are run.|Favqulodda vaziyat banklar boshqaruvidagi o'zgarishlarni tezlashtirdi.|A single protest can act as a catalyst for a national movement.|Birgina norozilik umummilliy harakatga turtki bo'lishi mumkin.
spark widespread concern|phrase|/spɑːk ˈwaɪdspred kənˈsɜːn/|keng xavotir uyg'otmoq|To make many people worried.|cause alarm · raise fears · provoke public anxiety|ko'plab odamlarni xavotirga solmoq.|Reports of contaminated milk sparked widespread concern among parents.|Ifloslangan sut haqidagi xabarlar ota-onalar orasida keng xavotir uyg'otdi.|Many parents became worried after news about unsafe milk.|Xavfli sut haqidagi xabardan keyin ko'plab ota-onalar xavotirga tushdi.|The decision to close the hospital sparked widespread concern.|Shifoxonani yopish qarori keng xavotir uyg'otdi.
lay the foundations for|phrase|/leɪ ðə faʊnˈdeɪʃnz fɔː/|... ga asos solmoq|To create the basic conditions from which something can develop.|establish the basis for · set up · prepare the groundwork for|biror narsa rivojlanishi uchun asosiy shart-sharoitni yaratmoq.|Ancient Greek thinkers laid the foundations for modern science.|Qadimgi yunon mutafakkirlari zamonaviy fanga asos solishgan.|Modern science grew out of the work of Greek philosophers.|Zamonaviy fan yunon faylasuflarining ishlaridan o'sib chiqqan.|Good habits in childhood lay the foundations for a healthy life.|Bolalikdagi yaxshi odatlar sog'lom hayotga asos soladi.
hinge on|phrasal verb|/hɪndʒ ɒn/|... ga bog'liq bo'lmoq|To depend completely on something.|depend on · rest on · turn on · be determined by|butunlay biror narsaga bog'liq bo'lmoq.|The success of the project hinges on government funding.|Loyihaning muvaffaqiyati davlat moliyalashtiruviga bog'liq.|Whether the project succeeds depends entirely on state money.|Loyiha muvaffaqiyati to'liq davlat puliga bog'liq.|The case hinged on the evidence of a single witness.|Ish bitta guvohning ko'rsatmasiga bog'liq edi.
spill over into|phrasal verb|/spɪl ˈəʊvər ˈɪntuː/|... ga yoyilib ketmoq|To spread from one area or situation into another.|spread to · extend into · affect · overflow into|bir soha yoki vaziyatdan boshqasiga tarqalmoq.|Stress at work often spills over into family life.|Ishdagi stress ko'pincha oilaviy hayotga ham yoyiladi.|Pressure from the job frequently affects people at home too.|Ishdagi bosim ko'pincha uydagi hayotga ham ta'sir qiladi.|There are fears the violence could spill over into neighbouring countries.|Zo'ravonlik qo'shni davlatlarga yoyilib ketishi mumkinligidan qo'rqishmoqda.
offset the impact of|phrase|/ˈɒfset ði ˈɪmpækt əv/|... ta'sirini qoplamoq, yumshatmoq|To reduce the effect of something by doing something else that balances it.|counterbalance · compensate for · cancel out · neutralise|boshqa narsa bilan muvozanatlab, biror narsaning ta'sirini kamaytirmoq.|Planting trees can partly offset the impact of carbon emissions.|Daraxt ekish uglerod chiqindilari ta'sirini qisman qoplashi mumkin.|New forests may help to balance out some of the pollution.|Yangi o'rmonlar ifloslanishning bir qismini muvozanatlashga yordam berishi mumkin.|Higher wages failed to offset the impact of rising prices.|Ish haqining oshishi narxlar o'sishi ta'sirini qoplay olmadi.
account for|phrasal verb|/əˈkaʊnt fɔː/|... ni tashkil qilmoq; ... ni izohlamoq|To form a particular amount of something, or to explain why something happened.|make up · constitute · explain · represent|biror narsaning ma'lum qismini tashkil qilmoq yoki sababini tushuntirmoq.|Transport accounts for a quarter of the city's emissions.|Transport shahar chiqindilarining to'rtdan bir qismini tashkil qiladi.|One in four tonnes of the city's pollution comes from vehicles.|Shahar ifloslanishining har to'rt tonnasidan biri transportdan keladi.|Genetics alone cannot account for the rise in allergies.|Allergiyalar ko'payishini faqat genetika bilan izohlab bo'lmaydi.
be inextricably linked to|phrase|/bi ˌɪnɪkˈstrɪkəbli lɪŋkt tuː/|... bilan ajralmas bog'liq bo'lmoq|To be so closely connected that the two cannot be separated.|be inseparable from · be bound up with · be intertwined with|bir-biridan ajratib bo'lmaydigan darajada bog'liq bo'lmoq.|The town's history is inextricably linked to the silk trade.|Shahar tarixi ipak savdosi bilan ajralmas bog'liq.|You cannot understand the town's past without its silk trade.|Shahar o'tmishini ipak savdosisiz tushunib bo'lmaydi.|Poverty is inextricably linked to poor health.|Qashshoqlik yomon sog'liq bilan ajralmas bog'liq.
outcome|noun|/ˈaʊtkʌm/|natija, oqibat|The final result of a process or situation.|result · consequence · end result · upshot|jarayon yoki vaziyatning yakuniy natijasi.|Nobody can predict the outcome of the election.|Saylov natijasini hech kim oldindan aytib bera olmaydi.|It is impossible to know who will win the vote.|Ovoz berishda kim g'olib bo'lishini bilib bo'lmaydi.|Early treatment greatly improves the outcome for patients.|Erta davolash bemorlar uchun natijani ancha yaxshilaydi.
underlying|adjective|/ˌʌndəˈlaɪɪŋ/|asosiy, yashirin (sabab)|Real but not immediately obvious.|fundamental · root · basic · hidden|haqiqiy, lekin birdaniga ko'zga tashlanmaydigan.|The underlying cause of the floods was deforestation.|Suv toshqinlarining asl sababi o'rmonlarning kesilishi edi.|The floods really happened because the forests had been cut down.|Toshqinlar aslida o'rmonlar kesilgani uchun sodir bo'ldi.|Doctors treated the pain but not the underlying problem.|Shifokorlar og'riqni davoladi, lekin asosiy muammoni emas.
precursor|noun|/priˈkɜːsə/|o'tmishdosh, darakchi|Something that existed before another thing and led to its development.|forerunner · predecessor · early form · antecedent|boshqa narsadan oldin mavjud bo'lib, uning rivojiga olib kelgan narsa.|The abacus was a precursor of the modern calculator.|Cho't zamonaviy kalkulyatorning o'tmishdoshi edi.|Calculators developed from earlier counting tools like the abacus.|Kalkulyatorlar cho't kabi avvalgi hisoblash vositalaridan rivojlangan.|Small tremors are sometimes a precursor to a major earthquake.|Kichik silkinishlar ba'zan kuchli zilzilaning darakchisi bo'ladi.
exacerbate|verb|/ɪɡˈzæsəbeɪt/|kuchaytirmoq, yomonlashtirmoq|To make a bad situation worse.|worsen · aggravate · intensify · compound|yomon vaziyatni yanada yomonlashtirmoq.|The drought was exacerbated by poor water management.|Qurg'oqchilik suvni yomon boshqarish tufayli yanada kuchaydi.|Bad planning made the water shortage even more serious.|Yomon rejalashtirish suv tanqisligini yanada jiddiylashtirdi.|Stress can exacerbate skin conditions.|Stress teri kasalliklarini kuchaytirishi mumkin.
prompt|verb|/prɒmpt/|undamoq, sabab bo'lmoq|To cause someone to do something or cause something to happen.|cause · lead to · trigger · motivate|kimnidir biror ishni qilishga undamoq yoki biror narsaga sabab bo'lmoq.|The accident prompted calls for stricter speed limits.|Baxtsiz hodisa tezlik cheklovlarini qat'iylashtirish talablariga sabab bo'ldi.|After the crash, people demanded lower speed limits.|To'qnashuvdan keyin odamlar tezlikni cheklashni talab qilishdi.|Rising prices prompted many families to grow their own vegetables.|Narxlarning oshishi ko'plab oilalarni o'z sabzavotlarini yetishtirishga undadi.
be conducive to|phrase|/bi kənˈdjuːsɪv tuː/|... ga qulay sharoit yaratmoq|To provide conditions that help something happen.|favour · encourage · promote · help|biror narsa yuz berishi uchun qulay sharoit yaratmoq.|A quiet room is conducive to concentration.|Tinch xona diqqatni jamlash uchun qulay sharoit yaratadi.|It is easier to focus in a silent environment.|Jimjit muhitda diqqatni jamlash osonroq.|Warm, wet conditions are conducive to the spread of disease.|Iliq va nam sharoit kasallik tarqalishiga qulay.
a prerequisite for|phrase|/ə priːˈrekwəzɪt fɔː/|... uchun zaruriy shart|Something that must exist or happen before something else is possible.|a precondition for · a requirement for · essential for|boshqa narsa mumkin bo'lishidan oldin bo'lishi shart bo'lgan narsa.|A good level of English is a prerequisite for the course.|Ingliz tilini yaxshi bilish kurs uchun zaruriy shart.|Students must already speak English well before they enrol.|Talabalar yozilishdan oldin ingliz tilida yaxshi gapira olishlari shart.|Political stability is a prerequisite for economic growth.|Siyosiy barqarorlik iqtisodiy o'sish uchun zaruriy shartdir.
have far-reaching consequences|phrase|/hæv ˌfɑː ˈriːtʃɪŋ ˈkɒnsɪkwənsɪz/|keng ko'lamli oqibatlarga ega bo'lmoq|To have important effects on many people or things.|have wide-ranging effects · affect many areas · have a major impact|ko'plab odam yoki narsalarga muhim ta'sir ko'rsatmoq.|The invention of the internet had far-reaching consequences for society.|Internetning ixtiro qilinishi jamiyat uchun keng ko'lamli oqibatlarga ega bo'ldi.|The internet changed almost every part of social life.|Internet ijtimoiy hayotning deyarli har bir sohasini o'zgartirdi.|Even a small rise in sea level could have far-reaching consequences.|Dengiz sathining ozgina ko'tarilishi ham keng ko'lamli oqibatlarga olib kelishi mumkin.
# Dalil, isbot va tadqiqot|Dalil va tadqiqot
a growing body of evidence|phrase|/ə ˈɡrəʊɪŋ ˈbɒdi əv ˈevɪdəns/|tobora ko'payib borayotgan dalillar|An increasing amount of research that supports an idea.|more and more research · mounting evidence · an increasing number of studies|biror fikrni qo'llab-quvvatlovchi tadqiqotlar miqdorining ortishi.|A growing body of evidence suggests that music helps memory.|Tobora ko'payib borayotgan dalillar musiqa xotiraga yordam berishini ko'rsatmoqda.|More and more studies indicate that music can improve recall.|Tobora ko'proq tadqiqotlar musiqa eslab qolishni yaxshilashini ko'rsatmoqda.|There is a growing body of evidence linking diet to mood.|Ovqatlanishni kayfiyat bilan bog'lovchi dalillar tobora ko'paymoqda.
draw a correlation between|phrase|/drɔː ə ˌkɒrəˈleɪʃn bɪˈtwiːn/|... o'rtasida o'zaro bog'liqlik topmoq|To show that two things change together in a related way.|link · connect · establish a relationship between|ikki narsa o'zaro bog'liq tarzda o'zgarishini ko'rsatmoq.|Researchers drew a correlation between screen time and poor sleep.|Tadqiqotchilar ekran oldida o'tkazilgan vaqt va yomon uyqu o'rtasida bog'liqlik topishdi.|More time on devices was associated with worse sleep.|Qurilmalarda ko'proq vaqt o'tkazish yomonroq uyqu bilan bog'liq ekani aniqlandi.|It is difficult to draw a correlation between wealth and happiness.|Boylik va baxt o'rtasida bog'liqlik topish qiyin.
remain inconclusive|phrase|/rɪˈmeɪn ˌɪnkənˈkluːsɪv/|aniq xulosaga kelinmagan holda qolmoq|To still not provide a clear answer or proof.|be unresolved · be uncertain · prove nothing definite|hali ham aniq javob yoki isbot bermaslik.|The results of the study remain inconclusive.|Tadqiqot natijalari hali aniq xulosaga olib kelmagan.|The research has not yet given a clear answer.|Tadqiqot hali aniq javob bermagan.|Evidence about the drug's long-term effects remains inconclusive.|Dorining uzoq muddatli ta'siri haqidagi dalillar hali aniq emas.
gather empirical data|phrase|/ˈɡæðər ɪmˈpɪrɪkl ˈdeɪtə/|tajribaviy ma'lumotlar to'plamoq|To collect information based on observation or experiment rather than theory.|collect observational evidence · record measurements · do field research|nazariya emas, kuzatish yoki tajribaga asoslangan ma'lumot to'plamoq.|The team spent two years gathering empirical data in the rainforest.|Jamoa tropik o'rmonda ikki yil davomida tajribaviy ma'lumotlar to'pladi.|Researchers observed and measured conditions in the forest for two years.|Tadqiqotchilar ikki yil davomida o'rmondagi sharoitlarni kuzatib o'lchashdi.|Philosophers speculated, but scientists began to gather empirical data.|Faylasuflar taxmin qilishgan, olimlar esa tajribaviy ma'lumot to'play boshlashgan.
be subject to scrutiny|phrase|/bi ˈsʌbdʒekt tə ˈskruːtəni/|sinchkov tekshiruv ostida bo'lmoq|To be examined very carefully.|be closely examined · be investigated · be checked thoroughly|juda sinchkovlik bilan tekshirilmoq.|All new medicines are subject to scrutiny by independent experts.|Barcha yangi dorilar mustaqil mutaxassislar tomonidan sinchkov tekshiriladi.|Independent specialists carefully check every new drug.|Mustaqil mutaxassislar har bir yangi dorini puxta tekshiradi.|The minister's spending has been subject to scrutiny.|Vazirning xarajatlari sinchkov tekshiruv ostida bo'ldi.
underpin a theory|phrase|/ˌʌndəˈpɪn ə ˈθɪəri/|nazariyaga asos bo'lmoq|To provide the support or basis for a theory.|support · form the basis of · strengthen · back up|nazariya uchun tayanch yoki asos bo'lmoq.|Fossil records underpin the theory of evolution.|Qazilma qoldiqlar evolyutsiya nazariyasiga asos bo'ladi.|Evidence from fossils supports the idea of evolution.|Qazilmalardan olingan dalillar evolyutsiya g'oyasini qo'llab-quvvatlaydi.|Several key assumptions underpin the economic theory.|Bir qancha asosiy taxminlar iqtisodiy nazariyaga asos bo'ladi.
disprove a hypothesis|phrase|/dɪsˈpruːv ə haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/|farazni rad etmoq|To show that an idea is wrong.|refute · show to be false · contradict · discredit|biror g'oyaning noto'g'ri ekanini ko'rsatmoq.|The new data disproved the hypothesis that the disease was spread by water.|Yangi ma'lumotlar kasallik suv orqali tarqaladi degan farazni rad etdi.|It turned out the illness was not passed on through water.|Kasallik suv orqali yuqmasligi ma'lum bo'ldi.|A single experiment can disprove a hypothesis.|Birgina tajriba farazni rad etishi mumkin.
corroborate findings|phrase|/kəˈrɒbəreɪt ˈfaɪndɪŋz/|natijalarni tasdiqlamoq|To provide extra evidence that supports research results.|confirm results · support findings · verify · back up|tadqiqot natijalarini qo'shimcha dalil bilan tasdiqlamoq.|A second study in Japan corroborated the findings.|Yaponiyadagi ikkinchi tadqiqot natijalarni tasdiqladi.|Research in Japan produced the same results.|Yaponiyadagi tadqiqot xuddi shunday natija berdi.|Satellite images corroborate the findings of the ground survey.|Sun'iy yo'ldosh suratlari yer usti tadqiqoti natijalarini tasdiqlaydi.
a longitudinal study|phrase|/ə ˌlɒŋɡɪˈtjuːdɪnl ˈstʌdi/|uzoq muddatli kuzatuv tadqiqoti|Research that follows the same people over a long period.|a long-term study · research over many years|bir xil odamlarni uzoq vaqt kuzatib boradigan tadqiqot.|A longitudinal study followed 5,000 children from birth to adulthood.|Uzoq muddatli tadqiqot 5 000 bolani tug'ilganidan voyaga yetguncha kuzatdi.|Researchers tracked the same children for many years.|Tadqiqotchilar bir xil bolalarni ko'p yillar davomida kuzatishdi.|Only a longitudinal study can show how habits change with age.|Faqat uzoq muddatli tadqiqot odatlar yosh bilan qanday o'zgarishini ko'rsata oladi.
peer-reviewed research|phrase|/ˌpɪə rɪˈvjuːd rɪˈsɜːtʃ/|ekspertlar tomonidan ko'rib chiqilgan tadqiqot|Research checked by other experts before it is published.|expert-checked studies · scholarly research · academically reviewed work|nashrdan oldin boshqa mutaxassislar tekshirgan tadqiqot.|The claim is not supported by any peer-reviewed research.|Bu da'vo ekspertlar ko'rib chiqqan hech qanday tadqiqot bilan tasdiqlanmagan.|No study checked by other scientists backs up this claim.|Boshqa olimlar tekshirgan biror tadqiqot bu da'voni tasdiqlamaydi.|Students should rely on peer-reviewed research rather than blogs.|Talabalar bloglarga emas, ekspertlar ko'rib chiqqan tadqiqotlarga tayanishi kerak.
replicate a study|phrase|/ˈreplɪkeɪt ə ˈstʌdi/|tadqiqotni takrorlamoq|To repeat research to see if the same results are obtained.|repeat an experiment · reproduce results · redo research|bir xil natija olinishini tekshirish uchun tadqiqotni qayta o'tkazmoq.|Other scientists were unable to replicate the study.|Boshqa olimlar tadqiqotni takrorlay olishmadi.|When others repeated the experiment, they got different results.|Boshqalar tajribani takrorlaganda boshqacha natija olishdi.|It is important to replicate a study before trusting its results.|Natijalariga ishonishdan oldin tadqiqotni takrorlash muhim.
lend credence to|phrase|/lend ˈkriːdns tuː/|... ga ishonchlilik bag'ishlamoq|To make an idea seem more likely to be true.|support · strengthen · add weight to · make more believable|biror fikrni haqiqatga yaqinroq qilib ko'rsatmoq.|The discovery lends credence to the theory that Vikings reached America.|Bu kashfiyot vikinglar Amerikaga yetib borgan degan nazariyaga ishonchlilik bag'ishlaydi.|The find makes it more likely that Vikings sailed to America.|Topilma vikinglar Amerikaga suzib borganini ehtimolliroq qiladi.|Recent events lend credence to his warnings.|So'nggi voqealar uning ogohlantirishlariga ishonchlilik bag'ishlaydi.
be borne out by|phrase|/bi bɔːn aʊt baɪ/|... bilan tasdiqlanmoq|To be shown to be true by evidence.|be confirmed by · be supported by · be proved by|dalillar orqali to'g'riligi ko'rsatilmoq.|Her predictions were borne out by later events.|Uning bashoratlari keyingi voqealar bilan tasdiqlandi.|What she predicted actually happened.|U bashorat qilgan narsa haqiqatan ham yuz berdi.|The theory is not borne out by the available data.|Nazariya mavjud ma'lumotlar bilan tasdiqlanmaydi.
a methodological flaw|phrase|/ə ˌmeθədəˈlɒdʒɪkl flɔː/|uslubiy kamchilik|A mistake in the way research was designed or carried out.|a weakness in the method · a design error · a research flaw|tadqiqotni loyihalash yoki o'tkazishdagi xato.|Critics pointed out a methodological flaw in the survey.|Tanqidchilar so'rovnomadagi uslubiy kamchilikni ko'rsatib o'tishdi.|The way the survey was carried out was criticised as faulty.|So'rovnoma o'tkazilgan usul noto'g'ri deb tanqid qilindi.|A methodological flaw meant the results could not be trusted.|Uslubiy kamchilik tufayli natijalarga ishonib bo'lmasdi.
methodology|noun|/ˌmeθəˈdɒlədʒi/|metodologiya, uslubiyat|The set of methods used in a particular study.|methods · approach · procedure · technique|ma'lum tadqiqotda qo'llangan usullar to'plami.|The report explains its methodology in detail.|Hisobot o'z metodologiyasini batafsil tushuntiradi.|The report describes exactly how the research was done.|Hisobot tadqiqot qanday o'tkazilganini aniq tasvirlaydi.|Different methodologies can produce very different results.|Turli metodologiyalar juda turli natijalar berishi mumkin.
validity|noun|/vəˈlɪdəti/|to'g'rilik, asoslilik|The quality of being reasonable, logical or acceptable.|soundness · accuracy · legitimacy · reliability|oqilona, mantiqiy yoki maqbul bo'lish sifati.|Some experts question the validity of the test.|Ba'zi mutaxassislar testning asosliligiga shubha bildirishadi.|Certain specialists doubt whether the test really measures what it claims.|Ayrim mutaxassislar test haqiqatan ham da'vo qilganini o'lchaydimi, shubha qilishadi.|The small sample size reduces the validity of the conclusions.|Kichik tanlanma hajmi xulosalarning asosliligini pasaytiradi.
reliability|noun|/rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti/|ishonchlilik|The quality of giving the same good results every time.|dependability · consistency · trustworthiness|har safar bir xil yaxshi natija berish sifati.|The reliability of eyewitness accounts is often questioned.|Guvohlar ko'rsatmalarining ishonchliligi ko'pincha shubha ostiga olinadi.|People's memories of events are not always trustworthy.|Odamlarning voqealar haqidagi xotiralari har doim ham ishonchli emas.|Engineers tested the reliability of the new engine.|Muhandislar yangi dvigatelning ishonchliligini sinab ko'rishdi.
anomaly|noun|/əˈnɒməli/|anomaliya, g'ayrioddiy holat|Something that is different from what is usual or expected.|irregularity · exception · oddity · deviation|odatiy yoki kutilgandan farq qiladigan narsa.|Scientists could not explain the anomaly in the data.|Olimlar ma'lumotlardagi g'ayrioddiy holatni tushuntira olishmadi.|An unexpected result in the figures puzzled researchers.|Raqamlardagi kutilmagan natija tadqiqotchilarni hayron qoldirdi.|The warm winter was seen as an anomaly.|Iliq qish g'ayrioddiy hol sifatida baholandi.
