# Iqtisodiyot va savdo|Iqtisodiyot
rent-seeking behaviour|noun|/ˈrent ˌsiːkɪŋ bɪˈheɪvjə/|renta izlash xatti-harakati|Trying to gain wealth by influencing rules rather than by creating value.|profiting from privilege · lobbying for unfair advantage|qiymat yaratish emas, qoidalarga ta'sir qilish orqali boylik orttirishga intilish.|Economists criticise rent-seeking behaviour by large corporations.|Iqtisodchilar yirik korporatsiyalarning renta izlash xatti-harakatini tanqid qiladi.|Big firms often lobby for rules that benefit them without helping society.|Yirik firmalar ko'pincha jamiyatga foyda keltirmaydigan, faqat o'zlariga qulay qoidalarni talab qiladi.|Rent-seeking behaviour wastes resources.|Renta izlash xatti-harakati resurslarni isrof qiladi.
beggar-thy-neighbour policies|phrase|/ˌbeɡə ðaɪ ˈneɪbə ˈpɒləsiz/|qo'shniga zarar keltiruvchi siyosat|Economic policies that help one country at the expense of others.|protectionist policies · self-serving trade measures|bir mamlakatga boshqalar hisobiga foyda keltiradigan iqtisodiy siyosat.|Beggar-thy-neighbour policies worsened the Great Depression.|Qo'shniga zarar keltiruvchi siyosat Buyuk depressiyani yanada og'irlashtirdi.|Countries protected themselves in ways that hurt other nations.|Davlatlar o'zini boshqa xalqlarga zarar yetkazadigan usullar bilan himoya qildi.|Leaders agreed to avoid beggar-thy-neighbour policies.|Rahbarlar qo'shniga zarar keltiruvchi siyosatdan qochishga kelishib olishdi.
moral hazard|noun|/ˈmɒrəl ˈhæzəd/|axloqiy xavf|The risk that people behave less carefully because they are protected from the consequences.|reckless behaviour due to protection · lack of incentive to be careful|odamlar oqibatlardan himoyalangani sababli kamroq ehtiyotkor bo'lish xavfi.|Bank bail-outs create moral hazard.|Banklarni qutqarish axloqiy xavf tug'diradi.|If banks know they will be rescued, they may take bigger risks.|Agar banklar qutqarilishini bilsa, kattaroq xavfga borishi mumkin.|Insurance can lead to moral hazard if people become careless.|Sug'urta, agar odamlar beparvo bo'lib qolsa, axloqiy xavfga olib kelishi mumkin.
the tragedy of the commons|noun|/ðə ˈtrædʒədi əv ðə ˈkɒmənz/|umumiy mulk fojiasi|The way shared resources are overused because each person acts in their own interest.|overuse of shared resources · collective resource depletion|har kim o'z manfaati uchun harakat qilgani sababli umumiy resurslarning haddan tashqari ishlatilishi.|Overfishing is a classic example of the tragedy of the commons.|Baliqni haddan tashqari ovlash umumiy mulk fojiasining klassik namunasidir.|Each fisherman catches as much as he can, so the fish run out.|Har bir baliqchi imkon qadar ko'p tutadi va baliqlar tugaydi.|Clean air can suffer from the tragedy of the commons.|Toza havo ham umumiy mulk fojiasidan zarar ko'rishi mumkin.
creative destruction|noun|/kriˈeɪtɪv dɪˈstrʌkʃn/|ijodiy vayronagarchilik|The process by which new industries replace old ones.|innovation replacing old industries · economic renewal|yangi tarmoqlarning eskilari o'rnini egallash jarayoni.|Streaming services are an example of creative destruction.|Striming xizmatlari ijodiy vayronagarchilikning namunasidir.|New online services have replaced video rental shops.|Yangi onlayn xizmatlar video ijara do'konlarining o'rnini egalladi.|Creative destruction causes job losses but also new opportunities.|Ijodiy vayronagarchilik ish o'rinlarini yo'qotsa ham, yangi imkoniyatlar yaratadi.
quantitative easing|noun|/ˈkwɒntɪtətɪv ˈiːzɪŋ/|miqdoriy yumshatish|A policy in which a central bank creates money to buy financial assets and support the economy.|money printing · central bank asset buying|markaziy bank iqtisodiyotni qo'llab-quvvatlash uchun pul yaratib, moliyaviy aktivlarni sotib olish siyosati.|The central bank used quantitative easing after the crisis.|Markaziy bank inqirozdan keyin miqdoriy yumshatishdan foydalandi.|The bank created new money to help the economy recover.|Bank iqtisodiyot tiklanishiga yordam berish uchun yangi pul yaratdi.|Critics say quantitative easing increased inequality.|Tanqidchilar miqdoriy yumshatish tengsizlikni oshirdi deyishadi.
supply-side bottlenecks|phrase|/səˈplaɪ saɪd ˈbɒtlneks/|taklif tomonidagi to'siqlar|Problems in production or delivery that limit the supply of goods.|production constraints · supply problems · shortages|tovarlar taklifini cheklaydigan ishlab chiqarish yoki yetkazib berishdagi muammolar.|Supply-side bottlenecks pushed up the price of computer chips.|Taklif tomonidagi to'siqlar kompyuter chiplari narxini oshirib yubordi.|Factories could not produce enough chips, so prices rose.|Zavodlar yetarlicha chip ishlab chiqara olmadi va narxlar oshdi.|The government is trying to ease supply-side bottlenecks.|Hukumat taklif tomonidagi to'siqlarni yumshatishga harakat qilmoqda.
a capital-intensive industry|phrase|/ə ˈkæpɪtl ɪnˌtensɪv ˈɪndəstri/|kapital talab qiladigan soha|An industry that needs a lot of money for equipment and buildings.|an industry needing heavy investment · an expensive sector|uskunalar va binolar uchun ko'p pul talab qiladigan soha.|Steel making is a capital-intensive industry.|Po'lat ishlab chiqarish ko'p kapital talab qiladigan soha.|Making steel requires very expensive machinery.|Po'lat ishlab chiqarish juda qimmat uskunalarni talab qiladi.|Small firms struggle to enter a capital-intensive industry.|Kichik firmalarga kapital talab qiladigan sohaga kirish qiyin.
crony capitalism|noun|/ˈkrəʊni ˈkæpɪtəlɪzəm/|tanish-bilishchilik kapitalizmi|An economy in which business success depends on close links with officials.|corrupt capitalism · favouritism in business|biznes muvaffaqiyati mansabdorlar bilan yaqin aloqaga bog'liq iqtisodiyot.|Crony capitalism discourages honest competition.|Tanish-bilishchilik kapitalizmi halol raqobatga to'sqinlik qiladi.|When contracts go to friends of politicians, fair firms cannot compete.|Shartnomalar siyosatchilarning do'stlariga berilganda, halol firmalar raqobatlasha olmaydi.|The report warns of crony capitalism in the energy sector.|Hisobot energetika sohasidagi tanish-bilishchilik kapitalizmidan ogohlantiradi.
a deflationary spiral|phrase|/ə diːˈfleɪʃənəri ˈspaɪrəl/|deflyatsion spiral|A cycle in which falling prices lead to lower wages and spending, causing prices to fall further.|a downward price cycle · a self-reinforcing fall in prices|narxlarning tushishi ish haqi va xarajatlarning kamayishiga, bu esa narxlarning yanada tushishiga olib keladigan aylanma.|Japan struggled with a deflationary spiral for years.|Yaponiya yillar davomida deflyatsion spiral bilan kurashdi.|Prices and wages in Japan kept falling for a long time.|Yaponiyada narxlar va maoshlar uzoq vaqt tushib bordi.|Central banks fear a deflationary spiral more than mild inflation.|Markaziy banklar yengil inflyatsiyadan ko'ra deflyatsion spiraldan ko'proq qo'rqadi.
inelastic demand|noun|/ˌɪnɪˈlæstɪk dɪˈmɑːnd/|noelastik talab|Demand that changes very little when prices change.|price-insensitive demand · steady demand|narx o'zgarganda deyarli o'zgarmaydigan talab.|Petrol has inelastic demand because people need it anyway.|Benzinga talab noelastik, chunki odamlarga u baribir kerak.|People keep buying fuel even when it becomes more expensive.|Yoqilg'i qimmatlashsa ham odamlar uni sotib olishda davom etadi.|Medicines often have inelastic demand.|Dorilarga talab ko'pincha noelastik bo'ladi.
diminishing marginal returns|phrase|/dɪˈmɪnɪʃɪŋ ˈmɑːdʒɪnl rɪˈtɜːnz/|kamayib boruvchi qo'shimcha daromad|The idea that each extra unit of input produces a smaller increase in output.|decreasing benefits · smaller gains from extra effort|har bir qo'shimcha sarf mahsulotni tobora kamroq oshirishi g'oyasi.|Adding more fertiliser eventually brings diminishing marginal returns.|Ko'proq o'g'it qo'shish oxir-oqibat kamayib boruvchi qo'shimcha daromad keltiradi.|After a point, extra fertiliser increases the harvest less and less.|Ma'lum nuqtadan keyin qo'shimcha o'g'it hosilni tobora kamroq oshiradi.|Studying for too long leads to diminishing marginal returns.|Juda uzoq o'qish kamayib boruvchi natijaga olib keladi.
a sovereign wealth fund|phrase|/ə ˈsɒvrɪn welθ fʌnd/|suveren boylik fondi|A state-owned fund that invests a country's money.|a state investment fund · a national wealth fund|mamlakat pulini sarmoya qiladigan davlatga tegishli fond.|Norway's sovereign wealth fund invests oil money worldwide.|Norvegiyaning suveren boylik fondi neft pullarini butun dunyoga sarmoya qiladi.|Norway saves its oil income by investing it abroad.|Norvegiya neft daromadini chet elga sarmoya qilib saqlaydi.|The sovereign wealth fund protects the economy for future generations.|Suveren boylik fondi iqtisodiyotni kelajak avlodlar uchun himoya qiladi.
stagflation|noun|/stæɡˈfleɪʃn/|stagflyatsiya|A situation of high inflation combined with high unemployment and slow growth.|inflation with stagnation · rising prices and recession|yuqori inflyatsiya yuqori ishsizlik va sekin o'sish bilan qo'shilgan holat.|The oil crisis of the 1970s led to stagflation.|1970-yillardagi neft inqirozi stagflyatsiyaga olib keldi.|Prices rose sharply while the economy stopped growing.|Iqtisodiyot o'sishdan to'xtagan paytda narxlar keskin oshdi.|Stagflation is very difficult for governments to fix.|Stagflyatsiyani hukumatlar tuzatishi juda qiyin.
arbitrage|noun|/ˈɑːbɪtrɑːʒ/|arbitraj|Buying something in one market and selling it in another at a higher price.|profiting from price differences · trading on price gaps|biror narsani bir bozorda sotib olib, boshqasida qimmatroq sotish.|Traders profit from arbitrage between different exchanges.|Treyderlar turli birjalar o'rtasidagi arbitrajdan foyda ko'radi.|They buy cheaply in one place and sell at a higher price elsewhere.|Ular bir joyda arzon sotib olib, boshqa joyda qimmatroq sotishadi.|Technology has made arbitrage much faster.|Texnologiya arbitrajni ancha tezlashtirdi.
solvency|noun|/ˈsɒlvənsi/|to'lov qobiliyati|The ability to pay all your debts.|financial soundness · ability to pay debts · creditworthiness|barcha qarzlarini to'lay olish qobiliyati.|Regulators are worried about the solvency of several banks.|Nazoratchilar bir nechta bankning to'lov qobiliyatidan xavotirda.|Officials fear some banks cannot pay what they owe.|Rasmiylar ba'zi banklar qarzlarini to'lay olmasligidan qo'rqishadi.|The company's solvency was in doubt.|Kompaniyaning to'lov qobiliyati shubha ostida edi.
fiscal|adjective|/ˈfɪskl/|fiskal, moliyaviy (davlat)|Relating to government money, taxes and spending.|financial · budgetary · tax-related · economic|davlat pullari, soliqlar va xarajatlarga oid.|The government announced new fiscal measures to cut the deficit.|Hukumat taqchillikni kamaytirish uchun yangi fiskal choralar e'lon qildi.|The state introduced tax and spending changes to reduce debt.|Davlat qarzni kamaytirish uchun soliq va xarajat o'zgarishlarini joriy etdi.|The fiscal year begins in April.|Moliya yili aprelda boshlanadi.
speculative|adjective|/ˈspekjələtɪv/|spekulyativ, taxminiy|Based on guesses, or involving financial risk for possible profit.|risky · uncertain · hypothetical · conjectural|taxminlarga asoslangan yoki ehtimoliy foyda uchun moliyaviy xavfni o'z ichiga olgan.|Speculative investment drove up property prices.|Spekulyativ sarmoya ko'chmas mulk narxlarini oshirib yubordi.|Buyers hoping to sell later at a profit made houses more expensive.|Keyinroq foyda bilan sotishni kutgan xaridorlar uylarni qimmatlashtirdi.|Most claims about the future of AI are speculative.|Sun'iy intellekt kelajagi haqidagi da'volarning ko'pi taxminiy.
a glut of|phrase|/ə ɡlʌt əv/|... ning haddan tashqari ko'pligi|Too much of something, more than is needed.|an oversupply of · a surplus of · an excess of|keragidan ortiq ko'p narsa.|A glut of oil caused prices to collapse.|Neftning haddan tashqari ko'pligi narxlarning keskin tushishiga sabab bo'ldi.|There was so much oil that it became very cheap.|Neft shunchalik ko'p ediki, u juda arzonlashdi.|The city faces a glut of office space.|Shaharda ofis maydonlari haddan tashqari ko'p.
profligate spending|phrase|/ˈprɒflɪɡət ˈspendɪŋ/|isrofgarchilik bilan pul sarflash|Spending money in a careless and wasteful way.|wasteful spending · extravagance · overspending|pulni beparvo va isrofgarchilik bilan sarflash.|The government was accused of profligate spending.|Hukumat isrofgarchilik bilan pul sarflashda ayblandi.|Critics said officials wasted public money.|Tanqidchilar rasmiylar davlat pulini isrof qilgan deyishdi.|Years of profligate spending left the city in debt.|Yillar davomidagi isrofgarchilik shaharni qarzga botirdi.
# Psixologiya va xulq-atvor|Psixologiya
salience bias|noun|/ˈseɪliəns ˈbaɪəs/|ko'zga tashlanuvchanlik xatosi|The tendency to focus on things that are most noticeable or dramatic.|focusing on the obvious · attention to the striking|eng ko'zga tashlanadigan yoki dramatik narsalarga e'tibor qaratishga moyillik.|Because of salience bias, people fear plane crashes more than car accidents.|Ko'zga tashlanuvchanlik xatosi tufayli odamlar avtohalokatdan ko'ra samolyot halokatidan ko'proq qo'rqadi.|Dramatic air disasters seem more dangerous than everyday road accidents.|Dramatik havo halokatlari kundalik yo'l hodisalaridan xavfliroq tuyuladi.|Salience bias can distort investment decisions.|Ko'zga tashlanuvchanlik xatosi sarmoya qarorlarini buzishi mumkin.
the sunk cost fallacy|noun|/ðə sʌŋk kɒst ˈfæləsi/|yo'qotilgan xarajat xatosi|Continuing something because you have already invested in it, even when stopping would be better.|throwing good money after bad · refusing to cut losses|to'xtatish yaxshiroq bo'lsa ham, unga allaqachon sarmoya qilgani uchun biror narsani davom ettirish.|Finishing a boring film because you paid for it is the sunk cost fallacy.|Pul to'lagani uchun zerikarli filmni oxirigacha ko'rish yo'qotilgan xarajat xatosidir.|People continue with something just because they have already spent money.|Odamlar allaqachon pul sarflagani uchungina biror ishni davom ettiradi.|Governments often fall victim to the sunk cost fallacy.|Hukumatlar ko'pincha yo'qotilgan xarajat xatosiga tushadi.
epistemic humility|noun|/ˌepɪˈstiːmɪk hjuːˈmɪləti/|bilishdagi kamtarlik|Recognising the limits of your own knowledge.|intellectual modesty · awareness of what you don't know|o'z bilimining chegaralarini tan olish.|Good scientists show epistemic humility.|Yaxshi olimlar bilishdagi kamtarlik ko'rsatadi.|They admit what they do not yet know.|Ular hali nimani bilmasliklarini tan olishadi.|Epistemic humility makes people more open to new ideas.|Bilishdagi kamtarlik odamlarni yangi g'oyalarga ochiqroq qiladi.
affective forecasting|noun|/əˈfektɪv ˈfɔːkɑːstɪŋ/|hissiy bashorat|Predicting how you will feel in the future.|predicting future emotions · emotional prediction|kelajakda o'zingizni qanday his qilishingizni bashorat qilish.|People are bad at affective forecasting.|Odamlar hissiy bashoratda yomon.|We usually misjudge how happy or sad future events will make us.|Biz odatda kelajakdagi voqealar bizni qanchalik baxtli yoki xafa qilishini noto'g'ri baholaymiz.|Affective forecasting errors affect major life decisions.|Hissiy bashoratdagi xatolar hayotdagi muhim qarorlarga ta'sir qiladi.
mirror neurons|noun|/ˈmɪrə ˈnjʊərɒnz/|ko'zgu neyronlari|Brain cells that respond both when you act and when you watch others act.|empathy cells · imitation neurons|siz harakat qilganda ham, boshqalarning harakatini kuzatganda ham faollashadigan miya hujayralari.|Mirror neurons may help us understand others' feelings.|Ko'zgu neyronlari boshqalarning his-tuyg'ularini tushunishimizga yordam berishi mumkin.|These brain cells may explain why we feel what others feel.|Bu miya hujayralari nima uchun boshqalar his qilganini his qilishimizni tushuntirishi mumkin.|Scientists still debate the role of mirror neurons.|Olimlar ko'zgu neyronlarining roli haqida hamon bahslashadi.
the fundamental attribution error|noun|/ðə ˌfʌndəˈmentl ˌætrɪˈbjuːʃn ˈerə/|asosiy sabab izlash xatosi|Blaming people's character for their behaviour while ignoring their situation.|blaming personality over circumstances · correspondence bias|vaziyatni e'tiborsiz qoldirib, odamlarning xatti-harakatini xarakteriga bog'lash.|Assuming a late colleague is lazy is the fundamental attribution error.|Kechikkan hamkasbni dangasa deb o'ylash asosiy sabab izlash xatosidir.|We may blame the person instead of considering traffic or other causes.|Biz tirbandlik yoki boshqa sabablarni hisobga olish o'rniga shaxsni ayblashimiz mumkin.|The fundamental attribution error affects how we judge strangers.|Asosiy sabab izlash xatosi notanishlarni qanday baholashimizga ta'sir qiladi.
pathological altruism|noun|/ˌpæθəˈlɒdʒɪkl ˈæltruɪzəm/|patologik fidoyilik|Helping others in a way that ends up harming yourself or them.|harmful selflessness · self-destructive helping|o'zingizga yoki ularga zarar keltiradigan tarzda boshqalarga yordam berish.|Carers who never rest may suffer from pathological altruism.|Hech qachon dam olmaydigan parvarishchilar patologik fidoyilikdan aziyat chekishi mumkin.|Helping others too much can damage the helper's own health.|Boshqalarga haddan tashqari yordam berish yordamchining o'z sog'lig'iga zarar yetkazishi mumkin.|Pathological altruism can enable harmful behaviour in others.|Patologik fidoyilik boshqalardagi zararli xatti-harakatlarga yo'l ochishi mumkin.
subliminal priming|noun|/sʌbˈlɪmɪnl ˈpraɪmɪŋ/|ongosti tayyorlash|Influencing behaviour with signals people do not consciously notice.|unconscious influence · hidden suggestion|odamlar ongli ravishda sezmaydigan signallar orqali xatti-harakatga ta'sir qilish.|Studies of subliminal priming flash words too fast to see.|Ongosti tayyorlash tadqiqotlarida so'zlar ko'rib bo'lmaydigan darajada tez ko'rsatiladi.|Words appear so briefly that people do not notice them, yet behaviour changes.|So'zlar shunchalik qisqa ko'rinadiki, odamlar sezmaydi, lekin xatti-harakat o'zgaradi.|Subliminal priming in adverts is banned in many countries.|Reklamada ongosti tayyorlash ko'plab mamlakatlarda taqiqlangan.
the hedonic treadmill|noun|/ðə hiːˈdɒnɪk ˈtredmɪl/|gedonik tegirmon|The tendency to return to the same level of happiness after good or bad changes.|hedonic adaptation · getting used to good fortune|yaxshi yoki yomon o'zgarishlardan keyin bir xil baxt darajasiga qaytishga moyillik.|Because of the hedonic treadmill, a pay rise soon stops making us happier.|Gedonik tegirmon tufayli ish haqining oshishi tez orada bizni baxtliroq qilmay qo'yadi.|We quickly get used to more money and feel the same as before.|Biz ko'proq pulga tez o'rganamiz va avvalgidek his qilamiz.|The hedonic treadmill explains why buying more rarely satisfies us.|Gedonik tegirmon nima uchun ko'proq xarid qilish bizni kamdan-kam qoniqtirishini tushuntiradi.
illusory superiority|noun|/ɪˈluːsəri suːˌpɪəriˈɒrəti/|xayoliy ustunlik|The tendency to think you are better than average.|overconfidence · the above-average effect|o'zini o'rtachadan yaxshiroq deb o'ylashga moyillik.|Most drivers show illusory superiority about their skills.|Ko'pchilik haydovchilar o'z mahorati haqida xayoliy ustunlik ko'rsatadi.|Nearly everyone believes they drive better than most people.|Deyarli hamma o'zini ko'pchilikdan yaxshiroq haydayman deb hisoblaydi.|Illusory superiority can make people ignore advice.|Xayoliy ustunlik odamlarni maslahatlarni e'tiborsiz qoldirishga olib kelishi mumkin.
vicarious trauma|noun|/vɪˈkeəriəs ˈtrɔːmə/|ikkilamchi travma|Emotional harm suffered by people who hear about or witness others' suffering.|secondary trauma · compassion fatigue|boshqalarning azob-uqubati haqida eshitgan yoki guvoh bo'lganlar ko'radigan ruhiy zarar.|Journalists covering wars can experience vicarious trauma.|Urushlarni yorituvchi jurnalistlar ikkilamchi travmani boshdan kechirishi mumkin.|Reporting on suffering can deeply affect the reporter.|Azob-uqubat haqida xabar berish muxbirga chuqur ta'sir qilishi mumkin.|Therapists receive training to prevent vicarious trauma.|Psixoterapevtlar ikkilamchi travmaning oldini olish uchun o'qitiladi.
stoic equanimity|noun|/ˈstəʊɪk ˌekwəˈnɪməti/|stoik xotirjamlik|Calmness and acceptance in difficult situations.|calm acceptance · composure · serenity under pressure|qiyin vaziyatlarda xotirjamlik va qabul qilish.|She faced her illness with stoic equanimity.|U kasalligiga stoik xotirjamlik bilan yuzlandi.|She stayed calm and accepted her illness without complaining.|U xotirjam qoldi va kasalligini shikoyat qilmay qabul qildi.|Stoic equanimity was considered a virtue in ancient Rome.|Qadimgi Rimda stoik xotirjamlik fazilat hisoblangan.
compartmentalisation|noun|/ˌkɒmpɑːtˌmentəlaɪˈzeɪʃn/|hissiyotlarni alohida bo'laklarga ajratish|Separating different parts of your life or feelings so they do not affect each other.|mental separation · keeping things apart|hayot yoki his-tuyg'ularning turli qismlarini bir-biriga ta'sir qilmasligi uchun ajratish.|Surgeons rely on compartmentalisation to stay calm.|Jarrohlar xotirjam qolish uchun hissiyotlarni ajratishga tayanadi.|They keep their personal feelings separate from their work.|Ular shaxsiy his-tuyg'ularini ishdan ajratib turadi.|Too much compartmentalisation can be unhealthy.|Haddan tashqari ajratish sog'liq uchun zararli bo'lishi mumkin.
