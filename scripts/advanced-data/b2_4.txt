# Qishloq xo'jaligi va oziq-ovqat|Qishloq xo'jaligi
crop yields|phrase|/krɒp jiːldz/|hosildorlik|The amount of food produced by crops in a given area.|harvests · agricultural output · production per hectare|ma'lum maydondagi ekinlardan olingan oziq-ovqat miqdori.|New irrigation methods have doubled crop yields in the valley.|Yangi sug'orish usullari vodiydagi hosildorlikni ikki baravar oshirdi.|Farmers in the valley now harvest twice as much as before.|Vodiydagi fermerlar endi avvalgidan ikki baravar ko'p hosil yig'ishadi.|Rising temperatures could reduce crop yields across Africa.|Haroratning ko'tarilishi butun Afrikada hosildorlikni kamaytirishi mumkin.
arable land|phrase|/ˈærəbl lænd/|ekin ekishga yaroqli yer|Land that can be used for growing crops.|farmland · cultivable land · fertile ground|ekin yetishtirish uchun foydalanish mumkin bo'lgan yer.|Only ten per cent of the country is arable land.|Mamlakat hududining atigi o'n foizi ekin ekishga yaroqli yer.|Just a tenth of the land can be used for farming.|Yerning faqat o'ndan bir qismidan dehqonchilikda foydalanish mumkin.|Cities are expanding onto valuable arable land.|Shaharlar qimmatli ekin yerlariga qarab kengaymoqda.
intensive farming|noun|/ɪnˈtensɪv ˈfɑːmɪŋ/|intensiv dehqonchilik|Farming that produces as much as possible from the land, often using chemicals and machines.|industrial agriculture · factory farming · high-input farming|ko'pincha kimyoviy moddalar va mashinalar yordamida yerdan imkon qadar ko'p hosil oladigan dehqonchilik.|Intensive farming has damaged the soil in many regions.|Intensiv dehqonchilik ko'plab mintaqalarda tuproqqa zarar yetkazdi.|Industrial agriculture has harmed the land in many places.|Sanoat usulidagi dehqonchilik ko'p joylarda yerga zarar yetkazdi.|Intensive farming produces cheap food but uses a lot of water.|Intensiv dehqonchilik arzon oziq-ovqat beradi, lekin ko'p suv sarflaydi.
irrigation systems|phrase|/ˌɪrɪˈɡeɪʃn ˈsɪstəmz/|sug'orish tizimlari|Networks of channels or pipes that bring water to crops.|watering systems · canals · water channels|ekinlarga suv yetkazadigan kanal yoki quvurlar tarmog'i.|Ancient irrigation systems allowed farming in the desert.|Qadimiy sug'orish tizimlari cho'lda dehqonchilik qilish imkonini bergan.|Old canals made it possible to grow crops in dry lands.|Qadimgi kanallar qurg'oqchil yerlarda ekin yetishtirish imkonini bergan.|Modern drip irrigation systems save large amounts of water.|Zamonaviy tomchilatib sug'orish tizimlari katta miqdorda suvni tejaydi.
genetically modified crops|phrase|/dʒəˌnetɪkli ˈmɒdɪfaɪd krɒps/|genetik modifikatsiyalangan ekinlar|Plants whose DNA has been changed to give them useful features.|GM crops · engineered plants · biotech crops|foydali xususiyat berish uchun DNKsi o'zgartirilgan o'simliklar.|Genetically modified crops can resist pests without chemicals.|Genetik modifikatsiyalangan ekinlar kimyoviy moddalarsiz zararkunandalarga qarshi tura oladi.|Engineered plants can protect themselves from insects.|Muhandislik yo'li bilan yaratilgan o'simliklar o'zini hasharotlardan himoya qila oladi.|Some countries have banned genetically modified crops.|Ba'zi mamlakatlar genetik modifikatsiyalangan ekinlarni taqiqlagan.
organic produce|phrase|/ɔːˈɡænɪk ˈprɒdjuːs/|organik mahsulotlar|Food grown without artificial chemicals.|chemical-free food · natural produce · organically grown food|sun'iy kimyoviy moddalarsiz yetishtirilgan oziq-ovqat.|Demand for organic produce has risen sharply in recent years.|So'nggi yillarda organik mahsulotlarga talab keskin oshdi.|More people now want food grown without chemicals.|Endi ko'proq odam kimyoviy moddalarsiz yetishtirilgan ovqatni xohlaydi.|Organic produce is usually more expensive than ordinary food.|Organik mahsulotlar odatda oddiy ovqatdan qimmatroq.
soil fertility|phrase|/sɔɪl fəˈtɪləti/|tuproq unumdorligi|How well soil can support the growth of plants.|soil quality · richness of the land · productivity of the soil|tuproqning o'simliklar o'sishini qanchalik ta'minlay olishi.|Rotating crops helps to maintain soil fertility.|Almashlab ekish tuproq unumdorligini saqlashga yordam beradi.|Changing crops each year keeps the land productive.|Har yili ekinlarni almashtirish yerni unumdor saqlaydi.|Floods can increase soil fertility by depositing rich mud.|Suv toshqinlari boy loyqa qoldirib tuproq unumdorligini oshirishi mumkin.
staple crops|phrase|/ˈsteɪpl krɒps/|asosiy oziq-ovqat ekinlari|The main crops that people in a region depend on for food.|basic foods · main food crops · dietary staples|mintaqa aholisi oziq-ovqat uchun tayanadigan asosiy ekinlar.|Rice and wheat are the world's most important staple crops.|Guruch va bug'doy dunyodagi eng muhim asosiy oziq-ovqat ekinlaridir.|Most people rely on rice and wheat as their basic food.|Ko'pchilik asosiy oziq-ovqat sifatida guruch va bug'doyga tayanadi.|Climate change threatens the staple crops of many poor nations.|Iqlim o'zgarishi ko'plab kambag'al mamlakatlarning asosiy ekinlariga tahdid solmoqda.
drought-resistant varieties|phrase|/ˈdraʊt rɪˌzɪstənt vəˈraɪətiz/|qurg'oqchilikka chidamli navlar|Types of plants that can survive with very little water.|hardy strains · crops that tolerate dry conditions|juda oz suv bilan ham yashay oladigan o'simlik turlari.|Scientists have developed drought-resistant varieties of maize.|Olimlar makkajo'xorining qurg'oqchilikka chidamli navlarini yaratishdi.|New types of maize can grow with little rain.|Makkajo'xorining yangi navlari kam yomg'irda ham o'sa oladi.|Farmers are switching to drought-resistant varieties as rainfall declines.|Yog'ingarchilik kamaygani sari fermerlar qurg'oqchilikka chidamli navlarga o'tmoqda.
vertical farming|noun|/ˈvɜːtɪkl ˈfɑːmɪŋ/|vertikal dehqonchilik|Growing crops in stacked layers, usually indoors.|indoor farming · stacked agriculture · urban farming|ekinlarni ustma-ust qatlamlarda, odatda yopiq joyda yetishtirish.|Vertical farming allows cities to grow food in tall buildings.|Vertikal dehqonchilik shaharlarga baland binolarda oziq-ovqat yetishtirish imkonini beradi.|Food can be produced indoors on many floors.|Oziq-ovqatni bino ichida ko'p qavatlarda yetishtirish mumkin.|Vertical farming uses far less water than traditional agriculture.|Vertikal dehqonchilik an'anaviy dehqonchilikka qaraganda ancha kam suv sarflaydi.
the food supply chain|phrase|/ðə fuːd səˈplaɪ tʃeɪn/|oziq-ovqat yetkazib berish zanjiri|All the stages food passes through from the farm to the consumer.|food distribution system · farm-to-shop network|oziq-ovqatning fermadan iste'molchigacha o'tadigan barcha bosqichlari.|A third of all food is wasted somewhere in the food supply chain.|Barcha oziq-ovqatning uchdan biri yetkazib berish zanjirining biror joyida isrof bo'ladi.|Much food is lost between the farm and the shop.|Ko'p oziq-ovqat ferma va do'kon o'rtasida yo'qoladi.|The strike caused serious problems in the food supply chain.|Ish tashlash oziq-ovqat yetkazib berish zanjirida jiddiy muammolarga sabab bo'ldi.
harvest|noun|/ˈhɑːvɪst/|hosil, hosil yig'imi|The gathering of crops, or the amount of crops gathered.|crop · yield · gathering · produce|ekinlarni yig'ish yoki yig'ilgan hosil miqdori.|Heavy rain has damaged this year's grape harvest.|Kuchli yomg'ir bu yilgi uzum hosiliga zarar yetkazdi.|Bad weather has spoiled the grapes this year.|Yomon ob-havo bu yil uzumni buzdi.|The whole village helps with the harvest every autumn.|Har kuzda butun qishloq hosil yig'imiga yordam beradi.
livestock|noun|/ˈlaɪvstɒk/|chorva mollari|Animals such as cows, sheep and goats kept on a farm.|farm animals · cattle · herds|fermada boqiladigan sigir, qo'y va echki kabi hayvonlar.|Livestock produce a large share of greenhouse gases.|Chorva mollari issiqxona gazlarining katta qismini chiqaradi.|Farm animals are a major source of climate-warming emissions.|Ferma hayvonlari iqlimni isituvchi chiqindilarning asosiy manbaidir.|The drought forced herders to sell their livestock.|Qurg'oqchilik chorvadorlarni mollarini sotishga majbur qildi.
fertiliser|noun|/ˈfɜːtəlaɪzə/|o'g'it|A substance added to soil to help plants grow.|plant food · manure · nutrients|o'simliklar o'sishiga yordam berish uchun tuproqqa qo'shiladigan modda.|Too much fertiliser can pollute rivers and lakes.|Haddan tashqari ko'p o'g'it daryo va ko'llarni ifloslantirishi mumkin.|Excess plant chemicals wash into water and harm it.|Ortiqcha kimyoviy o'g'itlar suvga yuvilib, unga zarar yetkazadi.|Farmers use natural fertiliser made from animal waste.|Fermerlar hayvon chiqindilaridan tayyorlangan tabiiy o'g'itdan foydalanadi.
pesticide|noun|/ˈpestɪsaɪd/|pestitsid, zararkunandalarga qarshi kimyoviy vosita|A chemical used to kill insects that damage crops.|insecticide · crop spray · chemical treatment|ekinlarga zarar yetkazadigan hasharotlarni o'ldirish uchun ishlatiladigan kimyoviy modda.|Some pesticides have been linked to the decline of bees.|Ba'zi pestitsidlar asalarilar kamayishi bilan bog'langan.|Certain crop chemicals may be killing bee populations.|Ayrim kimyoviy vositalar asalarilarni qirib yuborayotgan bo'lishi mumkin.|Organic farmers do not use artificial pesticides.|Organik fermerlar sun'iy pestitsidlardan foydalanmaydi.
cultivate|verb|/ˈkʌltɪveɪt/|yetishtirmoq, ishlov bermoq|To prepare land and grow crops on it.|grow · farm · plant · raise|yerni tayyorlab, unda ekin yetishtirmoq.|The ancient Egyptians cultivated wheat along the Nile.|Qadimgi misrliklar Nil bo'ylab bug'doy yetishtirishgan.|People in ancient Egypt grew wheat near the river.|Qadimgi Misrda odamlar daryo yaqinida bug'doy yetishtirishgan.|Tea is cultivated on the hills of Sri Lanka.|Shri-Lanka tepaliklarida choy yetishtiriladi.
overgrazing|noun|/ˌəʊvəˈɡreɪzɪŋ/|haddan tashqari mol boqish|Allowing animals to eat so much grass that the land is damaged.|excessive grazing · too many animals feeding on land|hayvonlarga shunchalik ko'p o't yedirishki, yer shikastlanadi.|Overgrazing has turned grassland into desert in parts of Asia.|Haddan tashqari mol boqish Osiyoning ayrim qismlarida yaylovni cho'lga aylantirdi.|Too many animals eating the grass destroyed the pasture.|O'tni juda ko'p hayvon yeb, yaylovni yo'q qildi.|Limiting herd sizes can prevent overgrazing.|Poda hajmini cheklash haddan tashqari mol boqishning oldini oladi.
crop failure|noun|/krɒp ˈfeɪljə/|hosilsizlik|A situation in which crops do not grow or produce food.|a failed harvest · poor harvest · loss of crops|ekinlar o'smaydigan yoki hosil bermaydigan vaziyat.|Repeated crop failure forced families to leave the region.|Takroriy hosilsizlik oilalarni mintaqani tark etishga majbur qildi.|Because the harvest failed again and again, people moved away.|Hosil qayta-qayta bo'lmagani sababli odamlar ko'chib ketishdi.|Crop failure in one country can raise food prices worldwide.|Bir mamlakatdagi hosilsizlik butun dunyoda oziq-ovqat narxlarini oshirishi mumkin.
# Xavf, inqiroz va bardoshlilik|Xavf va inqiroz
take precautionary measures|phrase|/teɪk prɪˈkɔːʃənəri ˈmeʒəz/|ehtiyot choralarini ko'rmoq|To do things in advance to prevent harm.|take precautions · act to prevent harm · be on the safe side|zararning oldini olish uchun oldindan choralar ko'rmoq.|Residents were told to take precautionary measures before the storm.|Aholiga bo'ron oldidan ehtiyot choralarini ko'rish aytildi.|People were advised to prepare for the storm in advance.|Odamlarga bo'ronga oldindan tayyorlanish maslahat berildi.|Hospitals took precautionary measures to stop the infection spreading.|Shifoxonalar infeksiya tarqalishini to'xtatish uchun ehtiyot choralarini ko'rishdi.
a contingency plan|phrase|/ə kənˈtɪndʒənsi plæn/|favqulodda vaziyat rejasi|A plan to be used if something goes wrong.|a backup plan · a plan B · an emergency plan|biror narsa noto'g'ri ketsa qo'llaniladigan reja.|Every school should have a contingency plan for floods.|Har bir maktabda suv toshqini uchun favqulodda reja bo'lishi kerak.|Schools need a backup plan in case of flooding.|Maktablarga suv toshqini bo'lgan holat uchun zaxira reja kerak.|The company had no contingency plan when its main supplier closed.|Asosiy yetkazib beruvchi yopilganda kompaniyaning favqulodda rejasi yo'q edi.
early warning systems|phrase|/ˈɜːli ˈwɔːnɪŋ ˈsɪstəmz/|erta ogohlantirish tizimlari|Equipment that gives advance notice of danger.|alert systems · advance warnings · alarm systems|xavf haqida oldindan xabar beruvchi uskunalar.|Early warning systems have saved thousands of lives during tsunamis.|Erta ogohlantirish tizimlari tsunami paytida minglab hayotni saqlab qoldi.|Alerts given in advance allowed many people to escape the waves.|Oldindan berilgan ogohlantirishlar ko'p odamga to'lqinlardan qochishga imkon berdi.|The region is installing early warning systems for earthquakes.|Mintaqa zilzilalar uchun erta ogohlantirish tizimlarini o'rnatmoqda.
avert a crisis|phrase|/əˈvɜːt ə ˈkraɪsɪs/|inqirozning oldini olmoq|To prevent a dangerous situation from happening.|prevent a disaster · head off trouble · avoid an emergency|xavfli vaziyat yuz berishining oldini olmoq.|Quick action by the central bank helped to avert a crisis.|Markaziy bankning tezkor harakati inqirozning oldini olishga yordam berdi.|The bank acted fast and prevented an economic disaster.|Bank tez harakat qilib, iqtisodiy falokatning oldini oldi.|Talks continued all night in an effort to avert a crisis.|Inqirozning oldini olish uchun muzokaralar tun bo'yi davom etdi.
weather the storm|phrase|/ˈweðə ðə stɔːm/|qiyinchilikdan omon chiqmoq|To survive a difficult period.|get through difficulties · survive hard times · ride out a crisis|qiyin davrdan omon o'tmoq.|Small shops struggled to weather the storm of the recession.|Kichik do'konlar turg'unlik qiyinchiligidan omon chiqishda qiynalishdi.|Many small businesses found it hard to survive the downturn.|Ko'plab kichik bizneslarga turg'unlikdan omon chiqish qiyin bo'ldi.|With strong savings, the family managed to weather the storm.|Katta jamg'arma tufayli oila qiyinchilikdan omon chiqishga muvaffaq bo'ldi.
spiral out of control|phrase|/ˈspaɪrəl aʊt əv kənˈtrəʊl/|nazoratdan chiqib ketmoq|To become worse very quickly in a way that cannot be stopped.|get out of hand · escalate rapidly · become unmanageable|to'xtatib bo'lmaydigan tarzda juda tez yomonlashmoq.|Without action, the debt could spiral out of control.|Chora ko'rilmasa, qarz nazoratdan chiqib ketishi mumkin.|If nothing is done, the debt may become unmanageable.|Hech narsa qilinmasa, qarzni boshqarib bo'lmay qolishi mumkin.|The small fire spiralled out of control in strong winds.|Kuchli shamolda kichik yong'in nazoratdan chiqib ketdi.
recover from setbacks|phrase|/rɪˈkʌvə frəm ˈsetbæks/|muvaffaqiyatsizliklardan keyin o'ziga kelmoq|To return to a good state after problems or failures.|bounce back · overcome difficulties · get back on track|muammo yoki muvaffaqiyatsizlikdan keyin yaxshi holatga qaytmoq.|Resilient people recover from setbacks more quickly.|Bardoshli odamlar muvaffaqiyatsizliklardan keyin tezroq o'ziga keladi.|Some people bounce back faster after failure.|Ba'zi odamlar muvaffaqiyatsizlikdan keyin tezroq tiklanadi.|The team recovered from early setbacks to win the league.|Jamoa dastlabki muvaffaqiyatsizliklardan keyin o'ziga kelib, ligada g'olib bo'ldi.
emergency preparedness|noun|/ɪˈmɜːdʒənsi prɪˈpeədnəs/|favqulodda vaziyatlarga tayyorgarlik|Being ready to deal with disasters and emergencies.|disaster readiness · crisis planning · being prepared|ofat va favqulodda vaziyatlarga qarshi kurashishga tayyor bo'lish.|The city has improved its emergency preparedness since the earthquake.|Zilziladan keyin shahar favqulodda vaziyatlarga tayyorgarligini yaxshiladi.|The city is now better ready for disasters.|Shahar endi ofatlarga yaxshiroq tayyor.|Emergency preparedness includes storing food and water.|Favqulodda vaziyatlarga tayyorgarlik oziq-ovqat va suv zaxirasini o'z ichiga oladi.
natural disasters|phrase|/ˈnætʃrəl dɪˈzɑːstəz/|tabiiy ofatlar|Events such as floods and earthquakes that cause great damage.|catastrophes · extreme weather events · natural hazards|katta zarar yetkazadigan suv toshqini va zilzila kabi hodisalar.|Natural disasters are becoming more frequent because of climate change.|Iqlim o'zgarishi tufayli tabiiy ofatlar tez-tez sodir bo'lmoqda.|Floods and storms happen more often as the climate warms.|Iqlim isigani sari suv toshqinlari va bo'ronlar ko'proq sodir bo'lmoqda.|Poor countries suffer most from natural disasters.|Kambag'al mamlakatlar tabiiy ofatlardan eng ko'p aziyat chekadi.
pose a threat to|phrase|/pəʊz ə θret tuː/|... ga tahdid solmoq|To be a possible danger to something.|threaten · endanger · put at risk · be a danger to|biror narsa uchun ehtimoliy xavf bo'lmoq.|Rising sea levels pose a threat to coastal cities.|Dengiz sathining ko'tarilishi qirg'oq bo'yidagi shaharlarga tahdid soladi.|Cities by the sea are in danger from higher water levels.|Dengiz bo'yidagi shaharlar suv sathi ko'tarilishi tufayli xavf ostida.|Invasive plants pose a threat to native species.|Bosqinchi o'simliklar mahalliy turlarga tahdid soladi.
at risk of|phrase|/æt rɪsk əv/|... xavfi ostida|In a situation where something bad might happen.|in danger of · vulnerable to · likely to suffer|yomon narsa yuz berishi mumkin bo'lgan vaziyatda.|Children who do not exercise are at risk of obesity.|Jismoniy mashq qilmaydigan bolalar semizlik xavfi ostida.|Inactive children may become overweight.|Harakatsiz bolalar ortiqcha vaznga ega bo'lishi mumkin.|Thousands of homes are at risk of flooding.|Minglab uylar suv bosish xavfi ostida.
reduce the risk of|phrase|/rɪˈdjuːs ðə rɪsk əv/|... xavfini kamaytirmoq|To make something bad less likely to happen.|lower the chance of · minimise the danger of · help prevent|yomon narsa yuz berish ehtimolini kamaytirmoq.|Wearing a helmet can reduce the risk of serious injury.|Dubulg'a kiyish jiddiy jarohat xavfini kamaytirishi mumkin.|Helmets make bad injuries less likely.|Dubulg'alar og'ir jarohatlar ehtimolini kamaytiradi.|Eating fish may reduce the risk of heart disease.|Baliq iste'mol qilish yurak kasalligi xavfini kamaytirishi mumkin.
restore stability|phrase|/rɪˈstɔː stəˈbɪləti/|barqarorlikni tiklamoq|To bring back a calm and steady situation.|bring back order · re-establish calm · stabilise|tinch va barqaror vaziyatni qaytarmoq.|The new government promised to restore stability to the economy.|Yangi hukumat iqtisodiyotga barqarorlikni qaytarishga va'da berdi.|Leaders said they would make the economy steady again.|Rahbarlar iqtisodiyotni yana barqaror qilishlarini aytishdi.|It took months to restore stability after the riots.|Tartibsizliklardan keyin barqarorlikni tiklash uchun oylar kerak bo'ldi.
hazard|noun|/ˈhæzəd/|xavf, xavf manbai|Something that could be dangerous or cause damage.|danger · risk · threat · peril|xavfli bo'lishi yoki zarar yetkazishi mumkin bo'lgan narsa.|Old electrical wires are a serious fire hazard.|Eski elektr simlari jiddiy yong'in xavfi manbaidir.|Ancient wiring could easily start a fire.|Eski simlar osongina yong'in chiqarishi mumkin.|Ice on the roads is a major hazard for drivers in winter.|Qishda yo'llardagi muz haydovchilar uchun katta xavf.
vulnerable|adjective|/ˈvʌlnərəbl/|zaif, himoyasiz|Easily harmed or hurt.|at risk · defenceless · exposed · susceptible|osongina zarar ko'radigan.|Elderly people are especially vulnerable during heatwaves.|Issiq to'lqinlari paytida keksalar ayniqsa zaif bo'ladi.|Older people face particular danger in very hot weather.|Juda issiq havoda keksalar alohida xavfga duch keladi.|Low-lying islands are vulnerable to rising seas.|Pastda joylashgan orollar dengiz sathining ko'tarilishiga qarshi himoyasiz.
collapse|verb|/kəˈlæps/|qulamoq, barbod bo'lmoq|To fall down suddenly, or to fail completely.|fall down · break down · fail · crumble|to'satdan qulab tushmoq yoki butunlay muvaffaqiyatsiz bo'lmoq.|The bridge collapsed after weeks of heavy rain.|Haftalab davom etgan kuchli yomg'irdan keyin ko'prik qulab tushdi.|The bridge fell down following a long period of rain.|Uzoq yomg'irli davrdan keyin ko'prik qulab tushdi.|Fish stocks collapsed because of overfishing.|Haddan tashqari ko'p ovlash tufayli baliq zaxiralari barbod bo'ldi.
crisis|noun|/ˈkraɪsɪs/|inqiroz|A time of great difficulty or danger.|emergency · disaster · catastrophe · critical situation|katta qiyinchilik yoki xavf davri.|The country is facing a water crisis.|Mamlakat suv inqiroziga duch kelmoqda.|There is a severe shortage of water in the country.|Mamlakatda jiddiy suv tanqisligi bor.|Many families were hit hard by the financial crisis.|Ko'plab oilalar moliyaviy inqirozdan qattiq zarar ko'rdi.
a worst-case scenario|phrase|/ə ˌwɜːst keɪs səˈnɑːriəʊ/|eng yomon holat|The most unpleasant thing that could possibly happen.|the worst possible outcome · the gloomiest forecast|yuz berishi mumkin bo'lgan eng yoqimsiz narsa.|In a worst-case scenario, sea levels could rise by two metres.|Eng yomon holatda dengiz sathi ikki metrga ko'tarilishi mumkin.|At the very worst, the sea might be two metres higher.|Eng yomon holatda dengiz ikki metr balandroq bo'lishi mumkin.|Engineers designed the dam to survive a worst-case scenario.|Muhandislar to'g'onni eng yomon holatga bardosh beradigan qilib loyihalashdi.
# Baholash, daraja va qamrov|Baholash va qamrov
a substantial proportion of|phrase|/ə səbˈstænʃl prəˈpɔːʃn əv/|... ning katta qismi|A large part of something.|a large share of · a considerable part of · many of|biror narsaning katta qismi.|A substantial proportion of the city's water is lost through leaks.|Shahar suvining katta qismi oqishlar orqali yo'qoladi.|Much of the water supply leaks away before it reaches homes.|Suvning ko'pi uylarga yetib bormasdan oqib ketadi.|A substantial proportion of students work part-time.|Talabalarning katta qismi yarim stavkada ishlaydi.
