# Xavf, inqiroz va bardoshlilik|Xavf va inqiroz
a ticking time bomb|phrase|/ə ˈtɪkɪŋ taɪm bɒm/|portlashga tayyor bomba, yashirin xavf|A situation that will become a serious problem in the future if nothing is done.|a looming disaster · a hidden danger · a crisis waiting to happen|hech narsa qilinmasa, kelajakda jiddiy muammoga aylanadigan vaziyat.|Rising obesity is a ticking time bomb for health services.|Semizlikning ortishi sog'liqni saqlash tizimi uchun portlashga tayyor bombadir.|Unless action is taken, obesity will overwhelm hospitals.|Chora ko'rilmasa, semizlik shifoxonalarni to'ldirib yuboradi.|The pension system is a ticking time bomb.|Pensiya tizimi yashirin xavf manbaidir.
a perfect storm|phrase|/ə ˈpɜːfɪkt stɔːm/|barcha yomon omillarning birlashuvi|A situation in which several bad things happen at the same time and make things very bad.|a combination of disasters · a worst-case combination|bir nechta yomon narsa bir vaqtda sodir bo'lib, vaziyatni juda yomonlashtirishi.|Drought, war and high prices created a perfect storm.|Qurg'oqchilik, urush va yuqori narxlar barcha yomon omillarning birlashuvini yuzaga keltirdi.|Several problems arrived at once and caused a severe crisis.|Bir nechta muammo bir vaqtda kelib, og'ir inqirozga sabab bo'ldi.|The company faced a perfect storm of falling sales and rising costs.|Kompaniya savdo pasayishi va xarajatlar oshishining bir vaqtda kelishiga duch keldi.
a domino effect|phrase|/ə ˈdɒmɪnəʊ ɪˌfekt/|domino effekti|A situation in which one event causes a series of similar events.|a chain reaction · a ripple effect · a knock-on effect|bitta voqea bir qator o'xshash voqealarga sabab bo'ladigan vaziyat.|The failure of one bank caused a domino effect across the system.|Bitta bankning barbod bo'lishi butun tizimda domino effektiga sabab bo'ldi.|When one bank failed, others quickly followed.|Bitta bank barbod bo'lgach, boshqalari ham tezda ergashdi.|Experts fear a domino effect if one country leaves the union.|Mutaxassislar bitta davlat ittifoqni tark etsa, domino effekti bo'lishidan qo'rqishadi.
hang by a thread|phrase|/hæŋ baɪ ə θred/|qil ustida turmoq|To be in a very dangerous or uncertain situation.|be in great danger · be very precarious · be close to failure|juda xavfli yoki noaniq vaziyatda bo'lmoq.|After the accident, his life hung by a thread.|Baxtsiz hodisadan keyin uning hayoti qil ustida turdi.|He was very close to dying after the accident.|Baxtsiz hodisadan keyin u o'limga juda yaqin edi.|The peace agreement is hanging by a thread.|Tinchlik kelishuvi qil ustida turibdi.
proactive risk governance|noun|/prəʊˈæktɪv rɪsk ˈɡʌvənəns/|faol xavf boshqaruvi|Managing dangers before they happen rather than reacting afterwards.|forward-looking risk management · preventive planning|xavflarni yuz bergandan keyin emas, oldindan boshqarish.|Proactive risk governance could have prevented the disaster.|Faol xavf boshqaruvi falokatning oldini olishi mumkin edi.|Planning for risks in advance would have stopped the disaster.|Xavflarni oldindan rejalashtirish falokatni to'xtatgan bo'lardi.|Insurers reward companies for proactive risk governance.|Sug'urtachilar kompaniyalarni faol xavf boshqaruvi uchun rag'batlantiradi.
a near miss|phrase|/ə nɪə mɪs/|zo'rg'a oldi olingan baxtsiz hodisa|A situation in which an accident almost happened.|a close call · a narrow escape · a lucky escape|baxtsiz hodisa sodir bo'lishiga oz qolgan vaziyat.|Two planes had a near miss over the airport.|Ikki samolyot aeroport ustida zo'rg'a to'qnashuvdan qochdi.|The aircraft very nearly crashed into each other.|Samolyotlar bir-biriga to'qnashishiga juda oz qoldi.|Every near miss should be reported and investigated.|Har bir zo'rg'a oldi olingan hodisa haqida xabar berilishi va tekshirilishi kerak.
damage limitation|noun|/ˈdæmɪdʒ ˌlɪmɪˈteɪʃn/|zararni cheklash|Actions to reduce the harm caused by a bad situation.|limiting the harm · minimising losses · containing damage|yomon vaziyat keltirgan zararni kamaytirish choralari.|The company is now focused on damage limitation.|Kompaniya endi zararni cheklashga e'tibor qaratmoqda.|The firm is trying to reduce the harm caused by the scandal.|Firma janjal keltirgan zararni kamaytirishga harakat qilmoqda.|The minister's apology was an exercise in damage limitation.|Vazirning kechirim so'rashi zararni cheklashga urinish edi.
a looming crisis|phrase|/ə ˈluːmɪŋ ˈkraɪsɪs/|yaqinlashib kelayotgan inqiroz|A serious problem that is about to happen.|an approaching crisis · an impending emergency|yuz berish arafasidagi jiddiy muammo.|Experts warn of a looming crisis in water supply.|Mutaxassislar suv ta'minotidagi yaqinlashib kelayotgan inqirozdan ogohlantirmoqda.|A serious water shortage will soon arrive unless action is taken.|Chora ko'rilmasa, tez orada jiddiy suv tanqisligi yuzaga keladi.|The government ignored the looming crisis.|Hukumat yaqinlashib kelayotgan inqirozni e'tiborsiz qoldirdi.
be fraught with danger|phrase|/bi frɔːt wɪð ˈdeɪndʒə/|xavf-xatarga to'la bo'lmoq|To be full of risks.|be very risky · be full of hazards · be perilous|xavflarga to'la bo'lmoq.|The journey across the desert was fraught with danger.|Cho'l orqali sayohat xavf-xatarga to'la edi.|Crossing the desert involved many serious risks.|Cho'lni kesib o'tish ko'plab jiddiy xavflarni o'z ichiga olgan.|Investing in new markets is fraught with danger.|Yangi bozorlarga sarmoya kiritish xavf-xatarga to'la.
a calculated risk|phrase|/ə ˈkælkjuleɪtɪd rɪsk/|o'ylangan tavakkal|A risk taken after carefully considering the possible results.|a considered gamble · a deliberate risk|ehtimoliy natijalarni puxta o'ylab ko'rgandan keyin qilingan tavakkal.|Opening a second shop was a calculated risk.|Ikkinchi do'konni ochish o'ylangan tavakkal edi.|The owner thought carefully before taking the chance.|Egasi tavakkal qilishdan oldin yaxshilab o'ylab ko'rdi.|Investors are willing to take a calculated risk.|Investorlar o'ylangan tavakkalga tayyor.
in the eye of the storm|phrase|/ɪn ði aɪ əv ðə stɔːm/|bo'ron markazida|At the centre of a difficult or controversial situation.|at the centre of controversy · in the middle of a crisis|qiyin yoki bahsli vaziyatning markazida.|The minister found himself in the eye of the storm.|Vazir o'zini bo'ron markazida topdi.|The minister was at the centre of the scandal.|Vazir janjalning markazida edi.|Nurses were in the eye of the storm during the pandemic.|Pandemiya davrida hamshiralar bo'ron markazida bo'lishdi.
perilous|adjective|/ˈperələs/|xatarli|Very dangerous.|dangerous · hazardous · risky · precarious|juda xavfli.|Migrants made a perilous journey across the sea.|Muhojirlar dengiz orqali xatarli safarga chiqishdi.|The trip across the sea was extremely dangerous.|Dengiz orqali safar juda xavfli edi.|The economy is in a perilous state.|Iqtisodiyot xatarli ahvolda.
cataclysmic|adjective|/ˌkætəˈklɪzmɪk/|halokatli, vayronkor|Causing sudden and violent destruction.|catastrophic · devastating · disastrous · calamitous|to'satdan va shiddatli vayronagarchilikka sabab bo'ladigan.|A cataclysmic eruption destroyed the ancient city.|Halokatli otilish qadimiy shaharni vayron qildi.|A huge volcanic explosion wiped out the city.|Ulkan vulqon otilishi shaharni yer bilan yakson qildi.|Scientists warn of cataclysmic climate change.|Olimlar halokatli iqlim o'zgarishidan ogohlantiradi.
unforeseen|adjective|/ˌʌnfɔːˈsiːn/|kutilmagan, oldindan ko'rilmagan|Not expected or predicted.|unexpected · unpredicted · unanticipated · surprise|kutilmagan yoki oldindan aytilmagan.|The project was delayed by unforeseen problems.|Loyiha kutilmagan muammolar tufayli kechikdi.|Problems nobody had predicted slowed the project down.|Hech kim oldindan ko'rmagan muammolar loyihani sekinlashtirdi.|Always keep money aside for unforeseen expenses.|Doimo kutilmagan xarajatlar uchun pul ajratib qo'ying.
foreboding|noun|/fɔːˈbəʊdɪŋ/|yomonlikni oldindan sezish|A feeling that something bad will happen.|sense of doom · apprehension · premonition · dread|yomon narsa yuz berishini oldindan his qilish.|She was filled with foreboding as the storm approached.|Bo'ron yaqinlashgani sari u yomonlikni oldindan sezdi.|She felt that something terrible was going to happen.|U dahshatli narsa yuz berishini his qildi.|The novel opens with a sense of foreboding.|Roman yomonlikni oldindan sezish hissi bilan boshlanadi.
inimical to|phrase|/ɪˈnɪmɪkl tuː/|... ga zararli, ... ga dushman|Harmful to or tending to prevent something.|harmful to · hostile to · damaging to · detrimental to|biror narsaga zararli yoki unga to'sqinlik qiladigan.|Constant noise is inimical to concentration.|Doimiy shovqin diqqatni jamlashga zararli.|It is very hard to focus when there is always noise.|Doimo shovqin bo'lsa, diqqatni jamlash juda qiyin.|Censorship is inimical to creativity.|Senzura ijodkorlikka dushmandir.
stave off|phrasal verb|/steɪv ɒf/|oldini olmoq, kechiktirmoq|To stop something bad from happening for a while.|prevent · ward off · hold off · avert|yomon narsaning yuz berishini bir muddat to'xtatib turmoq.|The loan helped the company stave off bankruptcy.|Qarz kompaniyaga bankrotlikning oldini olishga yordam berdi.|The borrowed money kept the firm from going bankrupt for now.|Olingan qarz firmani hozircha bankrot bo'lishdan saqladi.|Exercise may help stave off memory loss.|Jismoniy mashq xotira zaiflashuvining oldini olishga yordam berishi mumkin.
# Baholash, daraja va qamrov|Baholash va qamrov

a modicum of|phrase|/ə ˈmɒdɪkəm əv/|ozgina, bir oz|A small amount of something.|a little · a small amount of · a degree of · a touch of|biror narsaning ozgina miqdori.|With a modicum of effort, you could pass the exam.|Ozgina harakat bilan imtihondan o'ta olardingiz.|Even a little work would have been enough to pass.|Hatto ozgina mehnat ham o'tish uchun yetarli bo'lardi.|The plan shows a modicum of common sense.|Rejada bir oz sog'lom fikr ko'rinadi.
by orders of magnitude|phrase|/baɪ ˈɔːdəz əv ˈmæɡnɪtjuːd/|bir necha baravar, ko'p marotaba|By a very large amount, often ten times or more.|enormously · many times over · vastly|juda katta miqdorda, ko'pincha o'n baravar yoki undan ko'p.|New computers are faster by orders of magnitude.|Yangi kompyuterlar ko'p marotaba tezroq.|Today's computers are many times quicker than older ones.|Bugungi kompyuterlar eskilaridan bir necha baravar tezroq.|The new estimate is larger by orders of magnitude.|Yangi hisob-kitob bir necha baravar kattaroq.
the lion's share of|phrase|/ðə ˈlaɪənz ʃeər əv/|... ning katta qismi|The largest part of something.|the majority of · the bulk of · most of · the biggest portion of|biror narsaning eng katta qismi.|The capital receives the lion's share of investment.|Poytaxt sarmoyaning katta qismini oladi.|Most of the money goes to the capital city.|Pulning ko'p qismi poytaxtga ketadi.|Rent takes the lion's share of their income.|Ijara haqi ularning daromadining katta qismini oladi.

# Dalillash va muallif pozitsiyasi|Muallif pozitsiyasi
